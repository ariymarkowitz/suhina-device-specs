# Auto Shift (manual 29.4)

**Family:** modulation and pitch
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Auto Shift is a real-time monophonic pitch tracker and corrector with formant shifting, vibrato and
an LFO, made mainly for voice but usable on any monophonic source [manual]. It corrects the input
towards the notes of a scale (the Quantizer), or towards the notes of a MIDI side-chain track
(MIDI mode, mono or up to eight voices for harmonies) [manual]. New in Live 12.1 (October 2024),
available in all editions [source: 2]; it has a dedicated vibrato LFO and a general LFO, built-in
and user scales and a Live Mode for lower latency [source: 2], [source: 3].

## Signal flow

```text
audio in ─► Input Gain ─► pitch detector (range: High | Mid | Bass) ─► detected pitch, onsets
                    │
                    └────────► pitch/formant shifter ◄── target pitch ◄── Quantizer (scale, Strength, Smooth, Shift)
                                  ▲   ▲   ▲                             or MIDI notes (mono / poly voices, Glide, envelope)
        Pitch Shift + Fine ───────┘   │   └── Vibrato (Amount, Rate, Fade In, Natural)
        Formant Shift + Formant Follow┘
        LFO (Reset, Delay, Attack, waveform, Rate) ─► Pitch, Formant, Volume, Pan depths
        MIDI mod sources ─► Pitch, Formant, Volume, Pan (Velocity, Pressure, Mod Wheel, Pitch Bend, Note PB, Slide)
shifter ─► volume and pan modulation ─► Dry/Wet ◄── dry (latency compensated?) ─► out
```

- Stage order follows the section order in the manual; the exact order (for example whether the
  Pitch section's shift and the vibrato are summed with the correction in cents before one
  shifting stage) is [measure]. A single shifting stage driven by the sum of
  correction, Pitch Shift, vibrato and LFO is the likely structure [inferred].
- The Quantizer's Pitch Shift (scale degrees) is applied after the correction [manual].
- LFO modulation applies whether or not the signal is being corrected; the same for the
  vibrato, Pitch Shift and Formant Shift [manual].
- In MIDI mode, sound comes out only while notes are received [manual].
- Dry/Wet at 0 % means no correction is applied [manual]; whether that also removes
  vibrato, LFO and shifts, and whether the dry path is delayed to line up with the wet, are
  [measure].

## Behaviour

**Pitch detection.** Tracks one fundamental in the Input Pitch range chosen by the Pitch Range
toggles; each range has its own analysis window and latency, so the three ranges are different
analysis settings of one estimator (a longer window for the Bass range) [manual], [inferred]. The
estimator is probably autocorrelation-type (YIN or similar) with a voiced/unvoiced decision;
Ableton has a Max pitch-estimator DSP object [source: 4], so it may be the same code
[inferred]. The shape of "voiced"/"onset" detection (threshold, hold) is [measure]. When
nothing pitched is found, the input passes through uncorrected (so that breath and consonants
survive) [inferred].

**Quantizer.** The target is the nearest allowed pitch class in the scale (nearest in cents,
with some hysteresis to avoid flipping between neighbours [inferred]). The shift needed (the
Pitch Correction Meter) is multiplied by Correction Strength: 100 % goes all the way to the
target, lower values leave a share of the singer's own deviation [manual], [inferred]. Smooth
low-pass filters the correction over Smoothing Time (0 – 200 ms), so fast jumps between notes
become glides and slow vibrato passes through; with Smooth off the correction snaps at each
change [manual]. A one-pole smoother on the correction in cents is the likely structure
[inferred]; its dependence on Strength is [measure]. The "T-Pain" effect (Strength 100 %, Smooth
0 %) is mentioned in a review [source: 5].

**Scale.** Included Notes selects which of the twelve pitch classes are targets, from the Root
and Scale choosers, by hand, or from the clip's scale when Use Current Scale is on [manual]. The
list of scales matches Live's scale list (the Scale MIDI effect has the same) [inferred];
[measure]. The Quantizer's Pitch Shift moves the target in scale degrees (0 = no shift) [manual].

**MIDI mode.** The notes of the source track replace the scale as targets: the audio is shifted to
the played note, so it follows the melody; in Poly, each note gets one voice (2, 4 or 8), each a
separate pitch shift of the input, so a chord gives a harmony [manual], [inferred]. Per note, an
attack and release envelope shapes the voice; Note Latch holds notes; Glide slides between
overlapping Mono notes; Pitch Bend Range sets the bend scale; modulation sources (Velocity,
Pressure, Mod Wheel, Pitch Bend, Note PB, Slide) map to Pitch, Formant, Volume and Pan, per
target depth [manual]. Note PB is the per-note pitch bend from MPE; Slide is MPE's third
dimension (CC74) [inferred]. Units and signs of Mod Depth are [measure].

**Pitch and formant.** The shifter changes pitch by the sum of all sources. Formant Shift moves the
spectral envelope by up to ±100 %; what 100 % means as an interval is [measure]. Formant Follow
sets how much of the formant movement accompanies the pitch shift: the manual says higher values
give a more natural-sounding transposition [manual], and a review says formants can be set to track
the pitch to avoid a "munchkin" sound [source: 5]. So at a high Follow value a transposed voice
keeps a natural size, which means the formants are being held near their original place relative
to the pitch change; whether "Follow" means "formants move with the pitch" (chipmunk) or the
reverse is not stated in an unambiguous way, so the sign convention is [measure] (step 8). One
point is measured: at Formant Follow 0 and Formant Shift 0 the spectral envelope stays where it was
while the harmonics move (a shifted 1 kHz sine keeps its energy at the comb line nearest 1 kHz), so
the shifter is formant-preserving at Follow 0 [measured 12.4.6] (Measured).

**Vibrato.** A modulation of the shift, 0 – 200 cents deep, 2 – 15 Hz, fade-in on each onset,
and "Natural" adds slow random variation in rate and depth [manual], [inferred]. Waveform: sine-like
[measure].

**LFO.** One LFO, nine shapes, with Delay (0 – 1.5 s) and Attack (0 – 2 s) fading its level in;
Reset restarts the phase on each onset. Targets Pitch, Formant, Volume, Pan at separate depths
[manual]. The LFO acts regardless of correction. Units of the four depths: [measure]. The Phase
and stereo features of Shifter's LFO are absent.

**Volume and Pan.** Modulation targets of the LFO and of MIDI sources; depth units are
[measure]. The Pan target pans the (mono) output across stereo [inferred].

**Stereo and mono.** The device is described for a monophonic signal [manual]; what a stereo input
does (probably summed to mono for detection; shifting applies per channel or to the mono sum) is
[measure].

**Latency.** The readout shows the delay in ms; it depends on Pitch Range and Live Mode
[manual]. Values are [measure]. The device reports the latency to the host for delay compensation
[inferred]; Live's "keep latency" option is separate [source: 6].

**Dry/Wet.** At 0 %: no correction; 100 %: only the corrected signal [manual]; mix law [measure];
latency alignment of the dry path [measure].

## Edge cases

- Unpitched input (breath, consonants, silence): no detection; the sound passes through
  or is muted [measure]; the LFO's Reset uses the pitch onset [manual].
- Input outside the chosen Pitch Range: poorer tracking, octave errors [inferred]; the LED shows
  where it is [manual]. Measured: a steady 1 kHz sine with the default Pitch Range (Mid) was taken
  as 500 Hz, an octave low, and every shift was applied to that (Measured) [measured 12.4.6].
- Polyphonic input: not tracked (the device is for monophonic sources) [manual].
- Very fast pitch changes in Live Mode: glitches [manual].
- MIDI mode with no notes: silence [manual]. Note off: release of the envelope [manual].
- MIDI On switched off while notes are playing: the 12.1.5 and 12.2 fixes mention phasing
  artifacts, louder-than-expected sound and MIDI modulation staying active; the device must cut
  voices cleanly and ignore MIDI modulation when MIDI is off [source: 2].
- Poly with more notes than Voice Count: voice stealing [inferred], [measure] policy.
- Use Current Scale with MIDI input: not available [manual].
- Strength 0 %: no correction; with Smooth on or off no difference [inferred].
- Dry/Wet 50 % with Strength 100 %: a doubler effect [manual].
- Extreme Pitch Shift with formant follow: artifacts [inferred].
- Sample-rate dependence: the analysis windows are in ms [inferred].

## Measured

[measured 12.4.6] Behaviour run at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.6 (`measurements/29.04-auto-shift/`:
`autoshift-pitch` in the top100 set; `autoshift-strength`, `-scale`, `-vibrato`, `-lfo-waves`, `-lfo-depth`, `-formant`, `-gain-mix` in the remaining set; five more in the third set, rendered by Live 12.4.7: Measured › Third set): Quantizer, Smooth, LFO and Vibrato off, Formant Shift and
Follow 0, Dry/Wet 100 %, Pitch Range as loaded (Mid). Spectrum of the steady 1 kHz tone at -12 dBFS
(peaks within 50 dB of the strongest). These are observations, not a model.

- **The 1 kHz sine is tracked as 500 Hz**, and the output is a harmonic comb on the shifted
  fundamental 500 Hz x 2^(shift/12), whose lines are weighted by the input's spectrum, which stays at
  1 kHz [measured 12.4.6]:

  | Shift | Expected f0 (500 Hz x ratio) | Strongest lines (Hz, dB) | Tone level |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | 500 | 1000.0 (0); 902.7 and 1097.4 (-36) | -12.00 dBFS |
  | -12 st | 250 | 1000.0 (0); 750.0, 1250.0 (-12); 500.0, 1500.0 (-24) | -14.22 dBFS |
  | -7 st | 333.7 | 1001.1 (0); 667.4 (-12), 1334.9 (-13); 1668.6, 333.7 (-24) | -14.59 dBFS |
  | +7 st | 749.2 | 749.1 (0); 1498.3 (-23) | -16.94 dBFS |
  | +12 st | 1000 | 1000.0 (0); 902.7 and 1097.4 (-35) | -12.01 dBFS |
  | -100 ct | 471.9 | 943.9 (0); 1415.8 (-19); 471.9 (-25) | -12.54 dBFS |
  | +100 ct | 529.7 | 1059.5 (0); 529.7 (-22); 1589.2 (-27) | -12.23 dBFS |

  Every line is a whole multiple of the expected f0 to 0.01 % (749.14 / 500 = 1.49828 against
  2^(7/12) = 1.49831; 943.87 = 2 x 471.94). That is how a pitch-synchronous (PSOLA-type) shifter
  behaves when its detector has taken two cycles of the tone as one period: grains are re-spaced at
  the new period and the spectral envelope is kept [inferred from the measurement].
- **Pitch St. and Pitch Fine are exact ratios** of the detected pitch: 2^(n/12) for n semitones and
  2^(c/1200) for c cents [measured 12.4.6].
- **Level** varies with the shift for a pure tone (0 to -4.9 dB) because the comb line nearest 1 kHz
  takes a varying share; the staircase showed the same gain at every input level from -60 to 0 dBFS
  for each shift (within 0.1 dB), so the shifter is level-independent [measured 12.4.6].
- **No shift is not a clean pass**: at 0 st the tone carries side lines at ±97 Hz, 36 dB down, and
  a zero-crossing FM reading of 29 cents at 32 Hz [measured 12.4.6].
- **Latency**: the export's impulse is at 0.02 ms for the default; Live's own readout was not read.

### Quantizer: Strength and Scale

The 1 kHz tone is taken as 500 Hz (above), so the quantizer works on B4 + 21 cents and the output is the comb on the target, whose strongest line is at 1 kHz x target / input. Quantizer on, Smooth off, Root C, Major (`autoshift-strength`, `-scale`); strongest line of the steady tone [measured 12.4.6]:

| Strength | 0 % | 25 % | 50 % | 75 % | 100 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| line | 1000.0 Hz | 996.9 Hz | 993.8 Hz | 990.8 Hz | 987.7 Hz |
| shift from input | 0 | -5.4 ct | -10.8 ct | -16.0 ct | -21.2 ct |

- **Strength scales the correction linearly in cents**: the full correction is 21.2 cents (B5 at 987.77 Hz), and each 25 % takes 5.3 cents. The side lines of the comb (902.6 and 1097.4 Hz at Strength 0, 46 dB down) move with it [measured 12.4.6].
- **The target is the nearest note in the scale, by cents**: C minor, C minor pentatonic and C whole tone have no B, and all three move 1 kHz up to C6 (1046.5 Hz, +79 cents; the other neighbour, A# at -115 cents, is farther); B Phrygian, C Messiaen 3 and Chromatic contain B and give 987.7 Hz, as C Major does (`autoshift-scale`) [measured 12.4.6].
- Level stays at -12.1 to -12.3 dB in all cases [measured 12.4.6].

### Input Gain and Dry/Wet

`autoshift-gain-mix`, Quantizer off, +12 st [measured 12.4.6]:

- **Input Gain is an exact gain before the shifter**: -12 dB and +12 dB come out as -12.0 and +12.0 dB at every level from -60 to 0 dBFS, with 0.0 % THD and the same side lines relative to the tone (46 dB down) as at 0 dB. The impulse peak moves by the same amount (-18 and +6 dB against -6) [measured 12.4.6].
- **Dry/Wet is a power-complementary mix with an S-shaped law.** At +12 st the wet tone comes out in phase with the dry 1 kHz, so the tone level adds the two gains: +1.6 dB at 25 % and at 75 %, +3.0 dB at 50 %, 0 dB at 0 %. That fits wet = sin(theta), dry = cos(theta) with theta = 90 degrees x (1 - cos(pi x Dry/Wet)) / 2: wet 0.228, 0.707 and 0.974 at 25, 50 and 75 % (the impulse's pitched tap measures 0.228 of its 100 % level at 25 % and 0.707 at 50 %, and the dry tap 0.974 at 25 % and 0.707 at 50 %). So the dry part falls slowly at first, and the mix does not dip in the middle [measured 12.4.6; the form fitted to three points].
- 0 % is bit-identical to the bypass render, so the dry path carries no latency and adds no noise [measured 12.4.6].
- The impulse comes back as taps at 0.02 ms and 2.93 ms, each -6.0 dB (the pitched grain) [measured 12.4.6].

### Formant Shift and Follow

Strongest lines of the steady 1 kHz tone (`autoshift-formant`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Setting | Strongest line | Other lines |
|---|---|---|
| Formant Shift 0 (default) | 1000.0 Hz, -12.0 dB | side lines at 902.6 and 1097.4 Hz, -48 dB |
| Formant Shift -100 % | 500.0 Hz, -12.0 dB | 402.6 and 597.3 Hz, -48 dB |
| Formant Shift +100 % | 2000.1 Hz, -14.6 dB | 1500 and 2500 Hz, -27 dB; 1000 Hz, -41 dB |
| +12 st, Follow 0 | 1000.0 Hz, -12.0 dB | as no shift |
| +12 st, Follow 100 % | 2000.1 Hz, -12.0 dB | 1902.7 and 2097.4 Hz, -46 dB |

- **Formant Shift is plus or minus one octave at +/-100 %**: the spectral envelope moves by a factor of 2 down or up, so the 1 kHz line becomes 500 Hz or 2 kHz (the comb at 500 Hz keeps its lines at multiples of it). At +100 % the tone is 2.6 dB lower and has lines at 1.5 and 2.5 kHz at -27 dB, where the comb's neighbours straddle the envelope's new place [measured 12.4.6].
- **Follow 100 % moves the spectral envelope along with the pitch shift**: at +12 st the line is at 2000.1 Hz with the level of the unshifted tone, where Follow 0 leaves the envelope at 1 kHz [measured 12.4.6]. Follow 50 % was not measured.
- The noise response with Formant Shift -100 % falls from -1.9 dB at 63 Hz to -15 dB at 1 kHz and -31 dB at 16 kHz, and with +100 % from -5.0 dB at 63 Hz to -19 dB above 2 kHz (the noise is re-spaced and its envelope moved), where the unshifted default is flat at -0.1 dB [measured 12.4.6].

### Vibrato

Quantizer off, Smooth off; the steady tone's instantaneous frequency in cents against 1 kHz, from a band-passed analytic signal (`autoshift-vibrato`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Setting | Peak deviation | Rate read |
|---|---|---|
| Amt 200 ct, Rate 2 Hz | +203 and -218 cents | 2.00 Hz |
| Amt 200 ct, Rate 6 Hz | +201 and -219 cents | 6.00 Hz |
| Amt 200 ct, Rate 15 Hz | +193 and -205 cents | 15.00 Hz |
| Amt 100 ct, Rate 6 Hz | +102 and -106 cents | 6.00 Hz |
| Amt 200 ct, Natural on | +229 and -240 cents; the peak of each 250 ms varies from 177 to 240 | 5.9 Hz (5 or 6 cycles per second) |
| Amt 200 ct, Fade-In 1 s | 64, 88, 134, 187, 215 cents in the first five 250 ms | 6.00 Hz |

- **Amt is the peak deviation in cents**: 200 ct swings from -200 to +200 (400 p-p), 100 ct from -100 to +100; the rate is exact; the mean is within 1 cent of the input [measured 12.4.6].
- **Fade-In ramps the depth linearly from 0 to full over the set time** (about 215 cents after 1 s), starting from the tone's onset [measured 12.4.6].
- **Natural varies the depth and the rate**: the depth varies by about 15 % from cycle to cycle (peaks 177 to 240 cents against 199 to 219 without it) and the rate between 5 and 6 cycles per second at a nominal 6 Hz [measured 12.4.6].

### LFO shapes and depth

LFO on, Rate 1 Hz, Sync off, LFO > Pitch 6 st, pitch of the output by autocorrelation every 50 to 100 ms, in cents against the 500 Hz the tone is taken for (octave errors removed), over one cycle (`autoshift-lfo-waves`, `-lfo-depth`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| LFO Wave | Pitch over one cycle |
|---|---|
| Sine | +596 cents at the top and -595 at the bottom: plus and minus 6 st, a sine |
| Triangle | plus and minus 6 st (+558 and -562 at 100 ms steps), linear |
| Triangle 8 | a triangle in 8 steps per cycle, each 300 cents (3 st) |
| Triangle 16 | 16 steps per cycle, each about 150 cents |
| Saw Up | a rising ramp: -600 to +600 over the whole cycle, 120 cents per 100 ms, jumping down once per cycle |
| Saw Down | a falling ramp, +600 to -600, jumping up once per cycle |
| Rectangle | +600 for half the cycle and -600 for the other half |
| Random | a new random pitch each cycle, reached by a linear glide over the cycle |
| Random S&H | a new random pitch each cycle (1 s), held without a glide: 78, 118, 576, 303, 383, 434, 373 and 110 cents in the eight cycles seen |

- **LFO > Pitch is in semitones, plus and minus**: 2 st gives +198 and -198 cents; 6 st gives plus and minus 600 [measured 12.4.6]. 12 st was not read (the autocorrelation folds an octave).
- **LFO > Vol is a gain between 1 and 1 minus the depth**: 100 % swings the gain from 0.01 to 0.99 (a full tremolo), 50 % from 0.50 to 1.00 [measured 12.4.6].
- **LFO > Pan 100 % is a constant-power pan**: the left gain runs 0.02 to 1.41 and the right is the mirror image, L² + R² = 2 as in Auto Pan-Tremolo (1.98 at the centre) [measured 12.4.6].
- LFO > Form. 100 % moves the level of the tone between 0.68 and 1.00 and puts lines at 500 Hz (-21 dB), 1000 Hz (-24 dB) and 2 kHz (-26 dB), as the spectral envelope sweeps over the comb [measured 12.4.6].

### Third set (Live 12.4.7)

Five runs rendered by Live 12.4.7: `autoshift-lfo-timing`, `autoshift-vibrato-natural`, `autoshift-glide`, `autoshift-ranges`, `autoshift-formant-saw` (the last three on the extended probe). Pitch is read as the comb's fundamental (the 1 kHz tone is tracked as 500 Hz, as above) or as the strongest line, per 10 to 50 ms window [measured 12.4.7].

**LFO timing** (LFO > Pitch 6 st, Sine, 1 Hz, the steady tone; `autoshift-lfo-timing`) [measured 12.4.7]:

| Setting | Pitch over the first seconds of the tone |
|---|---|
| default (Retrig on, Delay 0, Fade-In 0) | the sine starts at 0 and rising at the tone's onset, ±600 cents |
| Delay 1 s | no modulation for exactly 1 s, then the same start (0, rising) |
| Fade-In 2 s | the depth ramps linearly from 0 at the onset to ±600 cents at 2 s (±88 cents in the first cycle, ±375 in the second, full from the third) |

| LFO > Form. 50 %, LFO > Pitch 0 | the pitch stays (the comb stays on multiples of 500 Hz); the strongest line moves from 1 kHz up to 1.5 kHz and down to 500 Hz and back once per cycle |

- **Retrig restarts the LFO at the start of the input**, Delay holds it off, and Fade-In ramps its depth linearly after the delay [measured 12.4.7]. The input's start after silence counts as the trigger.
- LFO > Form. moves the spectral envelope, not the pitch, by somewhere between half an octave and an octave each way at 50 % (read from which comb line is the strongest; the exact size is open) [measured 12.4.7].

**Vibrato at 50 ct, 6 Hz** (`autoshift-vibrato-natural`) [measured 12.4.7]:

| Setting | Peak and trough per 250 ms | Rate per 2 s |
|---|---|---|
| plain | +50 to +56, -51 to -59 cents | 5.98 Hz throughout |
| Natural on | +46 to +59, -44 to -66 cents | 5.74 to 6.13 Hz |
| Fade-In 1 s | +18 / -27, +34 / -30, +42 / -45, then full | 5.98 Hz |

- At 50 ct Natural varies the depth by about ±20 % and the rate by about ±4 %, as at 200 ct; Fade-In reaches full depth after about 0.75 to 1 s [measured 12.4.6; measured 12.4.7].

**The quantizer on a glide** (C major, the extended probe's 200 to 400 Hz glide over 4 s, 300 cents per second; `autoshift-glide`) [measured 12.4.7]:

- **Smooth off: steps to the scale notes**, each within 4 cents of the note (2 to 3 cents flat). The switch to the next note comes 18 to 21 cents past the midpoint between them (at -380, -182, 121, 319 and 619 cents against C4, where the midpoints are -400, -200, 100, 300 and 600): either a hysteresis of about 20 cents or a detection delay of about 65 ms at this glide rate [measured 12.4.7; which of the two is open].
- **Smooth works on the correction, not on the output pitch:** with Smooth 50 ms the output sits 15 to 20 cents above each note while the input keeps rising, which is the correction (note minus input) lagging 50 ms behind a ramp of 300 cents per second. With 200 ms the output follows the glide to within about ±25 cents, pulled only slightly towards the notes [measured 12.4.7].
- **Strength 50 %** puts the output halfway between the input and the note, in cents (G3+71 in gives G3+35) [measured 12.4.7].

**Pitch Range and Live Mode** (stored `Global_PitchRange` 0, 1, 2 and `Global_LiveMode`; C major; `autoshift-ranges`) [measured 12.4.7]:

- So range 0 is the low range (it alone tracks 50 Hz), range 2 the high one (it alone gets 3 kHz right and misses 150 Hz), and the default range 1 between them, as Low, Mid and High in the menu [measured 12.4.7; the names from the menu order].

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** an LFO with Sine, Triangle, Triangle 8, Triangle 16, Saw up and down,
  Square, Random and Random S&H; one-pole smoothing (correction smoothing, glide); dB
  conversions. An open-source pitch shifter such as Signalsmith Stretch (MIT) can transpose with
  a known latency; whether it can move formants separately has to be checked.
- **Missing, the large parts:** a monophonic pitch estimator with a voiced decision and
  low latency (three range presets, a Live-Mode variant); a pitch shifter that can change
  pitch and formants separately (PSOLA-style, or the stretch port extended with a formant
  factor and a spectral-envelope warp), per-voice for Poly (2/4/8 voices, so the shifter must be
  cheap or share analysis); the scale tables and the Scale MIDI-effect conventions; MIDI
  side-chain input to an effect (Pitch bend, MPE sources), which needs engine support beyond
  the audio side-chain ([common.md](common.md) › MIDI side-chain).
- **Hard to match:** the quality of the shifting and the detector, latency per range, Natural
  Vibrato's randomness, Formant Follow's exact meaning.
- **Real-time:** analysis and synthesis buffers pre-allocated; Poly voices pre-allocated (8).
- **Import:** `.als` element is `AutoShift` [als 12.4.6]. The 12.4 rename of parameters
  means older and newer sets may store different parameter names [source: 2]; read both. Parameter
  elements in a set saved from 12.4.6 [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `Global_InputGain` (stored 0 for 0 dB, in dB),
  `Global_DryWet` (stored 1 = 100 %), `Global_UseScale`, `MidiInput_Glide`, `MidiInput_PitchBendRange`
  (6), `MidiInput_AttackTime` and `MidiInput_ReleaseTime` (seconds: 0.02 = 20 ms), `MidiInput_Latch`,
  `PitchShift_ShiftSemitones`, `PitchShift_ShiftScaleDegrees`, `PitchShift_Detune` (cents),
  `PitchShift_FormantShift`, `PitchShift_FormantFollow`, `Quantizer_Active`, `Quantizer_Smooth`,
  `Quantizer_SmoothingTime` (seconds: 0.05), `Quantizer_Amount` (stored 1 = 100 %), `Quantizer_RootNote`,
  `Quantizer_InternalScale`, `Lfo_Enabled`, `Lfo_OnsetRetrigger`, `Lfo_Delay`, `Lfo_Attack`, `Lfo_Waveform`,
  `Lfo_SyncOn`, `Lfo_RateHz` (Hz), `Lfo_SyncedRate` (12), `Vibrato_Attack`, `Vibrato_RateHz` (Hz),
  `Vibrato_Amount`, `Vibrato_Humanization`, and `Modulation_LfoToPitchModAmount`, `...FormantModAmount`,
  `...VolumeModAmount`, `...PanModAmount`, `Modulation_MidiToPitchModSource`, `...FormantModSource`,
  `...VolumeModSource`, `...PanModSource`, `Modulation_MidiToPitchModAmount`, `...FormantModAmount`,
  `...VolumeModAmount`, `...PanModAmount`. Mapping notes: Vibrato Fade-In is stored as `Vibrato_Attack`,
  Vibrato Natural as `Vibrato_Humanization`, LFO Fade-In as `Lfo_Attack`, Strength as `Quantizer_Amount`.
  Not API parameters but stored as plain values [als 12.4.6]: `Global_PitchRange` (1 = Mid), `Global_LiveMode`,
  `MidiInput_Enabled` (MIDI On), `MidiInput_MonoPoly` (0 = Mono), `MidiInput_NumVoices` (1, see Controls),
  `QuantizerCustomNoteStates.0` to `.11` (the custom scale, one boolean per pitch class), `CurrentTab`, and the
  External Source as `MidiSideChain` (a routing). The meanings of the numbers are [inferred].
- The Pitch section overlaps with Shifter (Pitch mode), and the MIDI side-chain input is shared
  with other devices; build them on common groundwork.
