# Auto Shift (manual 29.4)

**Family:** modulation and pitch
**Note status:** dump checked

## Summary

Auto Shift is a real-time monophonic pitch tracker and corrector with formant shifting, vibrato and
an LFO, made mainly for voice but usable on any monophonic source [manual]. It corrects the input
towards the notes of a scale (the Quantizer), or towards the notes of a MIDI side-chain track
(MIDI mode, mono or up to eight voices for harmonies) [manual]. New in Live 12.1 (October 2024),
available in all editions [source: 2]; it has a dedicated vibrato LFO and a general LFO, built-in
and user scales and a Live Mode for lower latency [source: 2], [source: 3].

## Signal flow

```text
audio in ─► Input Gain ─► pitch detector (range: High | Mid | Bass) ─► detected pitch, onsets
                    │
                    └────────► pitch/formant shifter ◄── target pitch ◄── Quantizer (scale, Strength, Smooth, Shift)
                                  ▲   ▲   ▲                             or MIDI notes (mono / poly voices, Glide, envelope)
        Pitch Shift + Fine ───────┘   │   └── Vibrato (Amount, Rate, Fade In, Natural)
        Formant Shift + Formant Follow┘
        LFO (Reset, Delay, Attack, waveform, Rate) ─► Pitch, Formant, Volume, Pan depths
        MIDI mod sources ─► Pitch, Formant, Volume, Pan (Velocity, Pressure, Mod Wheel, Pitch Bend, Note PB, Slide)
shifter ─► volume and pan modulation ─► Dry/Wet ◄── dry (latency compensated?) ─► out
```

- Stage order follows the section order in the manual; the exact order (for example whether the
  Pitch section's shift and the vibrato are summed with the correction in cents before one
  shifting stage) is [measure]. A single shifting stage driven by the sum of
  correction, Pitch Shift, vibrato and LFO is the likely structure [inferred].
- The Quantizer's Pitch Shift (scale degrees) is applied after the correction [manual].
- LFO modulation applies whether or not the signal is being corrected; the same for the
  vibrato, Pitch Shift and Formant Shift [manual].
- In MIDI mode, sound comes out only while notes are received [manual].
- Dry/Wet at 0 % means no correction is applied [manual]; whether that also removes
  vibrato, LFO and shifts, and whether the dry path is delayed to line up with the wet, are
  [measure].

## Behaviour

**Pitch detection.** Tracks one fundamental in the Input Pitch range chosen by the Pitch Range
toggles; each range has its own analysis window and latency, so the three ranges are different
analysis settings of one estimator (a longer window for the Bass range) [manual], [inferred]. The
estimator is probably autocorrelation-type (YIN or similar) with a voiced/unvoiced decision;
Ableton has a Max pitch-estimator DSP object [source: 4], so it may be the same code
[inferred]. The shape of "voiced"/"onset" detection (threshold, hold) is [measure]. When
nothing pitched is found, the input passes through uncorrected (so that breath and consonants
survive) [inferred].

**Quantizer.** The target is the nearest allowed pitch class in the scale (nearest in cents,
with some hysteresis to avoid flipping between neighbours [inferred]). The shift needed (the
Pitch Correction Meter) is multiplied by Correction Strength: 100 % goes all the way to the
target, lower values leave a share of the singer's own deviation [manual], [inferred]. Smooth
low-pass filters the correction over Smoothing Time (0 – 200 ms), so fast jumps between notes
become glides and slow vibrato passes through; with Smooth off the correction snaps at each
change [manual]. A one-pole smoother on the correction in cents is the likely structure
[inferred]; its dependence on Strength is [measure]. The "T-Pain" effect (Strength 100 %, Smooth
0 %) is mentioned in a review [source: 5].

**Scale.** Included Notes selects which of the twelve pitch classes are targets, from the Root
and Scale choosers, by hand, or from the clip's scale when Use Current Scale is on [manual]. The
list of scales matches Live's scale list (the Scale MIDI effect has the same) [inferred];
[measure]. The Quantizer's Pitch Shift moves the target in scale degrees (0 = no shift) [manual].

**MIDI mode.** The notes of the source track replace the scale as targets: the audio is shifted to
the played note, so it follows the melody; in Poly, each note gets one voice (2, 4 or 8), each a
separate pitch shift of the input, so a chord gives a harmony [manual], [inferred]. Per note, an
attack and release envelope shapes the voice; Note Latch holds notes; Glide slides between
overlapping Mono notes; Pitch Bend Range sets the bend scale; modulation sources (Velocity,
Pressure, Mod Wheel, Pitch Bend, Note PB, Slide) map to Pitch, Formant, Volume and Pan, per
target depth [manual]. Note PB is the per-note pitch bend from MPE; Slide is MPE's third
dimension (CC74) [inferred]. Units and signs of Mod Depth are [measure].

**Pitch and formant.** The shifter changes pitch by the sum of all sources. Formant Shift moves the
spectral envelope by up to ±100 %; what 100 % means as an interval is [measure]. Formant Follow
sets how much of the formant movement accompanies the pitch shift: the manual says higher values
give a more natural-sounding transposition [manual], and a review says formants can be set to track
the pitch to avoid a "munchkin" sound [source: 5]. So at a high Follow value a transposed voice
keeps a natural size, which means the formants are being held near their original place relative
to the pitch change; whether "Follow" means "formants move with the pitch" (chipmunk) or the
reverse is not stated in an unambiguous way, so the sign convention is [measure] (step 8).

**Vibrato.** A modulation of the shift, 0 – 200 cents deep, 2 – 15 Hz, fade-in on each onset,
and "Natural" adds slow random variation in rate and depth [manual], [inferred]. Waveform: sine-like
[measure].

**LFO.** One LFO, nine shapes, with Delay (0 – 1.5 s) and Attack (0 – 2 s) fading its level in;
Reset restarts the phase on each onset. Targets Pitch, Formant, Volume, Pan at separate depths
[manual]. The LFO acts regardless of correction. Units of the four depths: [measure]. The Phase
and stereo features of Shifter's LFO are absent.

**Volume and Pan.** Modulation targets of the LFO and of MIDI sources; depth units are
[measure]. The Pan target pans the (mono) output across stereo [inferred].

**Stereo and mono.** The device is described for a monophonic signal [manual]; what a stereo input
does (probably summed to mono for detection; shifting applies per channel or to the mono sum) is
[measure].

**Latency.** The readout shows the delay in ms; it depends on Pitch Range and Live Mode
[manual]. Values are [measure]. The device reports the latency to the host for delay compensation
[inferred]; Live's "keep latency" option is separate [source: 6].

**Dry/Wet.** At 0 %: no correction; 100 %: only the corrected signal [manual]; mix law [measure];
latency alignment of the dry path [measure].

## Edge cases

- Unpitched input (breath, consonants, silence): no detection; the sound passes through
  or is muted [measure]; the LFO's Reset uses the pitch onset [manual].
- Input outside the chosen Pitch Range: poorer tracking, octave errors [inferred]; the LED shows
  where it is [manual].
- Polyphonic input: not tracked (the device is for monophonic sources) [manual].
- Very fast pitch changes in Live Mode: glitches [manual].
- MIDI mode with no notes: silence [manual]. Note off: release of the envelope [manual].
- MIDI On switched off while notes are playing: the 12.1.5 and 12.2 fixes mention phasing
  artifacts, louder-than-expected sound and MIDI modulation staying active; the device must cut
  voices cleanly and ignore MIDI modulation when MIDI is off [source: 2].
- Poly with more notes than Voice Count: voice stealing [inferred], [measure] policy.
- Use Current Scale with MIDI input: not available [manual].
- Strength 0 %: no correction; with Smooth on or off no difference [inferred].
- Dry/Wet 50 % with Strength 100 %: a doubler effect [manual].
- Extreme Pitch Shift with formant follow: artifacts [inferred].
- Sample-rate dependence: the analysis windows are in ms [inferred].

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** an LFO with Sine, Triangle, Triangle 8, Triangle 16, Saw up and down,
  Square, Random and Random S&H; one-pole smoothing (correction smoothing, glide); dB
  conversions. An open-source pitch shifter such as Signalsmith Stretch (MIT) can transpose with
  a known latency; whether it can move formants separately has to be checked.
- **Missing, the large parts:** a monophonic pitch estimator with a voiced decision and
  low latency (three range presets, a Live-Mode variant); a pitch shifter that can change
  pitch and formants separately (PSOLA-style, or the stretch port extended with a formant
  factor and a spectral-envelope warp), per-voice for Poly (2/4/8 voices, so the shifter must be
  cheap or share analysis); the scale tables and the Scale MIDI-effect conventions; MIDI
  side-chain input to an effect (Pitch bend, MPE sources), which needs engine support beyond
  the audio side-chain ([common.md](common.md) › MIDI side-chain).
- **Hard to match:** the quality of the shifting and the detector, latency per range, Natural
  Vibrato's randomness, Formant Follow's exact meaning.
- **Real-time:** analysis and synthesis buffers pre-allocated; Poly voices pre-allocated (8).
- **Import:** `.als` element is `AutoShift` [als 12.4.6]. The 12.4 rename of parameters
  means older and newer sets may store different parameter names [source: 2]; read both. Parameter
  elements in a set saved from 12.4.6 [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `Global_InputGain` (stored 0 for 0 dB, in dB),
  `Global_DryWet` (stored 1 = 100 %), `Global_UseScale`, `MidiInput_Glide`, `MidiInput_PitchBendRange`
  (6), `MidiInput_AttackTime` and `MidiInput_ReleaseTime` (seconds: 0.02 = 20 ms), `MidiInput_Latch`,
  `PitchShift_ShiftSemitones`, `PitchShift_ShiftScaleDegrees`, `PitchShift_Detune` (cents),
  `PitchShift_FormantShift`, `PitchShift_FormantFollow`, `Quantizer_Active`, `Quantizer_Smooth`,
  `Quantizer_SmoothingTime` (seconds: 0.05), `Quantizer_Amount` (stored 1 = 100 %), `Quantizer_RootNote`,
  `Quantizer_InternalScale`, `Lfo_Enabled`, `Lfo_OnsetRetrigger`, `Lfo_Delay`, `Lfo_Attack`, `Lfo_Waveform`,
  `Lfo_SyncOn`, `Lfo_RateHz` (Hz), `Lfo_SyncedRate` (12), `Vibrato_Attack`, `Vibrato_RateHz` (Hz),
  `Vibrato_Amount`, `Vibrato_Humanization`, and `Modulation_LfoToPitchModAmount`, `...FormantModAmount`,
  `...VolumeModAmount`, `...PanModAmount`, `Modulation_MidiToPitchModSource`, `...FormantModSource`,
  `...VolumeModSource`, `...PanModSource`, `Modulation_MidiToPitchModAmount`, `...FormantModAmount`,
  `...VolumeModAmount`, `...PanModAmount`. Mapping notes: Vibrato Fade-In is stored as `Vibrato_Attack`,
  Vibrato Natural as `Vibrato_Humanization`, LFO Fade-In as `Lfo_Attack`, Strength as `Quantizer_Amount`.
  Not parameters in the dump: Pitch Range, Live Mode, MIDI On, Mono/Poly, Voice Count.
- The Pitch section overlaps with Shifter (Pitch mode), and the MIDI side-chain input is shared
  with other devices; build them on common groundwork.
