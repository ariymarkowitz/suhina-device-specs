# Echo (manual 29.14)

**Family:** delay and looping
**Note status:** dump checked

## Summary

A two-line modulation delay with a tabbed panel: delay times, filtering and a tunnel display on
one tab, an LFO with envelope-follower blend on another, and "character" tools (gate, ducking,
noise, wobble, repitch) on a third, plus a built-in reverb and global width and output controls.
It does clean synced echoes, ping-pong, mid/side delays, tape-style and flanger-like effects, and
very long self-oscillating feedback [manual, source: 2].

## Signal flow

```text
in ─► Input gain (+ "D": also applied to the dry path, to distort it)
   ─► [Gate on input (Character tab)] ─► [Reverb: Pre position]
   ─► sum ─► delay lines L/R (or Mid/Side) ─► [Filter HP + LP] ─► [Reverb: Post position]
        ▲                                 │
        └── Feedback (Ø = inverted) ◄─────┘   [Reverb: Feedback position sits in this return path]
   wet ─► [Ducking: reduces wet while input is present] ─► Stereo width ─► Output gain ─► (mix)
   dry (+ Input gain if "D") ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────► (mix, Dry/Wet)
```

- The order above is the most likely one; the manual only states that Gate acts at the input,
  that the reverb can be before the delay, after it, or in the feedback loop, and that Ducking
  reduces the wet signal [manual]. The exact position of the filter relative to the feedback
  loop (whether the filter is inside the loop so repeats get progressively filtered) is
  [measure]; the filter is described as "Echo filter", and a typical design puts it in the loop
  [inferred].
- Modulation (LFO and envelope follower) drives delay time and filter frequency [manual].
- Noise and Wobble sit in the delay path (noise added to the signal, wobble modulating the delay
  time) [manual].
- The device switches itself off at least eight seconds after the input stops sounding, to save
  CPU, unless both Noise and Gate are on [manual].
- A reported rear panel ("Breakout" input and output) lets other devices be inserted into the
  feedback path, so each repeat runs through them [source: 7]; seen in one search excerpt of a review only
  [measure].

## Behaviour

- **Delay times.** Two independent lines (left, right) between 1 ms and 5 s [source: 2]. In Sync,
  the time is the chosen division in the Sync Mode: Notes (×1), Dotted (×1.5), Triplet
  (×2/3) of the note value, or a count of 16ths [source: 4, manual]. Offsets stretch or
  shrink each side by up to about one third [source: 4]; applied on top of the synced time
  [manual]. The default settings give dotted-eighth echoes that fade slowly [source: 4].
- **Stereo, Ping Pong, Mid/Side.** Stereo: left and right lines independent, each with its own
  feedback. Ping Pong: repeats alternate between channels [manual]. Mid/Side: input is encoded
  to mid/side, each delayed by its own line, decoded again [manual, source: 4]; where the Stereo
  knob acts on the side signal [source: 4].
- **Feedback.** Up to 150 % at the Max wrapper's top end [source: 2], so the loop can grow
  without limit; a limiter or soft clipper in the loop keeps it bounded [measure]. Ø inverts the
  polarity of what is fed back [manual].
- **Input and D.** Input gain applies to the wet path; with D on it also applies to the dry path,
  so it can clip the dry signal [manual]. The wrapper calls this "clip" [source: 2]; the clipping
  curve is [measure].
- **Filter.** A high-pass and a low-pass with adjustable resonance, probably 2-pole resonant
  filters [inferred]; slope is [measure].
- **Modulation.** One LFO with a left/right phase offset, shapes as listed, modulates both
  delay time and filter [manual]. An envelope follower of the input blends with it via Env Mix
  [manual]. Modulation x4 multiplies the delay-time depth by four [manual]. Depth units
  (milliseconds, ratio or cents) are [measure].
- **Gate.** Input gate with threshold and release; attack and hold are not exposed [manual];
  time constants [measure]. Only the release is a control: 50 ms – 3 s [source: 2].
- **Ducking.** Proportional reduction of the wet signal while the input is above the threshold:
  "proportionally" suggests a level-dependent gain reduction, not on/off [manual]. Law
  (ratio, depth) is [measure]. Release 20 ms – 1.5 s [source: 2].
- **Noise and Wobble.** Noise has a morph between types (for example hiss versus hum and
  crackle) [measure]; Wobble is slow irregular random modulation of the delay time, comparable to
  wow and flutter [source: 5]; the two Morph controls choose between types [manual].
- **Repitch.** Repitch on: delay-time changes are glided (resampled read), producing a pitch
  change. Off: crossfade between old and new times [manual]. Glide time 0.01 – 1 s
  [source: 2].
- **Reverb.** Built-in reverb with Amount and Decay; the Pre, Post and Feedback positions place
  it before the delay, after it, or in the feedback return [manual]. Algorithm [measure].
- **Auto-off.** After eight seconds or more of silent input the device stops processing, except
  when both Noise and Gate are on [manual].
- **Dry/Wet.** Linear or equal-loudness [manual].

## Edge cases

- Feedback above 100 % grows without bound; the safe limit and any saturation are [measure]
  (the wrapper's 150 % top is a hint that the device tolerates it).
- Delay time 1 ms with Modulation x4: deep flanging [manual].
- Changing Sync Mode while Sync is off changes nothing audible [manual].
- Stereo Link with different Offsets: offsets stay separate [manual].
- Switching Channel Mode live: delay times are carried into Mid and Side or Left and Right
  knobs; whether values are converted or stay is [measure].
- Gate on and signal below threshold: the input is muted, so the tail of feedback continues
  while the gate closes [inferred].
- Silence: auto-off after at least eight seconds; reverb tail and wobble stop with the device
  [inferred]; Noise and Gate both on keeps the device running [manual].
- Repitch on with the LFO driving the delay time gives vibrato; off gives crossfading steps
  [inferred].

## Measurement plan

1. ~~**Ranges and defaults.**~~ Answered by the dump of 12.4.6 (see Controls). Differences from the
   Max wrapper bounds: delay time tops out at 2.5 s (wrapper 5 s), gate release goes down to 0.1 ms
   (wrapper 50 ms), output bottoms at -inf (wrapper -76 dB); the others agree.
2. **Sync tables.** For each Sync Mode, step the Left control through every value and note
   the label and the resulting delay in ms at 120 BPM (impulse render). Also the Rate list
   in the Modulation tab.
3. **Offset.** Impulse at Sync 1/4 with Offset at ±max; measure delay shift in samples.
4. **Filter.** Noise through the filter with HP and LP at several settings and resonances:
   cutoff, Q, slope; check where the filter sits relative to the feedback loop by comparing the
   second repeat's spectrum to the first.
5. **Feedback.** Impulse at 100 % and 150 %: measure repeat ratios and any limiting; Ø: check
   polarity of repeats.
6. **Input and D.** Sine at −6 dBFS, Input +12 dB with D on and off, measure dry-path
   distortion (THD) and wet level.
7. **Gate and Ducking.** Tone bursts at −40, −20, −6 dBFS with various thresholds; read gate
   attack and opening time, release time; ducking depth versus input level (scatter plot of
   wet gain vs input level).
8. **Modulation.** Each waveform with Mod Delay 100 % at several delay times, with and without
   x4; read delay excursion; Phase 0 and 180; Env Mix from 0 to 100 % with bursts.
9. **Noise and Wobble.** Silent input, Noise on: record spectrum for Morph 0, 50, 100 %; Wobble:
   measure delay-time variation spectrum with a pure tone input (pitch deviation).
10. **Reverb and Stereo.** Impulse through each Reverb Location; measure decay time against
    Decay; Stereo 0, 100, 200 % with a mid-only and a side-only signal.
11. **Repitch.** Step delay time and measure pitch glide; compare with Repitch off.
12. **Mix.** 50 % sine level for linear and equal-loudness.
13. **Auto-off.** Measure how long after input stops the CPU use drops, and whether the output tail
    is cut.

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** biquad high-pass and low-pass with resonance, an LFO with the panel's
  shapes (the Noise shape could hold a random value per cycle, but smoothed or per-sample noise is
  closer [inferred]), dB conversions, a static clipping curve for the input; no oversampling is
  needed.
- **Also needed:** fractional delay lines with a long maximum time (5 s) and a mid/side mode; a
  note-value table (Notes, Dotted, Triplet, 16th); envelope followers, gate and ducker (a level
  detector that can be shared with the dynamics devices); a reverb for the built-in section
  (shareable with Reverb, 29.32); a noise generator with a morphable colour; a wobble generator
  (smoothed random plus slow drift); a crossfading reader for Repitch off.
- **Hard to match:** the ducking law, noise and wobble character, the filter-in-loop topology,
  the reverb sound.
- The tunnel and filter displays need delay time and filter curve data from the device to the
  UI.
