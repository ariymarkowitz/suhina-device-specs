# Amp (manual 29.1)

**Family:** distortion
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Amp models seven classic guitar amplifiers with physical-modelling technology developed together
with Softube. All seven share one set of controls (Gain, Bass, Middle, Treble, Presence, Volume), so
switching the model changes the character without changing the panel. The model is meant to be followed
by Cabinet [manual]. Beyond guitar it is used on drums, synths and keys.

## Signal flow

The manual gives no internal order. A guitar amplifier's own order is the best guess [inferred]:

```text
input ─► preamp (Gain) ─► tone stack (Bass, Middle, Treble; interacting) ─► power amp (Volume, Presence) ─► Dry/Wet ─► out
          [manual]         [manual]                                          [manual]                       [manual]
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────► dry ───────────────────────┘
```

- Gain is the level into the preamp; Volume is the output stage of the power amp [manual].
- Presence works in the power-amp stage [manual].
- Mono or "Dual" processing is chosen by the Output switch [manual].

## Behaviour

- **Gain vs Volume:** Gain primarily sets distortion; Volume sets the output level of the power stage, and in Blues, Heavy and Bass models a high Volume adds notable distortion [manual]. So the power amp saturates in those models and not in the others [manual].
- **Interactions.** The tone stack is modelled so that the controls influence each other, as on the real circuit; increasing the EQ levels can also raise distortion; turning up Treble can lower the bass and mid level; a fixed amount of "electricity" is shared [manual]. A passive tone stack (Fender / Marshall / Vox style) with cross-coupling is the likely structure [inferred]. Consequently a model of it cannot be a plain parametric EQ.
- **Presence.** A control in the power-amp feedback loop that raises or lowers highs and upper mids [manual]; in real amps it reduces negative feedback at high frequencies [inferred].
- **Physical modelling.** Component-level circuit simulation of valve stages and tone stacks (the Softube approach) [manual: "physical modelling technology"; details inferred]. This means per-model non-linear stages with memory (bias shifts, sag).
- **Mono vs Dual.** In Mono mode one amp processes the mono sum (or the first channel) [inferred]; in Dual mode two amps process left and right [manual]; the manual says the difference with a stereo source is large, and SOS reports Dual was needed to keep a tremolo's stereo movement [source: 3].
- **Tips.** Use with Cabinet after it [manual]; use EQ before and after for tone [source: 3].
- **12.4:** a "Guitar Amp Stack" Audio Effect Rack was added to the Core Library; no device change [source: 5].

## Edge cases

- **Gain at minimum:** silence: Gain 0 gives no output at all, not a clean signal [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Gain).
- **Volume high on Blues/Heavy/Bass:** distortion from the power stage [manual].
- **Mono source in Dual mode:** both channels equal; stereo only if the source differs [inferred].
- **Model switch while playing:** circuit state resets or crossfades: [measure].
- **Silence:** no hum or noise modelled: digital silence out at every Gain, Rock at Gain 10 included [measured 12.4.6].
- **Latency:** no mention in the manual; the render's impulse peak is at 0.14 ms (6 samples), so nothing beyond the exported, delay-compensated alignment shows [measured 12.4.6].
- **CPU:** Dual uses twice [manual].

## Measurement plan

1. **Model transfer curves.** For each of seven models, a 100 Hz and a 1 kHz sine from -60 dBFS to 0 dBFS in 6 dB steps, Gain at minimum, 25, 50, 75, 100 %, other controls at their middle, Dry/Wet 100 %: record output level and harmonic spectrum (even and odd) to see the pre- and power-stage clipping, any gating or sag; repeat with Volume at min, mid, max, since three models distort with Volume.
   Done: every model at Gain 5 and Rock at Gain 0 to 10 at 1 kHz (Measured › Models, Gain); every model at Gain 0 to 10 at 100 Hz with a 1 kHz up-and-down ramp (Measured › Gain of every model: no sag or memory); Blues at Volume 0, 5, 9, 10, Clean, Heavy and Bass at Volume 10 and the other six at Volume 0, 2.5, 7.5 (Measured › Volume, Volume of the other models). Harmonic spectra at 100 Hz are read only to the 6th harmonic.
2. **Tone stack.** Sine sweep at -40 dBFS with low Gain, for each model and Bass, Middle, Treble at min, mid, max in combination; read the frequency responses and the interactions (does Treble change the low and mid level?). Fit passive tone-stack topologies.
   Done for each control at 0 and 10 alone in every model, and six combinations on Rock (Measured › Tone stack, Tone stacks of the other models, Tone control interactions). Each model has its own stack; Middle scales the others as in a passive stack. No topology fitted yet.
3. **Presence.** Sweep for Presence at min, mid, max per model.
   Done for every model at Gain 1 (Measured › Presence, Presence of the other models): a high shelf on Clean, Boost, Heavy and Bass, a mid-high lift on Blues and Rock, a lift that stops at 5 on Lead.
4. ~~**Control ranges, units, defaults and tapers.**~~ Answered by the dump of 12.4.6 (see Controls).
5. **Dry/Wet and latency.** 50 % mix on a sine; impulse for latency with Mono and Dual.
   Done for Blues: the main impulse tap is at 0.14 ms in Mono and Dual, and 50 % is an equal-power-like mix (Measured › Mono and Dual).
6. **Mono vs Dual.** Stereo-different input (pure left tone, pure right tone with a different frequency): compare outputs.
   Done with one-sided tones: Mono sums at half amplitude, Dual has no crosstalk (Measured › Mono and Dual).
7. **Noise and DC.** Silence in; read noise floor and DC for each model at high Gain.
   Done for every model at Gain 10: digital silence, no noise, and DC is blocked (Measured › Gain, Gain of every model).

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** oversampling (4x or more for the high-gain models), biquad shelves and peaks for static EQ approximations, a DC blocker, static waveshaping curves as a starting point for simple stages, parameter smoothing.
- **Hard parts:** a circuit-style tone stack (cross-coupled filters), a valve-stage model (triode with grid conduction, bias, sag), a power amp with feedback loop (Presence), seven calibrated parameter sets, a Mono / Dual switch for the processing. A realistic route is a small fixed circuit-simulation structure with per-model constants, tuned against renders of the real device.
- **Hard to match:** all of the character; Softube's models are proprietary, so the models have to be original designs tuned to the measurements and must keep Live's names only at import.
- **Pair:** Cabinet (29.6) follows Amp; the two should share the Mono / Dual treatment.
- **`.als` element:** `Amp` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements: `On`, `AmpType` (stored 0 to 6, default set 2 = Blues), `Bass`, `Middle`, `Treble`, `Presence`, `Gain`, `Volume` (all stored on the panel scale 0 to 10: 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 9), `DualMono` (boolean, false), `DryWet` (stored 0 to 1, 1 = 100 %) [als 12.4.6].
