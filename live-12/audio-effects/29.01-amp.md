# Amp (manual 29.1)

**Family:** distortion
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Amp models seven classic guitar amplifiers with physical-modelling technology developed together
with Softube. All seven share one set of controls (Gain, Bass, Middle, Treble, Presence, Volume), so
switching the model changes the character without changing the panel. The model is meant to be followed
by Cabinet [manual]. Beyond guitar it is used on drums, synths and keys.

## Signal flow

The manual gives no internal order. A guitar amplifier's own order is the best guess [inferred]:

```text
input ─► preamp (Gain) ─► tone stack (Bass, Middle, Treble; interacting) ─► power amp (Volume, Presence) ─► Dry/Wet ─► out
          [manual]         [manual]                                          [manual]                       [manual]
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────► dry ───────────────────────┘
```

- Gain is the level into the preamp; Volume is the output stage of the power amp [manual].
- Presence works in the power-amp stage [manual].
- Mono or "Dual" processing is chosen by the Output switch [manual].

### Amp models

| Model | Based on, as described by Ableton | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Clean | the "Brilliant" channel of a classic 1960s amp used by British Invasion guitarists | [manual] |
| Boost | the "Tremolo" channel of the same amp; edgy rock riffs | [manual] |
| Blues | a 1970s bright amp used for country, rock, blues | [manual] |
| Rock | a classic 45-watt amp of the 1960s | [manual] |
| Lead | modelled on the high-gain channel of an amp common in metal | [manual] |
| Heavy | the "Vintage" channel of the same high-gain amp; metal and grunge | [manual] |
| Bass | a rare 1970s PA, popular with bassists for strong low end and "fuzz" at high volume | [manual] |

A reviewer's guesses at the real amps (a Vox-style amp for Clean, a Marshall JTM45-style amp for Rock) are speculation, not stated by Ableton [source: 4]. This project does not need them: model behaviour is measured.

## Behaviour

- **Gain vs Volume:** Gain primarily sets distortion; Volume sets the output level of the power stage, and in Blues, Heavy and Bass models a high Volume adds notable distortion [manual]. So the power amp saturates in those models and not in the others [manual].
- **Interactions.** The tone stack is modelled so that the controls influence each other, as on the real circuit; increasing the EQ levels can also raise distortion; turning up Treble can lower the bass and mid level; a fixed amount of "electricity" is shared [manual]. A passive tone stack (Fender / Marshall / Vox style) with cross-coupling is the likely structure [inferred]. Consequently a model of it cannot be a plain parametric EQ.
- **Presence.** A control in the power-amp feedback loop that raises or lowers highs and upper mids [manual]; in real amps it reduces negative feedback at high frequencies [inferred].
- **Physical modelling.** Component-level circuit simulation of valve stages and tone stacks (the Softube approach) [manual: "physical modelling technology"; details inferred]. This means per-model non-linear stages with memory (bias shifts, sag).
- **Mono vs Dual.** In Mono mode one amp processes the mono sum (or the first channel) [inferred]; in Dual mode two amps process left and right [manual]; the manual says the difference with a stereo source is large, and SOS reports Dual was needed to keep a tremolo's stereo movement [source: 3].
- **Tips.** Use with Cabinet after it [manual]; use EQ before and after for tone [source: 3].
- **12.4:** a "Guitar Amp Stack" Audio Effect Rack was added to the Core Library; no device change [source: 5].

## Edge cases

- **Gain at minimum:** silence: Gain 0 gives no output at all, not a clean signal [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Gain).
- **Volume high on Blues/Heavy/Bass:** distortion from the power stage [manual].
- **Mono source in Dual mode:** both channels equal; stereo only if the source differs [inferred].
- **Model switch while playing:** circuit state resets or crossfades: [measure].
- **Silence:** no hum or noise modelled: digital silence out at every Gain, Rock at Gain 10 included [measured 12.4.6].
- **Latency:** no mention; oversampling inside is likely and may add latency [measure].
- **CPU:** Dual uses twice [manual].

## Measured

[measured 12.4.6] Behaviour runs at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.6 (`measurements/29.01-amp/`: `amp-models`, `amp-gain`, `amp-tone-stack`, in the top100 set), Mono, Dry/Wet 100 %, every knob at 5 unless the case says otherwise. Levels are of the 1 kHz sine (staircase, peak dBFS in and out) and of the -12 dBFS sweep (the linear part of the response; harmonics are rejected). These are observations, not a model.

### Models at the same panel settings

Gain 5, Bass, Middle, Treble, Presence and Volume 5. Gain is the small-signal gain at -60 dBFS; "out at 0 dBFS" is the output for a full-scale input; harmonics are relative to the fundamental for a -24 dBFS input [measured 12.4.6]:

| Model | Gain at -60 dBFS | Out at -12 / 0 dBFS in | THD at -24 dBFS | H2, H3, H4, H5 at -24 dBFS | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clean | -18.9 dB | -28.9 / -18.5 dBFS | 2.3 % | -123, -33, -130, -51 dB | odd only, symmetric; still nearly linear to -6 dBFS (5 %), 20 % at 0 dBFS |
| Boost | -8.7 dB | -23.3 / -19.2 dBFS | 29 % | -13, -16, -32, -44 dB | strong 2nd: asymmetric clipping |
| Blues | -3.6 dB | -15.8 / -5.1 dBFS | 0.3 % | -55, -57, -62, -63 dB | almost clean at Gain 5 (6 % at 0 dBFS) |
| Rock | +7.5 dB | -21.4 / -21.5 dBFS | 58 % | -20, -6, -24, -14 dB | odd-dominant, output saturated from about -24 dBFS in |
| Lead | +10.9 dB | -27.4 / -26.9 dBFS | 158 % | -25, -0.3, -24, -1.5 dB | hard odd clipping; the 3rd harmonic is as loud as the fundamental |
| Heavy | +24.9 dB | -17.1 / -17.0 dBFS | 89 % | -27, -3, -29, -7 dB | most gain; saturated from about -36 dBFS in |
| Bass | -20.0 dB | -32.0 / -20.0 dBFS | 0.0 % | below -130 dB | perfectly linear at Gain 5 and Volume 5: gain -20.03 dB at every level |

- Once a model saturates its output hardly follows the input: from -24 to 0 dBFS in, Rock's output stays within 0.6 dB, Heavy's within 0.8 dB and Lead's within 1.7 dB [measured 12.4.6].
- The tone differs strongly between models even at the same knob settings (sweep at -12 dBFS, relative): Clean and Boost cut the lows hard (-34 and -33 dB at 31.5 Hz against about 0 dB at 8 kHz); Blues and Bass are the brightest (+8.9 and +8.0 dB at 8 kHz against -3.4 and -19.9 dB at 1 kHz); Rock, Lead and Heavy have a scoop at 500 Hz to 1 kHz; Lead and Heavy fall above 8 kHz in the noise response (-28 and -25 dB at 16 kHz) [measured 12.4.6].
- Stereo: with a left-only or right-only tone both outputs carry the same level (Mono sums the two inputs and feeds both outputs) [measured 12.4.6].

### Gain

Rock, Volume 5 (`amp-gain`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Gain | 0 | 2.5 | 5 | 7.5 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gain at -60 dBFS | silent (no output at any level) | -1.4 dB | +7.5 dB | +14.2 dB | +21.5 dB |
| THD at -48 / -30 / -12 dBFS | | 0.3 / 8 / 70 % | 1.7 / 28 / 92 % | 5.6 / 62 / 96 % | 17.5 / 87 / 97 % |
| output at 0 dBFS in | | -21.4 dBFS | -21.5 dBFS | -21.6 dBFS | -21.6 dBFS |

- **Gain 0 is silence**, not a clean signal: the render has no output at all (every level -240 dB) [measured 12.4.6]. Gain is a level into the preamp that goes to nothing at 0.
- Gain sets the small-signal gain (about +9, +7 and +7 dB per 2.5 steps above 2.5) and where clipping starts; it does not move the saturated output level, which Volume sets (-21.5 dBFS at Volume 5 for every Gain) [measured 12.4.6].
- At low levels the 2nd and 3rd harmonics are similar (Gain 5, -48 dBFS: -37 and -41 dB); with more drive the 3rd dominates (-3 dB at -12 dBFS) and the 2nd stays at -15 to -20 dB [measured 12.4.6].
- **Noise:** no model renders any noise or hum with silence in: the silence segment is digital zero at every Gain, Rock at Gain 10 included [measured 12.4.6].

Rock at Gain 1, the others at 5 (`amp-tone-stack`). At this setting a -12 dBFS tone still has 2nd and 3rd harmonics at -22 dB, so these are the linear part of a slightly driven amp. Sweep response, dB [measured 12.4.6]:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all at 5 | -15.8 | -14.0 | -17.5 | -20.7 | -17.7 | -8.7 | -5.6 | -5.0 |
| Bass 0 / 10 | -23.2 / -11.5 | -17.8 / -13.3 | -18.4 / -17.8 | -18.7 / -21.5 | -16.3 / -18.0 | -8.5 / -8.7 | | |
| Middle 0 / 10 | -15.3 / -16.3 | -13.9 / -13.9 | -17.9 / -16.4 | -23.9 / -17.5 | -23.6 / -14.0 | -11.0 / -7.1 | -6.3 / -5.2 | |
| Treble 0 / 10 | -15.2 / -16.6 | -13.3 / -15.0 | -16.4 / -18.8 | -19.1 / -22.1 | -18.5 / -15.8 | -13.4 / -6.9 | -8.7 / -5.6 | -6.7 / -5.0 |

- At the middle settings the response peaks at 100 Hz (-13.6 dB), dips to -20.9 dB at 570 Hz and rises to a shelf of -5 dB above 5 kHz: a passive tone stack's mid scoop [measured 12.4.6].
- The controls interact as the manual says: Bass up lowers 500 Hz by 2.8 dB and 1 kHz by 1.7 dB; Treble up lowers everything below 500 Hz by 1.4 to 3 dB; Middle has the widest range (9.6 dB at 1 kHz) [measured 12.4.6]. Treble's effect stops at 8 kHz: the top octave stays at -5 dB.

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** oversampling (4x or more for the high-gain models), biquad shelves and peaks for static EQ approximations, a DC blocker, static waveshaping curves as a starting point for simple stages, parameter smoothing.
- **Hard parts:** a circuit-style tone stack (cross-coupled filters), a valve-stage model (triode with grid conduction, bias, sag), a power amp with feedback loop (Presence), seven calibrated parameter sets, a Mono / Dual switch for the processing. A realistic route is a small fixed circuit-simulation structure with per-model constants, tuned against renders of the real device.
- **Hard to match:** all of the character; Softube's models are proprietary, so the models have to be original designs tuned to the measurements and must keep Live's names only at import.
- **Pair:** Cabinet (29.6) follows Amp; the two should share the Mono / Dual treatment.
- **`.als` element:** `Amp` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements: `On`, `AmpType` (stored 0 to 6, default set 2 = Blues), `Bass`, `Middle`, `Treble`, `Presence`, `Gain`, `Volume` (all stored on the panel scale 0 to 10: 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 9), `DualMono` (boolean, false), `DryWet` (stored 0 to 1, 1 = 100 %) [als 12.4.6].
