# Erosion (manual 29.17)

**Family:** distortion
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Erosion degrades the input by modulating the time of a very short delay with a mix of a sine wave
and band-pass-filtered noise. The result ranges from noisy artefacts to something like downsampling
distortion [manual]. The device was redesigned in Live 12.4 (Noise Blend and Stereo Width replace
older mode switches); sets saved before 12.4 load the earlier device as Erosion Legacy [manual],
[source: 2]. This note covers the 12.4 device and records the legacy controls where they differ.

## Signal flow

```text
input ─► very short delay line (time modulated) ─► out
              ▲
              │ modulation = (1 − Noise Blend)·sine(Freq) + Noise Blend·noise(band-pass: Freq, Filter Width) ; scaled by Amount
              │ Stereo Width: modulation mono (0 %) … independent in left and right (100 %)
```

- The modulated element is a short delay [manual]. Amount is linear in the sine's peak delay excursion: 0.16 ms (about 7 samples at 44.1 kHz) at 100 % [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Amount and Freq).
- Erosion has no Dry/Wet control in the manual. SOS says that in the earlier device Amount mixes wet and dry: at zero only the dry signal is heard [source: 3]. The 12.4 device does not: Amount 0 gives the whole signal through a near-neutral short delay, and above 0 the whole signal is modulated, with no dry part [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Amount 0 and latency).
- Algorithmic latency is about 2 ms in 12.4, previously about 5 ms [source: 2].

## Behaviour

- **Delay modulation.** `y[n] = x[n − d[n]]` where `d` is a few samples to a few ms plus the modulation; the delay line is read with interpolation (linear or higher) [inferred]. A fast sine of a few kHz as modulation produces strong frequency-modulation sidebands, which sound like aliasing or downsampling [manual], [inferred].
- **Modulator.** `m[n] = (1−b)·sin(2π·f·t) + b·noise_bp[n]`, `b` = Noise Blend [manual], [inferred]. The noise is filtered by a band-pass at `f` with a bandwidth given by Filter Width [manual]. The sine and the noise are combined with weights (not necessarily linear in amplitude).
- **Amount.** Scales the modulation depth in time (the Y axis) [manual]; linear: peak delay excursion of the sine 0.0016 ms per percent [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Amount and Freq).
- **Stereo Width.** At 0 % one modulator (mono) is used for both channels; at 100 % independent modulators for the two channels (as the old Wide Noise); in between the two are mixed [manual], [inferred]. It applies to the sine too: phase offset between the channels [inferred]; SOS reports that Wide Noise used separate delay lines for left and right [source: 3].
- **Display.** The spectrum of the input and the modulation graphic [manual]. The filter-width animation is a visual aid [source: 2].
- **Filter Width disabled** when Noise Blend = 0 % [manual].
- **Not a bitcrusher.** CDM: Erosion is neither a downsampler nor a bit crusher like Redux [source: 2].

## Edge cases

- **Amount 0:** the modulation is zero; the output is the input delayed by the fixed base delay (about 2 ms latency reported) [inferred]; the render shows a level-neutral, slightly smoothed signal (-2.6 dB at 20 kHz) and no dry mix [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Amount 0 and latency).
- **Noise Blend 0 %:** Filter Width has no effect and is greyed out [manual].
- **Stereo Width 0 %:** mono modulation, a mono input stays mono [inferred]; at 100 % a mono input becomes stereo [inferred].
- **Freq extremes:** very low Freq gives slow wobble (chorus or vibrato-like), very high gives aliasing-like noise [inferred].
- **Tempo, transport:** no tempo sync; the modulation oscillator free-runs [inferred].
- **Latency:** about 2 ms in 12.4 [source: 2]; compensated by Live [inferred].
- **Old sets:** load as Erosion Legacy [manual].

## Measured

[measured 12.4.6] Behaviour run at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.6 (`measurements/29.17-erosion/`: `erosion-amount`, in the top100 set): sine modulator only (Noise Blend 0 %), Filter Width 1.0, Stereo Width 0 %. Read from the 1 kHz steady tone (8 s, -12 dBFS): the sideband amplitudes at 1 kHz plus k times Freq (k up to 6) were fitted with Bessel functions J_k(beta) of one modulation index beta, which for delay modulation is beta = 2 pi x 1000 Hz x (peak delay excursion). The `modulation` FM figures of `analyse.py` (cents at 0.12 Hz) do not describe this device and are not used. These are observations, not a model.

### Amount and Freq

| Case | beta | Peak delay excursion | Sidebands -1 / -2 / -3 re carrier | Carrier level re bypass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amount 25 %, 500 Hz | 0.250 | 0.040 ms (1.8 samples) | -17.9 / -42 / -70 dB | -0.14 dB |
| Amount 50 %, 500 Hz | 0.505 | 0.080 ms (3.5 samples) | -11.7 / -30 / -51 dB | -0.56 dB |
| Amount 100 %, 500 Hz | 1.01 | 0.161 ms (7.1 samples) | -4.8 / -16 / -32 dB | -2.34 dB |
| Amount 100 %, 20 Hz | 1.005 | 0.160 ms (7.05 samples) | -4.7 / -16 / -32 dB | -2.35 dB |
| Amount 50 %, 5 kHz | 0.480 | 0.076 ms (3.4 samples) | -12.1 / -31 / -51 dB | -0.56 dB |
| Amount 100 %, 5 kHz | 0.965 | 0.154 ms (6.8 samples) | -5.1 / -18 / -32 dB | -2.35 dB |

All values [measured 12.4.6]. Sidebands are spaced exactly by Freq (the plus and minus sidebands agree within 0.3 dB at 500 Hz; at 5 kHz only the upper ones are in range).

- **The sine modulator is a pure delay modulation:** the sidebands of every case fit one Bessel index (residual below 1e-4 of the energy at 500 Hz and 20 Hz), and the carrier falls by J0(beta) (-0.56 dB at 0.505, -2.34 dB at 1.01). There is no unmodulated dry signal mixed in [measured 12.4.6].
- **Amount is linear in the delay excursion:** about 0.0016 ms (0.07 samples) of peak excursion per percent: 0.040, 0.080 and 0.161 ms at 25, 50 and 100 % (double each time) [measured 12.4.6]. So the full-scale excursion is about +/-0.16 ms (+/-7 samples at 44.1 kHz) in the sine mode [measured 12.4.6].
- **Freq does not change the excursion much:** 0.160 ms at 20 Hz, 0.161 at 500 Hz and 0.154 at 5 kHz (4 % lower, which is probably the interpolation or smoothing in the delay line, not a control law) [measured 12.4.6] [inferred].
- **The mean delay does not depend on Amount:** the phase of the 1 kHz carrier relative to the bypass render is the same 0.20 samples at Amount 0, 25 and 50 % and 0.22 at 100 %, so the modulation is centred on the base delay [measured 12.4.6].
- **Left and right are bit-identical** at Stereo Width 0 % [measured 12.4.6].

### Amount 0 and latency

- **Amount 0 is not a bypass but a nearly neutral short delay:** the 1 kHz level equals the bypass (-12.00 dB), the impulse is spread over two samples (0.46 and 0.08 at samples 0 and 1 against 0.50 for the bypass), the response is +0.5 dB up to 2 kHz, -0.3 dB at 10 kHz, -1.5 dB at 15 kHz and -2.6 dB at 20 kHz, and the group delay against the bypass is 0.13 samples (0.20 samples phase delay at 1 kHz) [measured 12.4.6]. That is a fractional delay read with an interpolator.
- **Dry/Wet question:** at Amount 0 the output is the whole signal delayed, not a dry plus wet mix, and above 0 the whole signal is delay-modulated (no dry component) [measured 12.4.6]. SOS's statement that Amount mixes dry and wet [source: 3] does not hold for the 12.4 device.
- **Latency:** the export is delay-compensated, so the fixed base delay (about 2 ms in 12.4 [source: 2]) cannot be read from these renders: the impulse sits at the bypass position, apart from the 0.2 sample above [measured 12.4.6]. Measuring it needs a render without delay compensation.

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** a sine oscillator, a biquad band-pass with the bandwidth in octaves (noise filter), one-pole parameter smoothing.
- **Also needed:** a fractional delay line with interpolation (shared with chorus, flanger and Roar's feedback); a noise generator (white or similar, seeded per channel); a modulation mix with Noise Blend; a stereo-width mix of two modulators; the XY display.
- **Hard to match:** exact modulation depth in time; the base delay and interpolation type (these determine the sound); the stereo morph.
- **Related:** Vinyl Distortion (29.41) needs a seeded noise generator for its crackle; the two can share it.
- **`.als` element:** `Erosion2` for the 12.4 device [als 12.4.6]; old sets map to Erosion Legacy, whose element name is [measure]. Parameter elements in the set saved from Live 12.4.6 [als 12.4.6], each holding a `Manual` value: `On` (bool), `Amount` (fraction, 0.2 = 20 %), `Frequency` (Hz, 500), `FilterWidth` (1.0), `NoiseBlend` (fraction, 0.5) and `StereoWidth` (fraction, 0.25). So percentages are stored as 0..1 fractions and Frequency in Hz.
