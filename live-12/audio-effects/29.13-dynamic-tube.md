# Dynamic Tube (manual 29.13)

**Family:** distortion
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Dynamic Tube is a tube-style saturator with three tube models (A, B, C), a Tone control that
decides where in the spectrum the distortion lands, Drive and Bias for the amount and the working
point, and a built-in envelope follower that moves Bias with the input level, so the distortion
changes dynamically [manual]. It has no LFO, no side-chain and no Dry/Wet in the manual's text
(see below) [manual], [source: 3].

## Signal flow

```text
input ─► Drive ─► tube model (A | B | C) with Bias ─► Tone shaping of the distortion ─► Output ─► out
          [manual]  [manual]       ▲                    [manual]                         [manual]
                                   │ Bias + Envelope · envelope follower(input; Attack, Release)
                                   └────────────────── [manual]
```

- The envelope follower listens to the input signal and offsets the Bias [manual].
- Where Tone sits (a filter in front of the tube, behind it, or in a split path that filters only the distortion part) is [measure]. The manual says Tone "sets the spectral distribution of the distortions", which suggests it acts on the generated distortion only [inferred].
- A Dry/Wet control: the dump of 12.4.6 lists one (0 - 100 %, default 100 %) [dump 12.4.6], as French course material did [source: 3 (an unreliable French summary)]; the manual's text for 29.13 lists none [manual]. So the device has a Dry/Wet parameter; the dry path's alignment is [measure].

## Behaviour

- **Tube models.** Three different non-linear transfer functions that differ in how much they distort at low level: A has a threshold (clean until loud), C distorts at every level, B in between [manual]. Measured at Bias 0: A and B are the same, clean to -12 dBFS and distorting at -6 to 0 dBFS with all harmonics, C has a 2nd harmonic from -60 dBFS up; at Bias 50 % B has a 2nd harmonic like A and C and a small 3rd, 34 to 41 dB below the fundamental, that A and C lack [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Tubes against Bias). Probable structure: a bias-shifted asymmetric soft saturator `y = f(drive · x + bias)` where Bias moves the operating point; with A the curve has a flat or linear zone near the origin and soft knees; with C the knee is at the origin [inferred]. A triode-like `f` gives even harmonics (2nd) when overdriven; the forum describes the device as producing 2nd harmonic distortion and compressing large signals a little [source: 2].
- **Bias.** Pushes the signal into the non-linear region; very high values make the signal "break apart" [manual]. Measured: it adds even harmonics (mainly the 2nd), lowers the small-signal gain to -6 dB at 50 % and to -38 dB (tube C) or a dead zone (tube A) at 100 %, and leaves no DC at the output [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Tubes against Bias).
- **Envelope.** Effective bias = `Bias + Envelope · env`, where `env` is the follower of the input (attack and release smoothing) [manual], [inferred]. "Envelope" is bipolar [manual]. Negative values lower the bias on loud signals, "expansion", positive raise it [manual]. The follower's detector type (peak or RMS) and scaling are [measure]. SOS notes that Dynamic Tube is the one of Live's effects with an envelope follower and no LFO [source: 3].
- **Tone.** Spectral distribution of the distortion products toward high or toward low and mid [manual]. Possible forms: a tilt filter before the tube changes which frequencies are driven; a filter after the tube shapes what remains [inferred].
- **Drive.** Gain before the tube [manual]; the output level is compensated: Drive slides the distortion curve along the input-level axis by exactly its dB value and leaves the small-signal gain unchanged [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Drive).
- **Output.** A simple gain after the process [manual].
- **Dry/Wet.** See the open question under Signal flow.
- **Stereo.** Both channels share the tube and the envelope or have independent followers: [measure].
- **Latency.** None stated; Hi-Quality oversampling may add some [measure].

## Edge cases

- **Envelope at 0:** Attack and Release have no effect [manual].
- **Tube A, Bias low:** passes the signal unchanged until its threshold [manual].
- **Tube C:** distorts at all levels, including quiet ones [manual].
- **Very high Bias:** the signal breaks up and may drop out or give DC [manual], [inferred].
- **Negative Envelope with loud input:** distortion falls as the input gets louder [manual].
- **Silence:** a biased curve may leave a DC offset: none; silence in gives digital silence out, and a 1 kHz tone through Bias 50 % or 100 % has a mean below 3e-5 [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Tubes against Bias).
- **Tube switching while playing:** may click [measure].
- **High sample rate:** the envelope times are in seconds; aliasing reduces with Hi-Quality [manual].

## Measured

[measured 12.4.6] Behaviour runs at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.6 (`measurements/29.13-dynamic-tube/`: `dyntube-types-bias`, `dyntube-drive` in the top100 set; `dyntube-tone`, `-envelope`, `-env-time`, `-output-wet` in the remaining set), Tone 0, Envelope 0, Output 0 dB, Dry/Wet 100 %, Mono. Read from the 1 kHz staircase (-60 to 0 dBFS in 3 dB steps): the level gain (rms x sqrt 2 against the step's peak), THD and the 2nd to 6th harmonics relative to the fundamental, and the mean of the 1 kHz steady tone for DC. Drive was 0 dB in the Bias run, tube C at Bias 50 % in the Drive run unless stated. These are observations, not a model.

### Tubes against Bias (Drive 0 dB)

Gain is the output level minus the input level, where the output level is the 1 kHz step's rms times the square root of 2 (the harmonics are included, so a distorted wave reads as a sine of that peak); "-" means no output [measured 12.4.6]:

| Tube, Bias | Gain at -60 dBFS | Gain at -12 / -6 / 0 dBFS | THD at -48 / -24 / -12 / 0 dBFS | H2 / H3 at -24 dBFS (dB) | Remark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, 0 % | 0.0 dB | 0.0 / 0.0 / -1.0 dB | 0.0 / 0.0 / 0.0 / 13 % | below -125 / below -125 | clean up to -12 dBFS (0.1 % THD at -6); at 0 dBFS H2 -19.5, H3 -23 dB |
| A, 25 % | 0.0 dB | 0.0 / -1.0 / -2.8 dB | 0.0 / 0.0 / 0.4 / 27 % | below -125 / below -125 | clean up to -18 dBFS, 0.4 % at -12; at 0 dBFS H2 -12, H3 -22 dB |
| A, 50 % | -6.0 dB | -5.4 / -5.3 / -5.3 dB | 1.1 / 15 / 40 / 43 % | -16 / -124 | 2nd harmonic dominates; output saturates at -5.3 dBFS |
| A, 100 % | - (silent) | - / -58 / -16 dB | - | - | no output at all up to -12 dBFS; at -6 dBFS the output is -64 dBFS with all harmonics at 0 to -6 dB (a dead zone, the tone barely crosses it) |
| B, 0 % | 0.0 dB | 0.0 / 0.0 / -1.0 dB | 0.0 / 0.0 / 0.0 / 11 % | below -125 / below -125 | like A at 0 %: 0.2 % THD at -6 dBFS, H2 -21, H3 -25 dB at 0 dBFS |
| B, 50 % | -5.7 dB | -5.0 / -4.9 / -5.2 dB | 0.4 / 6.1 / 23 / 43 % | -24 / -58 | between A and C: H2 and a small H3 |
| C, 0 % | +0.1 dB | 0.0 / -0.4 / -1.3 dB | 0.04 / 0.6 / 2.3 / 12 % | -45 / -70 | distorts from the quietest level: H2 -73 dB at -60 dBFS, rising 6 dB per 6 dB of level |
| C, 50 % | -6.0 dB | -6.0 / -5.8 / -5.4 dB | 0.2 / 3.2 / 12 / 38 % | -30 / -127 | only H2 (and weak H4); output saturates at -5.4 dBFS |
| C, 100 % | -37.7 dB | -32.9 / -23.9 / -15.0 dB | 3 / 49 / 840 / 72 % | -6 / -31 | the signal "breaks apart": gain -38 dB, then expanding to -15 dB at 0 dBFS |

- **Bias is a level-dependent gain as well as an operating point.** At 50 % the small-signal gain falls to -6.0 dB (C), -5.7 dB (B) and -6.0 dB (A) with a constant 2nd harmonic at -40 to -54 dB relative, and at 100 % tube C passes only -37.7 dB and tube A nothing up to -12 dBFS [measured 12.4.6]. Tube A and B at Bias 0 to 25 % keep 0 dB gain [measured 12.4.6].
- **Tube A threshold:** at Bias 0 the THD stays below 0.5 % up to -6 dBFS and is 13 % at 0 dBFS; at Bias 25 % it stays below 0.5 % up to -12 dBFS and is 12.6 % at -6 dBFS; at 50 % it distorts already at -60 dBFS (1 % THD); at 100 % nothing comes out at all until the input peak is above -12 dBFS. So raising Bias first lowers the level at which tube A starts to distort (about 6 dB from 0 to 25 %), and at the top it turns into a dead zone [measured 12.4.6]. The onset is abrupt: at the first distorted step all of H2 to H6 are within 20 dB of each other [measured 12.4.6].
- **Tube C at Bias 0 never goes clean:** its THD at -60 dBFS is 0.03 % (H2 -73 dB) and the 2nd harmonic grows in step with level, as an even-order term in the curve gives; tube A and B at Bias 0 are free of 2nd to 6th harmonics (below -80 dB) up to -12 dBFS [measured 12.4.6]. This confirms the manual's tube descriptions [manual].
- **Even harmonics at Bias:** at Bias 50 % the 2nd harmonic is the main product on all three tubes (-7.5 dB relative at 0 dBFS in on A, -7.5 on B, -8.5 on C), with the 4th at -22, -23 and -30 dB; the 3rd and 5th are below -95 dB on A and C but only 34 to 41 dB down on B [measured 12.4.6]. Small signals at Bias 50 % carry a level-independent 2nd harmonic (-40 dB on A, -48 on B, -54 on C) up to about -48 dBFS (a kink in the curve at zero rather than a smooth curvature); the ratio then rises with level [measured 12.4.6] [inferred].
- **No DC offset:** the mean of the 1 kHz steady tone is below 3e-5 (-90 dBFS) for every tube and Bias, including the Bias 50 % cases whose curve is asymmetric (DC is removed) [measured 12.4.6]. Silence in gives digital silence out [measured 12.4.6].

### Drive

Tube C, Bias 50 % (`dyntube-drive`), tubes A and B at Bias 50 % with +6 dB [measured 12.4.6]:

- **Drive moves the curve sideways on the input-level axis and does not change the small-signal gain.** For Drive -15, -6, +6 and +15 dB, the gain and THD at input level L equal those of the Drive 0 dB case at L + Drive, within 0.001 dB and 0.004 % THD. Tubes A and B at +6 dB behave the same against their own Drive 0 curves [measured 12.4.6].
- So the output is the tube's curve of (Drive times input), divided by the same Drive again, or an equivalent compensation: gain at -60 dBFS is -6.0 dB for every Drive setting, and the saturated output stays at -5.3 dBFS (about 0.54 of full scale) instead of growing with Drive [measured 12.4.6] [inferred from the measurement: Drive changes the distortion amount, not the level].
- THD at 0 dBFS in: 8.8 % (Drive -15), 23.5 % (-6), 37.6 % (0), 42.1 % (+6), 43.3 % (+15) for tube C, with the 2nd at -21 / -13 / -8.5 / -7.5 dB [measured 12.4.6].

Open from these runs: Hi-Quality, and the test tone was 1 kHz, not 200 Hz. Tone, Envelope, Attack/Release, Output and Dry/Wet follow.

### Tone

Tube C, Bias 50 %, Drive +6 dB (`dyntube-tone`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Tone | gain at -60 dBFS | H2 relative at -24 / -12 / -6 dBFS | THD at -12 / 0 dBFS | gain at 63 / 254 Hz (sweep) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| -1 | -6.0 dB | -24.7 / -13.2 / -9.1 dB | 21.8 / 39.7 % | -6.0 / -6.0 dB |
| -0.5 | -6.0 | -24.3 / -12.8 / -8.7 | 22.8 / 41.2 % | -6.0 / -5.9 |
| 0 (default) | -6.0 | -24.1 / -12.6 / -8.5 | 23.5 / 42.1 % | -5.9 / -5.8 |
| +0.5 | -6.0 | -23.8 / -12.3 / -8.3 | 24.2 / 43.0 % | -5.7 / -5.5 |
| +1 | -6.0 | -23.4 / -11.9 / -8.0 | 25.4 / 44.5 % | -5.4 / -5.1 |

- **Tone has a small effect on a 1 kHz sine**: from -1 to +1 the 2nd harmonic rises by 1.3 dB (0.65 dB per unit) and THD at 0 dBFS from 39.7 to 44.5 %, and the low end (63 to 254 Hz) gains 0.6 to 0.7 dB between 0 and +1 (and loses 0.1 dB at -1); the high end (above 1 kHz) is unchanged to 0.2 dB. The 3rd and 4th harmonics move with it by about 1 dB. The small-signal gain is the same for every Tone [measured 12.4.6]. A filter before the shaper that tilts the low end would give this; its effect on a signal with more bass, or on a 200 Hz tone, was not measured.

### Envelope

Tube A, Bias 30 %, Drive +6 dB, 1 kHz staircase (`dyntube-envelope`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Envelope | THD at -24 / -12 / -6 / 0 dBFS | gain at -12 / -6 / 0 dBFS | H2 at -12 dBFS |
|---|---|---|---|
| -300 % | 0.3 / 0.3 / 0.3 / 0.3 % | 0.0 / 0.0 / 0.0 dB | -51 dB |
| -100 % | 0.1 / 2.5 / 11.4 / 17.0 % | -0.1 / -0.8 / -1.4 dB | -36 dB |
| 0 (default) | 0.5 / 17.8 / 29.9 / 36.5 % | -1.6 / -3.2 / -4.1 dB | -16 dB |
| +100 % | 0.6 / 37.3 / 52.8 / 62.0 % | -4.4 / -6.9 / -8.6 dB | -9 dB |
| +300 % | 9.6 / 104 / 223 / 14 % | -19.2 / -44.5 / -89.0 dB | -2 dB |

- **Envelope shifts the working point of the tube with the level it follows**: a positive value pushes the bias up as the level rises, so a loud signal is driven into the dead zone (gain -19 dB at -12 dBFS and -89 dB at 0 dBFS for +300 %, where the output nearly vanishes), and a negative value pulls it down, so a loud signal stays clean: at -300 % the curve is a straight 0.0 dB line with 0.3 % THD at every level up to 0 dBFS. The small-signal gain does not change (0.0 dB at -60 to -36 dBFS in every case) [measured 12.4.6].
- Between -100 and +100 % the effect is progressive: THD at -12 dBFS 2.5, 17.8 and 37.3 % at -100, 0 and +100 %, i.e. each 100 % moves the effective Bias by an amount that depends on the level, and the level scale is about 12 dB (nothing below -36 dBFS changes) [measured 12.4.6].

### Attack and Release

Tube C, Bias 0, Envelope +300 %; the level of the 2nd harmonic through the -30, -6, -30 dBFS bursts (`dyntube-env-time`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Case | H2 in the loud burst (10 ms windows after the step) | H2 in the quiet tail |
|---|---|---|
| Attack 1 ms (Release 35 ms) | -28 dB at once, then -29 dB | -64 to -71 dB |
| Attack 50 ms | -22, -21 dB at once, steady | -68 to -73 dB |
| Attack 400 ms | -25 rising to -21 dB over about 0.35 s | -69 to -74 dB |
| Release 1 ms | -21 dB, steady | -64 dB, no recovery |
| Release 300 ms | -23, -27, -28, -29 dB (falls within 40 ms) | -73 dB, climbing to -66 dB over 0.3 s and drifting down to -69 |
| Release 1500 ms | -24, -28, -29, -30 dB | -91 dB, climbing 24 dB to -67 dB over about 0.8 s |

- **Attack slows the arrival of the distortion** (the 400 ms case needs 0.35 s to settle) and **Release holds it after the burst** (the 1500 ms case starts the quiet tail 20 dB below the others and takes about 0.8 s to come back up), which is what an envelope follower driving the bias would do. The steady level of the 2nd harmonic in the burst also depends on Attack (-29 dB at 1 ms against -21 dB at 50 ms), because a very fast follower follows the carrier's own ripple [measured 12.4.6; the structure inferred].

### Output and Dry/Wet

Tube C, Bias 50 %, Drive +12 dB, 1 kHz staircase and impulse (`dyntube-output-wet`) [measured 12.4.6]:

- **Output is an exact gain after the tube**: -15 and +15 dB give -15.0 and +15.0 dB at every level, and the THD and harmonics are the same as at 0 dB (H2 -18.2 dB at -24 dBFS in all three); the impulse peaks are -15.6 and +14.4 dB against -0.6 [measured 12.4.6].
- **Dry/Wet is a linear crossfade**: at 50 % the small-signal gain is -2.5 dB, which is 0.5 + 0.5 x 0.5 (the wet gain is -6.0 dB) = 0.75; an equal-power law would read +0.5 dB. The 2nd harmonic at -24 dBFS is -27.7 dB against the fundamental, which is what a 0.5 share of the wet harmonic over the mixed fundamental gives. At 0 % the render is the bypass (0.0 dB, no distortion), and the dry tap is at 0 ms, so the dry path carries no delay [measured 12.4.6].

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** an asymmetric soft-clipping curve (the biased soft curve; a basis for the A, B, C curves), oversampling (Hi-Quality), a DC blocker (after the bias), a level follower with separate attack and release coefficients, biquad low shelf, high shelf and peak for Tone, parameter smoothing.
- **Also needed:** three calibrated tube curves with level-independent knees; a bias path with an envelope term; the Tone structure.
- **Related:** Overdrive (29.27) needs a similar biased asymmetric curve and a level follower (for its Dynamics); the two can share them.
- **`.als` element:** `Tube` [als 12.4.6] (the summary header's name; not `DynamicTube`). Parameter elements [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `DryWet` (stored 1 = 100 %), `PreDrive` (the Drive, dB), `PostDrive` (the Output, dB), `Bias`, `AutoBias` (the Envelope amount), `AutoBiasAttack` (ms, stored 5), `AutoBiasRelease` (ms, stored 35), `Tone`, `Type` (0 A, 1 B, 2 C). The pairing of `PreDrive`, `PostDrive` and `AutoBias` with Drive, Output and Envelope is by name.
