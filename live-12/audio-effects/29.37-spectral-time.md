# Spectral Time (manual 29.37)

**Family:** delay and looping
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

A spectral (frequency-domain) effect with two sections that can be used alone or in series: a
Freezer that holds the spectrum of the input (manually, at every detected onset, or at regular
intervals) and a spectral Delay that delays frequencies by different amounts, with feedback,
frequency shifting and per-frequency spread. A spectrogram shows dry and wet signals. It is used
to sustain sounds indefinitely, smear frequencies over time, or add metallic echoes [manual].

## Signal flow

```text
in ─► Input Send (gain) ─┬─► Freezer ─► Delay ─► out        (Frz > Dly, default)
                         └─► Delay   ─► Freezer ─► out      (Dly > Frz)
                         ▲
dry (latency-matched, or zero latency if that option is on) ──► Dry/Wet ─► out
```

- Freezer and Delay each have an on switch; with both on and Frz > Dly the Delay is fed by the
  Freezer's output [manual].
- Processing is by short-time spectral analysis and resynthesis (an STFT) [inferred]; Resolution
  sets the size and so the latency [manual].
- The Delay section has its own Dry/Wet that affects only that section; the global Dry/Wet mixes
  the processed signal with the dry [manual].
- The spectrogram shows dry in yellow and wet in blue and can be hidden [manual].

## Behaviour

- **Freeze.** Captures the current spectrum and holds it, so the sound sustains indefinitely
  [manual]. A typical implementation holds the magnitudes of the last analysis frame and
  regenerates phases that advance consistently (a phase-vocoder freeze) [inferred]; whether
  phases are randomised or advanced is [measure].
- **Manual mode.** Freeze on = freeze; fade in and fade out in ms shape how the frozen signal
  appears and disappears [manual].
- **Retrigger, Onsets.** An onset detector on the input with Sensitivity; each onset triggers a
  new freeze [manual]. Detector type (spectral flux?) and thresholds [measure].
- **Retrigger, Sync.** New freezes every Interval (ms or beat value) [manual].
- **Fade shapes.** Crossfade: the new freeze fades in over X-Fade percent of the Interval while
  the old one (or the dry signal) fades out; one freeze sounds at a time. Envelope: each freeze
  has its own fade in and fade out; up to eight overlap and sum [manual].
- **Delay.** Delayed copies of spectral data: each frequency band (bin) is delayed by Time and
  Tilt/Spray offsets, fed back and shifted [manual]. Structure: per-bin frame delay lines of
  spectral frames (bins or magnitudes with phase), with Feedback summing delayed frames into the
  input, Shift moving the content up or down in frequency per pass (bin rotation or a
  frequency-domain shifter) [inferred]. Measured with Tilt, Spray and Shift at 0: an impulse comes
  back as single-sample echoes spaced exactly the (rounded) delay, each Feedback times the one before,
  in both channels, at unity gain (0.999), so at these settings the delay behaves like a plain delay line of
  256-sample resolution (Measured › Delay timing).
- **Tilt.** Delay time varies with frequency: `delay(f) = Time + Tilt × g(f)` with a monotonic g
  [inferred]; the mapping is [measure]. The range −2 … +2 in the wrapper suggests a multiple of
  Time [inferred; measure].
- **Spray.** Per-bin random delay offsets within a range (wrapper max 0.4) [source: 2]; a
  scaled random number per bin per frame or per bin held [measure].
- **Mask.** Limits Tilt and Spray to frequencies above (positive) or below (negative) a corner
  that moves with the value [manual]; corner mapping [measure].
- **Stereo.** The width of Tilt and Spray between channels: at 0 both channels get the same
  delays, at more the channels get differing random or tilted offsets [manual, inferred].
- **Order.** Frz > Dly: the delay processes frozen sound; Dly > Frz: the freezer freezes the
  delayed signal [manual].
- **Resolution.** Sets the analysis size: Low (lowest latency, smeared transients), Mid, High,
  Ultra (best frequency accuracy, highest latency) [manual, source: 2]. FFT sizes and the latency in
  samples per level are [measure]; the device reports the latency to the host [inferred].
- **Zero Dry Signal Latency.** Dry path not delayed to match [manual].
- **Dry/Wet.** Global mix; the Delay section's own Dry/Wet is a separate setting [manual].

## Edge cases

- Freeze on silence: holds silence [inferred].
- Freeze Manual on with the Delay on: the delayed copies of the frozen sound ring through
  Feedback [inferred].
- Envelope shape with more than eight overlapping freezes: oldest is dropped [manual says up to
  eight; behaviour at nine is [measure]].
- Interval very short (20 ms) with long fades: freezes overlap constantly [inferred].
- Time at its minimum and feedback high: dense metallic ringing [manual].
- Shift non-zero with feedback: the spectrum climbs or falls with each repeat [manual].
- Resolution change during playback: the buffers are reset; there may be a click or a gap
  [measure].
- Tempo change in a synced mode: Time changes; handling [measure].
- Mono input: Stereo, Tilt and Spray create stereo output when Stereo > 0 [inferred].
- Sample rate: bin-based parameters scale with the sample rate (Shift in Hz, frame sizes in
  samples) [inferred].
- Transport stop with Sync Interval: free-running timer or transport clock [measure].

- The first echo is at the quantised delay with no extra latency: the engine's own latency is not added on top of the delay time

### Delay: Feedback, Delay Mix, Send and the global Dry/Wet

Delay alone at 250 ms (a quantised 249.61 ms), the impulse (`spectral-time-delay-feedback`, `spectral-time-mix-latency`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Case | Echo levels (re the impulse), every 249.61 ms |
|---|---|
| Feedback 0 % | 0.999 (-0.01 dB), no repeats |
| Feedback 25 % | 0.999, 0.250 (-12.0 dB), 0.062 (-24.1), 0.016 (-36.1) |
| Feedback 50 % | 0.999, 0.500, 0.250, 0.125 ... (earlier runs) |
| Feedback 75 % | 0.999, 0.749 (-2.5 dB), 0.562 (-5.0), 0.421 (-7.5), 0.316 (-10.0) ... |
| Feedback 100 % | 0.999 at every repeat for at least 2 s (no growth, no loss) |
| Delay Mix 50 % (Feedback 50 %) | dry 0.706 at 0 ms (-3.0 dB), then 0.706, 0.353, 0.177 ... |
| Delay Mix 0 % | the dry impulse alone, 0.999 at 0 ms |

- **Feedback is the exact gain of each repeat** (25, 50, 75, 100 % give 0.250, 0.500, 0.749 and 0.999 per trip) up to 100 %, which holds without growth; the first echo is always 0.999 [measured 12.4.6].
- **Delay Mix is an equal-power crossfade of the dry and the delay section's output**: at 50 % the dry and the first echo are both 0.706 (-3.0 dB), and later echoes are the 100 % ones times 0.706; the dry is not delayed [measured 12.4.6].
- **The global Dry/Wet is equal-power too, and Send is an exact gain**: with both sections off (and with the Freezer on but not frozen) the output is the input to within 0.01 dB, with no latency in the export: the render of the idle Freezer is bit-identical to the default; with Dry/Wet 50 % the output rises by 3.0 dB at every level (the dry and the identical wet sum coherently: 0.707 + 0.707 = 1.414 at 0 ms), so the wet path of the idle device is aligned with the dry; Input Send -6 and -12 dB give exactly -6.0 and -12.0 dB at every level [measured 12.4.6]. Delay with Time 10 ms and Mix 100 % gives the first echo at 11.61 ms (512 samples) at 0.999 and nothing at 0 ms [measured 12.4.6].

### Tilt, Spray, Mask and Stereo

Delay 100 ms (a quantised 98.7 ms), Feedback 0, the impulse response analysed in third-octave bands: the energy-weighted arrival time (ms) of each band, and the 10 % and 90 % points of the energy (`spectral-time-tilt-spray`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Case | 100 Hz | 200 | 400 | 800 | 1.6 k | 3.2 k | 6.4 k | 12.8 k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tilt 0, Spray 0 | 99 | 99 | 99 | 99 | 99 | 99 | 99 | 99 |
| Tilt +500 ms | 194 | 237 | 290 | 340 | 392 | 447 | 502 | 557 |
| Tilt -500 ms | 502 | 461 | 416 | 362 | 308 | 253 | 198 | 143 |
| Spray 200 ms | 190 | 213 | 198 | 204 | 197 | 222 | 185 | 202 |
| Tilt +500 ms, Mask +0.5 | 123 | 144 | 177 | 228 | 291 | 367 | 459 | 557 |
| Tilt +500 ms, Mask -0.5 | 183 | 215 | 232 | 238 | 234 | 218 | 188 | 143 |

- **Tilt adds a delay that is linear in the logarithm of the frequency**: +500 ms adds 95 ms at 100 Hz rising by 52 ms per octave to 458 ms at 12.8 kHz (extrapolated: about 500 ms at 20 kHz and 0 at 25 to 30 Hz); -500 ms is the mirror, the long delay at the bottom (403 ms added at 100 Hz) falling by 51 ms per octave to 44 ms at 12.8 kHz. The total added delay never goes negative: the sign picks which end gets the long delay, the magnitude (in ms) is the span between the two ends (about 500 ms over the audible 10 octaves) [measured 12.4.6]. The tilt takes energy from the low bands (-4.7 dB at 100 Hz, -5.2 at 200, -3.5 at 400 against Tilt 0) and leaves the high ones within 0.3 dB [measured 12.4.6].
- **Spray adds a random delay per frequency bin, uniform from 0 to the Spray**: with 200 ms the mean arrival of every band is 185 to 222 ms (the base 99 ms plus 100 ms) and the 10 % and 90 % points are at 111 to 146 and 252 to 287 ms from 400 Hz up (a uniform delay on 0 to 200 ms after the base of 99 ms would give 119 and 279 ms): the spread is the same at all frequencies, the lowest band being too coarse (about 47 ms) to show it [measured 12.4.6].
- **Mask limits the tilt to one end, as a gradual weight of the effect**: Mask +0.5 leaves 25 % of the tilt's added delay at 100 Hz, 33 % at 200, 41 % at 400, 54 % at 800, 66 % at 1.6 kHz, 77 % at 3.2, 89 % at 6.4 and 100 % at 12.8 kHz (about 11 percentage points per octave): the low end is masked. Mask -0.5 is the mirror: 88 % at 100 Hz, 84, 70, 58, 46, 34, 22 and 10 % at 12.8 kHz. So a positive Mask protects the low frequencies, a negative one the high; Mask 0 was not rendered with a tilt and the +-1 range was not tried [measured 12.4.6].
- **Stereo gives each channel its own random pattern**: with Spray 200 ms and Stereo 100 % the first taps differ between left and right (for example 0.030 / 0.039 at 104 ms), where Spray alone gives identical channels [measured 12.4.6].

### Freezer: Onsets and Sync

**Onsets** (`spectral-time-freeze-onsets`: Frozen on, Retrigger Onsets, Crossfade 2 ms; the staircase from -60 to 0 dBFS in 3 dB steps of 0.25 s, and the -30 / -6 / -30 dBFS bursts) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Sensitivity | Staircase output | Bursts out (dBFS) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 % and 25 % | silent (never triggered) | silent |
| 50 % (default) | -30.7 dBFS held for every step from -60 to -6 dBFS in, then -0.9 at 0 dBFS in | -5.2, -10.2, -14.3 |
| 75 % | -30.7 held to -24 dBFS, then -17.3, -9.3, -0.9 at -12, -6, 0 dBFS in | -5.2, -17.4, -14.3 |
| 100 % | follows the steps closely (-73.5, -53.4, -45.6, -30.6, -16.7, -7.2, -0.3) | -42.9, -6.0, -10.4 |

- **The detector triggers on rises in level, and a higher Sensitivity triggers on smaller ones**: nothing in the probe triggers it at 0 or 25 %, one capture happens near the start of the staircase at 50 % (its output stays at the captured -30.7 dBFS regardless of the input level, the signature of a frozen snapshot) and another when the last, +6 dB, step arrives; at 75 % the 3 dB steps of -12, -6 and 0 dBFS each re-capture; at 100 % almost every step does. The step sizes and levels at which the detector fires were not mapped further [measured 12.4.6].
- A frozen snapshot of a tone from the staircase has a THD of 320 % in the analysis: the frozen spectrum is not the tone's, but a held noisy copy [measured 12.4.6].

**Sync retriggering with a crossfade or an envelope** (`spectral-time-freeze-sync`: Frozen on, Retrigger Sync every 500 ms, the staircase and the impulse) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Case | Staircase gain at -48 / -12 / 0 dBFS in | Impulse response |
|---|---|---|
| X-Fade 0 % | -5.4 / -4.7 / -5.7 dB (irregular between steps) | a plateau of 1.318 (2.4 dB) from 116 ms |
| X-Fade 50 % | -7.9 / -6.8 / -8.1 | the plateau rising from 0.1 dB at 186 ms to 2.4 dB at 255 ms |
| X-Fade 100 % | -16.1 / -15.4 / -17.0 | rising from -1.2 dB at 325 ms; a 0.6 s build-up (t63 = 621 ms) |
| Envelope, Fade 2 ms in / 40 ms out (the defaults) | silent | peak -6.1 dB at 23 ms, no sustained sound |
| Envelope, Fade 300 ms in and out | -8.6 / -7.9 / -8.8 dB | rising from -5.5 dB at 116 ms to 2.1 dB over about 380 ms |
| Interval 1/8 note (250 ms) | the same as 500 ms | the plateau from 23 ms |

- **X-Fade lowers the held level**: the held output is 2.5 dB under X-Fade 0 at 50 % and 11 dB under at 100 %, as a crossfade between two independent frozen snapshots (equal-amplitude, uncorrelated, so the sum is weaker mid-fade) would give; the build-up after the trigger takes about 70 ms at 50 % and 0.6 s at 100 % [measured 12.4.6], [inferred] for the reason.
- **The Envelope fade type with the default Fade In 2 ms and Fade Out 40 ms produced no sound** over the whole probe, including the staircase and the bursts; with 300 ms and 300 ms it holds the sound with a swell of about 380 ms. The silence at the short fades is not understood [measured 12.4.6].
- **Interval**: a beat interval of 1/8 (250 ms at 120 BPM) renders the same staircase as 500 ms; the impulse shows the first freeze at 23 ms instead of 116 ms, i.e. the freeze happens earlier in the Sync cycle [measured 12.4.6].

|---|---|---|---|
| 1/8 bar (6) | 250 ms | 249.615 ms (11 008 samples) | 43 |
| 1/4 bar (9) | 500 ms | 499.229 ms (22 016) | 86 |

- **The list entries are bar fractions and the delay is the nearest multiple of 256 samples** for the first four steps (43.07, 86.13, 129.2 and 344.5 steps round to 43, 86, 129 and 345) [measured 12.4.7].
- **The longest delay is about 1022 steps (5.93 s)**: the 3-bar step, 6 s nominal, comes out at 1022 x 256 samples (the full-length input correlates with the output at 0.91, not 0.98 as at the shorter steps), so the buffer holds about 5.93 s; the Time control stops at 3 s and the Synced list has entries up to 8 bars (16 s) that cannot be reached [measured 12.4.7; the cap is inferred from one step].

**Resolution, Zero Dry Signal Latency, the order and AvoidFreezingOnsets** (`spectral-time-resolution-order`: Delay 250 ms, Feedback 0, Dry/Wet 100 % unless stated; the echo of the impulse):

| Case | echo | in 256-sample steps |
|---|---|---|

- **The delay grid depends on Resolution**: at Resolution 2 the 250 ms delay (11 025 samples) comes out at 11 264 samples, which is 11 x 1024, against 43 x 256 at the other two settings; so the hop of the engine is at least 1024 samples at Resolution 2 (rounding 10.77 up) while Resolutions 0 and 1 are on a grid that divides 256 (a 128-sample grid cannot be told from 256 here) [measured 12.4.7; the hop sizes are inferred from one delay value].
- **Zero Dry Signal Latency shows the device's latency**: with it on the dry sound is at 0 and the echo comes 1280 samples (29.0 ms, 5 hops of 256) later than without it (12 288 against 11 008): the latency the device reports at Resolution 1, which Live compensates in the other renders (as the first echo "with no extra latency" in 12.4.6). The dry and the echo are both 0.354 = 0.5012 x 0.707, so Dry/Wet is the equal-power crossfade of 12.4.6 [measured 12.4.7; the reading as reported latency is inferred].

There is no render with the option off and the same Sensitivity, so what it changes is not read [measured 12.4.7] [measure: the same case with AvoidFreezingOnsets false].

**Manual freeze, toggled during the render** (`spectral-time-freeze-lane`: Manual, Frozen on at 22.0 s during the steady tone and off at 31.0 s, 0.25 s into the noise; Fade Type Crossfade with X-Fade 0 %, and Envelope with Fade In 100 ms and Fade Out 500 ms):

- **A freeze of a steady tone is silent in level and holds**: the output is -15.0 dB rms before, at and after the freeze at 22 s (nothing audible happens), it stays at -15.0 dB after the input stops at 28.75 s through the 2 s gap, and the noise that starts at 30.75 s does not appear before the release (the frozen sound replaces the live input) [measured 12.4.7].
- **Crossfade at X-Fade 0 % ends the freeze at once**: at 31.0 s the 1 kHz line falls from -12.0 to -39 dB within 20 ms and the output is the live noise (-18.1 dB rms) from the next 50 ms window [measured 12.4.7].
- **Envelope with Fade Out 500 ms fades the frozen sound over the fade time**: the 1 kHz line falls -12.4, -13.4, -14.4, -17.1, -20.6, -28.2 and -46 dB at 0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 and 0.5 s after the release, close to a linear amplitude ramp to zero over 0.5 s (a ramp would give -0.0, -0.9, -1.9, -4.4, -8.0, -14.0 dB; the measured fade is 0.5 to 2 dB under it) while the live noise (-18 dB rms) is mixed in [measured 12.4.7]. The Fade In of 100 ms is not visible at the freeze of a steady tone (the held and the live sound are the same level) [measured 12.4.7].

### Fourth set (Live 12.4.7)

[measured 12.4.7] One run at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.7 (`measurements/fourth/`: `spectral-time-order-reference`), 120 BPM, Resolution as loaded: Freezer on in Retrigger Sync mode every 500 ms and Frozen on, Delay on at 250 ms with Feedback 0, both Dry/Wet at 100 %, ProcessingOrder stored 0 (the loaded Frz > Dly) and 1. These are observations, not a model.

**Processing order** (`spectral-time-order-reference`; the level of the output in 50 ms windows against the input):

| Event in the input | ProcessingOrder 0 (Frz > Dly) | ProcessingOrder 1 (Dly > Frz) |
|---|---|---|
| the impulse at 0.5 s | a held sound of -34 dBFS from 0.70 to 1.25 s | nothing |
| the steady 1 kHz tone from 20.75 s | silent until 21.20 s, full level (-15.0 dB) from 21.25 s | -32 dB from 21.00 s, full level from 21.50 s |
| the same tone ending at 28.75 s | full level to 29.20 s, gone from 29.30 s | -20 dB from 29.00 to 29.50 s, gone from 29.55 s |

- **The freezer retriggers on a grid of the 500 ms, and the order decides what it captures.** In order 0 the freezer sees the input: the impulse and the tone are caught at the tick on or after them (0.5 s; 21.0 s, and 28.5 s as the last tick with the tone), the held sound lasts to the next tick and the delay then moves it 250 ms later (a hold from 21.0 s is heard from 21.25 s, the last hold 28.5 to 29.0 s ends at 29.25 s). In order 1 the freezer sees the delayed input: a hold starts at the tick itself, with what the delay line then outputs, so the tone arriving at 21.0 s (20.75 s + 250 ms) is caught only as its first instant at the 21.0 s tick (-32 dB) and in full at 21.5 s, and the last tick with sound, 29.0 s, catches the end of the delayed tone and holds a part of it (-20 dB) [measured 12.4.7].
- **A sound shorter than the grid can be lost in order 1.** The impulse is delayed to 0.75 s, between the ticks at 0.5 and 1.0 s, and never reaches the freezer; in order 0 it is caught at 0.5 s. This is why the third set's order 1 render had no impulse [measured 12.4.7].
- **The stored 0 is Frz > Dly and 1 is Dly > Frz**, as the wrapper's numbering suggested [measured 12.4.7].

## Measurement plan

1. **Ranges and defaults.** Settled by the dump [dump 12.4.6] for the parameters it lists. The Resolution, order and Zero Dry Signal Latency defaults come from plain values in the saved set [als 12.4.6]. Still to read: the spectrogram toggle's default (no stored element was found for it) and what `Freezer_AvoidFreezingOnsets` is.
   More: Zero Dry Signal Latency, Resolution and the order are stored elements that reach the audio; AvoidFreezingOnsets was rendered without a reference (Measured › Third set).
2. **Resolution and latency.** Impulse through the device at each Resolution (Delay and Freezer
   off, Dry/Wet 100 %, Zero Dry Signal Latency off and on): measure latency in samples; use a
   sweep to estimate frame size from time smearing and frequency resolution (spectral
   peaks of a two-tone input).
   Partly: the delay grid at Resolution 0, 1, 2 (256 samples, 256, 1024) and the latency of 1280 samples at Resolution 1 from Zero Dry Signal Latency (Measured › Third set). The latency at the other Resolutions and the frequency resolution are open.
3. **Freeze.** Sine burst then silence; Freeze: spectrum and level over time (does it decay,
   shimmer?), phase handling, behaviour with noise; Fade In/Out measured on the envelope.
   Partly: a manual freeze of the steady tone, released into noise, with Crossfade at 0 % and with Envelope Fade Out 500 ms (Measured › Third set). Freezing noise, the spectrum over time of the frozen sound and Fade In are open.
4. **Onsets.** Click and drum loop: when freezes happen versus the transient, at Sensitivity 0,
   50, 100 %.
   Done at Sensitivity 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 %: no trigger at 0 and 25 %, more re-captures with higher Sensitivity (Measured › Freezer: Onsets and Sync). The levels at which the detector fires were not mapped with a click or drum loop.
5. **Sync and crossfade.** Steady input, Interval 500 ms: render with Crossfade at X-Fade 10 %,
   50 %, 100 %; Envelope with various Fade times; count stacked freezes up to eight and nine.
   Partly: Crossfade at 0, 50 and 100 %, Envelope at 2/40 ms (silent) and 300/300 ms, beat 1/8 (Measured › Freezer: Onsets and Sync). Eight and nine stacked freezes were not counted.
6. **Delay.** Impulse in Delay-only mode at Mode Time 100 ms: repeat spacing and level versus
   Feedback; Notes modes lists at 120 BPM.
   Partly: delay times for Time 10, 100, 250, 1000 ms, Synced 1/8 and the 16th, Triplet, Dotted modes at 3 (rounded to 256-sample steps), repeat spacing and the 0.5 ratio at Feedback 50 % (Measured › Delay timing). Still open: other Feedback values, the other Synced steps.
   Feedback 25, 75 and 100 % added: each repeat is the exact Feedback fraction, 100 % holds (Measured › Delay: Feedback, Delay Mix, Send and the global Dry/Wet).
   More: Synced steps 6, 9, 12 and 18 (and the template's 15): the nearest multiple of 256 samples, capped near 5.93 s (Measured › Third set).
7. **Shift.** Sine at 1 kHz with Shift +100 Hz: frequency of each repeat (additive vs ratio).
   Done: Shift is additive in Hz and applied on every repeat; the sign is the displayed sign (Measured › Delay: Shift).
8. **Tilt, Spray, Mask, Stereo.** Broadband impulse or sweep: arrival time as a function of
   frequency (spectrogram) for several Tilt values; Spray at 0.1 – 0.4 and its time distribution;
   Mask at ±50 %, ±100 % to find the corner frequency; L/R differences at Stereo 100 %.
   Done for Tilt +-500 ms, Spray 200 ms, Mask +-0.5 with Tilt +500 ms and Stereo 100 % with Spray: Tilt is linear in log frequency (52 ms per octave for 500 ms), Spray a uniform random delay, Mask a gradual weight (Measured › Tilt, Spray, Mask and Stereo).
9. **Order and mix.** Compare both orders on the same input; Dry/Wet laws for both mix controls.
   Done: Delay Mix and the global Dry/Wet are equal-power, the dry not delayed, Input Send an exact gain (Measured › Delay: Feedback, Delay Mix, Send and the global Dry/Wet). The order of the sections could not be set (not an API parameter).
   The order is a stored element (`ProcessingOrder`); both orders were rendered with the Freezer retriggering every 500 ms and the Delay at 250 ms: stored 0 puts the delay after the freezer (holds heard 250 ms after their tick), 1 puts it before (holds start at the tick and capture the delayed input) (Measured › Fourth set). Other freezer modes and delay times in both orders are open.

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** parameter smoothing and dB conversions. Signalsmith Stretch and
  Signalsmith Linear (MIT) have STFT machinery, aimed at time-stretching; check before reusing.
- **Also needed:** an STFT framework with selectable frame sizes (Resolution), onset detection,
  per-bin frame delay lines with feedback, bin shift, random-per-bin generators, an envelope
  fade stack for up to eight freezes, tempo-synced interval and note tables, a spectrogram view,
  latency reporting and a dry-path delay compensation switch.
- **Hard to match:** frame sizes and window, how freeze regenerates phase, how Tilt, Spray
  and Mask are defined; they decide the sound.
- **Real-time:** all frame buffers allocated at prepare time; no FFT planning in the
  callback.
- **.als element name:** `Spectral` [dump 12.4.6] (the display name is Spectral Time). Parameter elements [als 12.4.6] (stored units where they differ from the display): `On`, `Freezer_On`, `Freezer_FreezeOn`, `Freezer_SyncIntervalUnits` (0 = Milliseconds), `Freezer_SyncIntervalBeats` (index, 6), `Freezer_SyncIntervalSeconds` (seconds, 0.5), `Freezer_MainMode`, `Freezer_RetriggerMode`, `Freezer_FadeType`, `Freezer_FadeIn` and `Freezer_FadeOut` (seconds, 0.002 and 0.04), `Freezer_CrossfadePercent`, `Freezer_Sensitivity` (0.5), `Delay_On`, `Delay_TimeSeconds` (seconds, 0.25), `Delay_TimeUnit` (0 = Time), `Delay_TimeSixteenths` (3), `Delay_TimeDivisions` (index, 15), `Delay_Feedback`, `Delay_Tilt`, `Delay_Spray`, `Delay_Mask`, `Delay_StereoSpread`, `Delay_FrequencyShift`, `Delay_DryWet` (0.5), `InputSendGain` (linear gain, 1 = 0 dB), `DryWet` (0.5). Not parameters but plain values on the device element [als 12.4.6]: `Resolution` (1 = Mid), `ProcessingOrder` (0 = Frz > Dly), `ZeroLatencyDry` (false), `Freezer_AvoidFreezingOnsets` (false). The meanings of the numbers are [inferred].
- The Max wrapper `abl.device.spectraltime~` is a reliable source of bounds for the parameters
  in this note [source: 2]; defaults are not given there.
