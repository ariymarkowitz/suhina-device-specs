# Pedal (manual 29.28)

**Family:** distortion
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Pedal is a guitar-pedal distortion with three circuits (Overdrive, Distortion, Fuzz), a Gain control,
a post-distortion EQ of Bass, Mid (three switchable centre frequencies) and Treble plus a Sub
switch, an Output level and Dry/Wet [manual]. It is made for guitar together with Tuner, Amp and
Cabinet, but is used on drums, vocals and synths too [manual]. It has no side-chain, no tempo
parts and no display.

## Signal flow

```text
input ─► Gain ─► distortion circuit (Overdrive | Distortion | Fuzz) ─► EQ: Bass peak 100 Hz · Mid peak (500 Hz | 1 kHz | 2 kHz) · Treble shelf 3.3 kHz · Sub low shelf <250 Hz ─► Output ─► Dry/Wet ─► out
          [manual]  [manual]                                           [manual]                                                                                      [manual]  [manual]
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────► dry ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- The EQ comes after the distortion [manual].
- Where Output sits relative to Dry/Wet and whether the dry path gets the Output gain is [measure].
- The EQ is "adaptive": the Q rises as the boost rises [manual].
- Hi-Quality (context menu) reduces aliasing; so there is internal oversampling in the high-quality mode [manual], [inferred].

## Behaviour

**Distortion circuits.** Three different non-linear stages that Gain drives. The manual describes only their character [manual]; the Max reference calls the three objects `abl.dsp.overdrive~` ("overdriven guitar pedal"), `abl.dsp.distortion~` ("tight and aggressive") and `abl.dsp.fuzz~` ("heavy and broken") with identical attribute sets (gain, bass, mid, midfreq, treble, sub, mix) [source: 2], so these are the Pedal's three types as separate Max objects [inferred]. Probable structures [inferred]:

- Overdrive: a soft (tanh-like) saturation with moderate gain, maybe a mid-hump pre-filter as in a classic overdrive pedal, mostly odd harmonics, little compression. Measured: odd harmonics only (the 2nd and 4th below -75 dB), THD at most 11.5 %, 5.8 % at full scale [measured 12.4.6].
- Distortion: a harder clipper (diode-style or hard knee) after a stronger gain and a tighter low-cut, giving "tight" low end. Measured: symmetric (odd only), 35.8 % THD when saturated, with the 3rd and 5th close to those of a hard-clipped sine; a hard knee at Gain 0 % [measured 12.4.6].
- Fuzz: an asymmetric, bias-dependent curve (transistor-fuzz-like) with gating/sputter at low levels, strongest even harmonics and a loose low end; "unstable" suggests the bias or gain depends on the input level (sag) [inferred]. Measured: the 2nd harmonic is the largest product (-4 to -7 dB), THD 56 to 72 % at high level, and at Gain 25 % a flat-spectrum 3.5 % distortion at -36 dBFS [measured 12.4.6].

Gain probably maps to pre-distortion gain over a range of tens of dB (more in Distortion and Fuzz), with 0 % giving a minimum gain that still saturates, as the manual notes [manual], [inferred]. Measured small-signal gain, 0 to 100 %: Overdrive +7.2 to +33.3 dB, Distortion +6.4 to about +48 dB, Fuzz +13.1 to +43.8 dB, with a steep first quarter and a flattening top; the output where each circuit saturates is -5.1 (Overdrive), -12 (Distortion) and -10.2 to -10.6 dBFS (Fuzz) whatever the Gain [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Gain at low level, The three circuits).

**EQ.** After the distortion, three filters plus Sub, all adaptive [manual]:

- Bass: a peak (bell) filter at 100 Hz with gain up to the Bass amount [manual]. The gain range in dB is [measure] (likely about ±12 to ±18 dB [inferred]).
- Mid: a peak at the chosen frequency; the bandwidth is narrow at 500 Hz and wide at 2 kHz [manual].
- Treble: a high shelf at 3.3 kHz [manual].
- Sub: a low shelf at 250 Hz boosting below it; the boost size is [measure] [manual].
- Adaptive Q: Q increases with boost amount (so large boosts become narrower) [manual]. The function is [measure]; a linear map of boost to Q is the likely form [inferred].
- The cut side: whether a cut (negative values) also changes Q is [measure]. The manual says "boosting EQ" for Mid; the Max reference says "boost or cut" [source: 2].

**Mix.** Dry/Wet is a mix control, with `mix` 0 – 1 in the Max wrapper [source: 2]. The law is [measure]. If Hi-Quality oversampling is used, the dry signal may be delayed to match [measure].

**Interaction with other devices.** A compressor before Pedal gives a more even distortion; a filter with resonance before it gives "screaming" [manual]. Utility's Gain before Pedal can lower the drive further [manual].

**Stereo.** Left and right are processed independently with the same settings [inferred].

## Edge cases

- **Gain 0 %:** still distorts [manual].
- **Bass -100 % and Sub on:** the low end is cut at 100 Hz and boosted below 250 Hz at the same time; the manual presents this as a deliberate pairing [manual].
- **Fuzz at Gain 100 % and very low input:** may gate or sputter [inferred].
- **Silence in:** only dry silence out; Fuzz with a bias could output DC, which a DC-blocker (if present) removes [inferred].
- **High sample rate:** the EQ corners are fixed in Hz [inferred]; aliasing falls with Hi-Quality [manual].
- **Switching the type while playing:** may click; whether the device crossfades is [measure].
- **Latency:** none documented; Hi-Quality may add oversampling latency [measure].

## Measured

[measured 12.4.6] Behaviour runs at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.6 (`measurements/29.28-pedal/`: `pedal-types`, `pedal-gain-taper` in the top100 set; `pedal-eq-mid`, `-eq-bass-treble`, `-output-mix` in the remaining set; `pedal-curves-low-high`, `pedal-hiquality`, `pedal-switching` in the third set, rendered by Live 12.4.7: Measured › Third set), EQ flat (Bass, Mid, Treble 0 %, Mid Freq Mid), Sub off, Output 0 dB, Dry/Wet 100 %, Mono. Levels are of the 1 kHz staircase (-60 to 0 dBFS in 3 dB steps, peak in and out); "gain" is the output level minus the input level in dB, each the step's rms times the square root of 2 (the harmonics are included); harmonics are relative to the fundamental. These are observations, not a model.

### Gain at low level (the Gain control)

Small-signal gain is read from the staircase's quietest steps (-60 to -51 dBFS) and extrapolated to zero level where the gain was still falling with level; for Gain 0 % and for Distortion 50 % it is the plain value at -60 dBFS [measured 12.4.6]:

| Gain | Overdrive | Distortion | Fuzz |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 % | +7.2 dB | +6.4 dB | +13.1 dB |
| 25 % | +24.5 dB | +27.2 dB | +18.1 dB |
| 50 % | +29.4 dB | +35.7 dB | +32.0 dB |
| 75 % | +31.9 dB | +44.0 dB | +37.4 dB |
| 100 % | +33.3 dB | +48 dB (at least; already 28 % THD at -60 dBFS) | +43.8 dB |

- **The Gain control is far from linear in dB.** Overdrive gains 17 dB between 0 and 25 %, then only 4.9, 2.5 and 1.4 dB per further quarter; Distortion gains 21 dB in the first quarter, 8.5, 8.3 and about 4 dB after; Fuzz 5.0, 13.9, 5.4 and 6.4 dB. The step sizes differ per circuit, so there is no shared law for all three [measured 12.4.6]. The curve does not start from 0 dB: with Gain 0 % the small-signal gain is still +6 to +13 dB, consistent with "0 % is not no distortion" [manual] [measured 12.4.6].
- **Gain moves the knee in input level but hardly in output level.** The input level at which the gain has dropped 3 dB below its small-signal value falls as Gain rises: Overdrive -18 / -39 / -45 / -48 / -51 dBFS at Gain 0 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100 %; Distortion -15 / -36 / -45 / -51 / -54; Fuzz -21 / -27 / -42 / -48 / -54. The output level at that knee is nearly the same for every Gain in Distortion (-12.1 to -13.0 dBFS) and Fuzz (-12.0 to -14.3 dBFS), and drifts from -14 to -21.5 dBFS for Overdrive, which is the softer curve [measured 12.4.6].

### The three circuits

Output at 0 dBFS in, THD and harmonics at -12 and 0 dBFS in [measured 12.4.6]:

| Circuit, Gain | Out at 0 dBFS in | THD at -36 / -24 / -12 / 0 dBFS | H2 / H3 / H4 / H5 at -12 dBFS (dB) | H2 / H3 / H4 / H5 at 0 dBFS (dB) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overdrive 0 % | -5.1 dBFS | 0 / 2.0 / 7.5 / 5.5 % | -146 / -23 / -160 / -35 | -146 / -26 / -162 / -35 |
| Overdrive 100 % | -5.1 dBFS | 10.7 / 11.5 / 9.8 / 5.8 % | -111 / -21 / -116 / -30 | -77 / -25 / -83 / -35 |
| Distortion 0 % | -12.0 dBFS | 0 / 0 / 24.5 / 33.8 % | -117 / -12.6 / -124 / -23 | -88 / -10.7 / -90 / -17 |
| Distortion 100 % | -11.9 dBFS | 35.5 / 35.7 / 35.7 / 35.8 % | -58 / -10.4 / -59 / -16.2 | -49 / -10.4 / -51 / -16.2 |
| Fuzz 0 % | -10.6 dBFS | 0 / 30 / 56 / 71 % | -6.6 / -26 / -12.3 / -17.5 | -4.5 / -19 / -14 / -12 |
| Fuzz 100 % | -10.2 dBFS | 64 / 56 / 63 / 72 % | -6.0 / -32 / -11.6 / -14.6 | -4.3 / -18 / -14.4 / -12.1 |

- **Overdrive: odd harmonics only, soft, low distortion.** The 2nd and 4th are below -75 dB (at Gain 100 % and 0 dBFS in), so the curve is symmetric; the 3rd is at about -20 to -26 dB and the 5th at -30 to -35 dB. THD stays at 10 to 11 % at moderate level and falls to 5.5 to 5.8 % at 0 dBFS in (the output flattens: the peak is -5.1 dBFS at every Gain). Gain 0 % is clean up to -36 dBFS (THD 0.0 %) and gets 2 % at -24 dBFS [measured 12.4.6].
- **Distortion: a harder, symmetric clipper.** Odd only again; when saturated the 3rd is at -10.4 dB, the 5th at -16.2 dB and THD 35.8 %, the values of a hard-clipped sine driven four times over its clip level (-10.3 dB, -16.3 dB, 35.3 % for a sine of amplitude 4 clipped at 1), so this circuit behaves as a hard clipper at about -14.5 dBFS [measured 12.4.6] [inferred from the measurement]. At Gain 0 % it is a hard knee: perfectly clean (gain 6.4 dB, THD 0.0 %) to -24 dBFS, then 24.5 % at -12 dBFS. Its output peak saturates at -12.0 dBFS at every Gain, 7 dB below Overdrive's [measured 12.4.6].
- **Fuzz: asymmetric, very distorted.** The 2nd harmonic is the strongest product (-4.3 to -6.6 dB) with the 4th at -11.5 to -14.4 dB and the 5th at -12 to -17.5 dB; the 3rd is 14 to 26 dB lower than the 2nd. THD rises to 72 % at 0 dBFS in. Output peak -10.2 to -10.6 dBFS. At Gain 0 % it is clean to -36 dBFS (H2 -75 dB) but already 30 % THD at -24 dBFS; at Gain 25 % small signals carry about 3.5 % distortion at -36 dBFS with flat low harmonics (-33 to -39 dB), a noisy or gated character; from Gain 50 % the 2nd harmonic is at -5 to -6 dB from -36 dBFS up [measured 12.4.6].
- **The saturated output level hardly depends on Gain:** the output for a full-scale input differs by 0.0 dB (Overdrive), 0.1 dB (Distortion) and 0.2 dB (Fuzz) between Gain 25 and 100 % [measured 12.4.6].
- The three circuits are different curves, not one curve at different gains: at the same Gain 50 % and input of -12 dBFS the outputs are -11.1 (Overdrive), -11.9 (Distortion) and -10.0 dBFS (Fuzz) while the THD is 9.7, 35.7 and 63 % [measured 12.4.6].

Open from these runs: 20 Hz and 5 kHz tones, Hi-Quality. The EQ, Output and Dry/Wet follow.

- **Mid is a bell whose centre and width depend on Mid Freq**: centres 528 Hz, 1.07 kHz and about 1.9 kHz (Low, Mid, High) with widths of 1.06, 2.19 and 3.5 octaves, and a gain of +7.7, +7.1 and +6.7 dB at +100 %. A cut mirrors the boost exactly. +50 % gives 63 % of the dB of +100 % (4.5 against 7.1) and is wider (2.67 against 2.19 octaves): the Q falls as the gain is turned down, as an adaptive-Q peak does the other way round [measured 12.4.6].
- **Bass is a peak, not a shelf**: +13.1 dB at 96 Hz (1.55 octaves wide) with +4.0 dB left at 40 Hz and nothing above 400 Hz; +50 % gives 56 % of the dB (7.3) and a wider bell, and the cut mirrors it [measured 12.4.6].

- **Sub adds a low shelf of +7.9 dB** below about 325 Hz (half-gain edge), and is additive in dB with Bass: with Bass -100 % the response is -13.1 + 7.2 = -5.9 dB at 97 Hz and -4.0 + 7.8 = +3.8 dB at 40 Hz [measured 12.4.6]. None of the EQ moves anything above 6 kHz except Treble (16 kHz within 0.3 dB for the others).

   Partly: 1 kHz only, 3 dB steps, for every type at Gain 0, 25, 50, 75, 100 % (Measured › The three circuits, Gain at low level): saturated output level, THD and harmonics of each circuit. Still open: 20 Hz and 5 kHz (pre-filtering, low-end behaviour) and gating in Fuzz.

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** soft (tanh) and asymmetric curves (Overdrive and Fuzz: an asymmetric curve with a bias input gives even and odd harmonics); oversampling (Hi-Quality, or always on); a DC blocker; biquad peaks (Bass, Mid), a low shelf (Sub) and a high shelf (Treble); parameter smoothing; dB conversions.
- **Missing:** a diode-style hard-knee clipper with pre-emphasis for Distortion; a level-dependent bias for Fuzz; the adaptive-Q mapping for the EQ (a function from boost to Q: needs measurement); a three-type switch with crossfade.
- **Hard to match:** the character of the three circuits; the EQ gain ranges and adaptive Q.
- **Related:** Overdrive (29.27) is a band-pass, drive into a biased asymmetric soft curve, a tone filter and Dynamics. Pedal differs: three circuit types, no band-pass before the distortion, a three-band EQ plus Sub after it, Gain 0 – 100 %.
- **`.als` element:** `Pedal` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements in the set saved from Live 12.4.6 [als 12.4.6], each holding a `Manual` value: `On` (bool), `Type` (index 0 – 2), `Gain` (the control the API calls `Drive`; 0..1 fraction), `Output` (dB), `Bass`, `Mid`, `Treble` (−1..1 fractions, 0 = 0 %), `MidFreq` (index 0 – 2; the loaded value is 1), `Sub` (bool) and `DryWet` (fraction, 1 = 100 %). The element called `Gain` is the Gain knob (the API's `Drive`); there is no separate gain parameter.
