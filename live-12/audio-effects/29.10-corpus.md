# Corpus (manual 29.10)

**Family:** reverb and resonance
**Note status:** dump checked

## Summary

A resonant-body effect. Whatever audio goes in strikes a physically modelled object (a beam,
marimba bar, string, membrane, plate, or a pipe or tube) and the object rings at a tuned
frequency, in stereo through two resonators. The tuning can be fixed in Hz or follow incoming
MIDI notes, an LFO can modulate it, and a band-pass filter, a bleed of the dry signal and a
limiter finish the output. It is the resonator section of the Collision instrument, offered as an
effect [manual, source: 2, 3]. Typical uses: tuning drums to a key, adding body and ring to
percussion, and metallic or wooden resonant colouring.

## Signal flow

```text
                         +--> resonator L (modal model) --Pos. L--+
in --(Dry/Wet: input    -+                                          +--Width--> [Bleed: + input] --> [band-pass Filter] --> Gain --> limiter --+
     level to the        +--> resonator R (modal model) --Pos. R--+                                                                              |
     resonators)                                                                                                                                  v
in ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- dry --------------------------------------------------> sum --> out
LFO (two LFOs, one per channel) -> resonator frequency
MIDI (sidechain): note -> Tune/Transpose (Frequency); Note Off -> mute (Off Decay)
```

- Two resonators, one per stereo side, of the same type; Spread detunes them against each
  other and Pos. L and Pos. R pick different listening points [manual].
- Dry/Wet works at the resonators' input: lowering it does not cut resonances already
  ringing, it stops new input from being processed [manual]. So the dry path is
  `input * dry`, the wet path takes `input * wet` into the resonators, and the ring-out is
  never faded [manual, inferred].
- Bleed is mixed in after the resonators [manual]; whether it comes before or after the filter
  and Gain is [measure].
- The order of Filter, Gain and the limiter is [inferred]: filter, gain, limiter. The limiter's
  LED sits beside Gain [manual].

## Behaviour

- **Model.** Each object type is a bank of modes: parallel resonant filters at the object's
  partial frequencies, each with its own damping, struck by the input [inferred; modal
  synthesis is the standard way to build such resonators and the manual describes "physical
  modeling technology" with inharmonicity and overtone count]. The input is the excitation;
  there is no internal noise or pulse generator [manual].
- **Mode ratios (physics-based, to be checked by measurement) [inferred]:**
  - String: close to harmonic (1, 2, 3 ...) with a little stiffness-based stretch, which Inharm
    scales.
  - Beam (free-free bar): about 1, 2.76, 5.40, 8.93, 13.34 times the fundamental.
  - Marimba: a beam whose first overtones are tuned by the arch cut; often about 1 : 4 : 10.
  - Membrane (rectangular): modes `sqrt(m^2 + (n / ratio)^2)` for integers m and n, Ratio
    changing the aspect.
  - Plate (rectangular): modes with a squared dependence, Ratio changing the aspect.
  - Pipe: odd harmonics (1, 3, 5) when closed at one end, becoming all harmonics when open at
    both ends, Opening interpolating between them; Tube: open at both ends, all harmonics.
- **Decay and Material.** Each partial's damping is a function of its frequency; Material
  tilts that function: low values damp the highs faster, high values damp the lows faster
  [manual]. A usual form is `damping(f) = b1 + b2 f^2` with Decay shifting b1 [inferred].
- **Hit and Pos. L / Pos. R.** The excitation and pickup weights of each mode follow a spatial
  mode shape evaluated at the hit or listen position (for example a sine of mode number
  times position). At 0 % (centre) the even modes of a symmetric object vanish; toward the
  edge more modes appear [inferred]. Pipe and Tube ignore both [manual].
- **Bright.** A tilt on the mode amplitudes [manual].
- **Quality.** The number of modes calculated (Eco fewest) [manual].
- **Pipe and Tube.** No Quality, Material, Bright, Inharm, Hit, Pos. L and Pos. R and no
  Bleed; Radius replaces Material, and Opening applies to Pipe only [manual]. The model for these two
  is likely a waveguide (a delay loop with end reflections) rather than modes [inferred].
- **Tune.** Fundamental in Hz. With Frequency on, the held MIDI note sets the pitch and Tune
  (as Transpose) and Fine add to it; PB Range scales pitch bend [manual, source: 2].
- **Spread.** Offsets the two resonators' frequencies in opposite directions [manual].
- **LFO.** Modulates the resonant frequency (pitch) of the two resonators, each with its own
  LFO; Phase offsets the LFOs of the two channels; Spin gives them slightly different
  speeds; the LFO Amount scale is [measure] [manual].
- **Off Decay.** At Note Off the damping is raised according to the slider so the ring stops
  fast (100 %: at once) [manual].
- **Dry/Wet and ring-out.** See Signal flow; the dry/wet is on the excitation level [manual].
- **Limiter.** A built-in limiter on the output; its threshold, attack and release are
  [measure] [manual].
- **Stereo.** The two resonators produce left and right; a mono input feeds both
  [inferred].

## Edge cases

- Tune at the extreme top: resonator frequency above Nyquist; must be clamped [inferred].
- Tune very low with Eco quality: little energy, hence Bleed to restore highs [manual].
- Frequency on with no MIDI track selected or no notes held: the last note is kept [inferred];
  at the start, the Tune value applies [measure].
- Several notes held: Last/Low picks one; one resonator frequency at a time, so Corpus is
  monophonic [manual, inferred].
- Off Decay 100 % with very short notes: the ring is muted immediately at Note Off [manual].
- Switching Resonance Type while ringing: the old model is replaced; whether it clicks or
  crossfades is [measure].
- Pipe or Tube selected: controls listed above are disabled/hidden [manual].
- Very high Gain or resonance: the limiter engages and its LED lights [manual].
- LFO shapes noise: Phase and Spin have no effect [manual].
- Silence in: the resonators ring out and then fall silent; denormals must be flushed
  [inferred].
- Transport stop, tempo change: synced LFO re-locks to the song position [inferred].

## Measurement plan

1. ~~**Ranges and defaults.**~~ Answered by the dump of 12.4.6 (see Controls), which lists one
   range per parameter. Still open: whether the ranges change with the Resonance Type (the dump was
   taken only with the loaded type, Beam).
2. **Impulse responses per type.** An impulse (or a short noise burst) at Dry/Wet 100 %,
   Bleed 0, Filter off, defaults: render the output and take a long FFT to list the partial
   frequencies and amplitudes of each type at several Tune values; compare against the ratios
   above; sweep Inharm and Ratio and read partial shifts.
3. **Decay.** Per partial, fit exponential decay time at Decay and Material extremes to find the
   damping law, and the maximum and minimum decay times; same for Radius on Pipe and Tube.
4. **Hit and Pos.** Sweep Hit at fixed Pos., and Pos. L and R at fixed Hit; record partial
   amplitudes per setting.
5. **Quality.** Count the partials above the noise floor at Eco, (Mid), High.
6. **Width and Spread.** L/R correlation and inter-channel partial offsets in cents versus Spread.
7. **LFO.** Render a sine-input to the resonators; read the modulation depth versus Amount (in
   cents), the Rate range and the sync list, each shape's wave, Phase, Offset, Spin.
8. **Filter.** Noise through Filter on versus off; read centre frequency, bandwidth, slope.
9. **Bleed, Gain, limiter.** Level steps; limiter threshold and timing with a loud burst; LED.
10. **Dry/Wet.** Burst in, drop Dry/Wet during the ring: confirm the ring is not cut; read
    the dry and wet levels at 50 %.
11. **MIDI.** Play notes from a MIDI track: Frequency on, Transpose, Fine, PB Range, Last/Low
    priority with overlapping notes, Off Decay at 0, 50, 100 %.
12. **Latency.** Impulse with Dry/Wet at 0 % and 100 %.

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** a resonant biquad band-pass per mode and for the Filter section; an LFO
  with sine, triangle, saw, square and stepped noise (smooth noise needs an interpolated random
  source); parameter smoothing; dB conversions; a DC blocker.
- **Also needed:** a modal resonator bank (per-mode two-pole resonators or a sinusoidal bank
  with exponential decays), the mode-ratio tables for the seven types, a waveguide for Pipe and
  Tube, a limiter (likely one shared with other devices), a MIDI side-chain input for an effect
  ([common.md](common.md) › MIDI side-chain), a per-note state for Off Decay.
- **Hard to match:** the physical-model details (the model comes from a collaboration with
  Applied Acoustics Systems, so the exact modal data is theirs) [manual]. Build the models
  from published acoustics, not from the original, and compare by measurement.
- **Control mapping:** Tune is in Hz in the effect, unlike Collision's semitones [manual,
  source: 3]; Transpose replaces it under MIDI.
- **.als element:** `Corpus` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `ResonanceType`,
  `ResonatorQuality` (stored 2), `Frequency` (the Tune, stored 0.5, on the internal 0..1 scale, not Hz),
  `Transpose`, `FineTranspose`, `Detune` (Spread), `Decay` (stored 0.5, internal scale), `FreqDamping`
  (Material), `Radius`, `AmpFreq` (Bright), `Inharmonics`, `TubeOpening` (Opening), `Ratio`,
  `ExcitationX` (Hit), `ListeningXL`, `ListeningXR` (stored 0.1 and 0.9), `LfoOnOff`, `LfoType`, `LfoSync`,
  `LfoRate` (stored 0.5, internal scale), `LfoSyncRate` (stored 4), `LfoStereoMode`, `LfoSpin`, `LfoPhase`
  (degrees, stored 180), `LfoOffset`, `LfoAmount`, `FilterOn`, `FilterMidFreq` (Hz, stored 1000),
  `FilterBandWidth` (stored 4), `MidiPitch` (MIDI Frequency), `MidiMode`, `PitchBendRange` (5), `MidiGate`
  (Note Off), `DecayOnNoteOff` (Off Decay), `Drive` (stored 0.5; presumably the Gain, since no other
  element fits), `StereoWidth`, `Bleed`, `DryWet` (stored 0.5). Several pairings are by name only.
