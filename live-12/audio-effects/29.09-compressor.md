# Compressor (manual 29.9)

**Family:** dynamics
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (first run, top100 and remaining runs, [Behaviour](#behaviour))

## Summary

A feed-forward dynamics device that turns the gain down when the level passes a threshold (downward compression), or, in its Expand mode, turns the gain up above the threshold (upward expansion). It has a soft knee, a Peak or RMS level detector, a linear or logarithmic envelope, three lookahead times, automatic makeup, a dry/wet mix for parallel compression, and a side-chain with its own filter. It is the general-purpose compressor; the bus-style one is Glue Compressor (29.21) and the limiting one is Limiter (29.24).

## Signal flow

Stages marked with the tag that supports them. Shared parts: [common.md](common.md) (side-chain section, dry/wet).

```text
input ──┬──────────────► lookahead delay (0 / 1 / 10 ms) [manual] ──► gain element ──► makeup + Out ──► dry/wet ──► output
        │                                                                   ▲                             ▲
        └─► detector input = mix of (own input, external side-chain × Gain)  │                             │
              by the side-chain Dry/Wet [manual]                             │                    dry = input (delayed to match?) [measure]
              └─► side-chain EQ (optional) [manual]
                    └─► level detector: Peak or RMS [manual]
                          └─► gain computer: threshold, ratio, knee [manual]; Expand inverts the sense [manual]
                                └─► envelope: attack / release, Lin or Log, Auto release [manual]
                                      └─► gain reduction ─► orange GR meter ──────┘
```

- The detector sees the signal before the lookahead delay, so the gain can already be falling when the delayed audio arrives [manual, 29.9: lookahead exists to avoid reacting late; inferred for the exact tap].
- The side-chain only feeds the detector; it is never heard [manual, 29.9.1].
- Dry/Wet crosses the compressed signal with the uncompressed one; at 0 % the device is effectively bypassed [manual].
- Order of makeup, Out and Dry/Wet is not stated. Most likely the dry/wet blend takes the gain-reduced signal after makeup and Out, so Dry/Wet at 0 % is the unity-gain input [inferred] [measure].

## Behaviour

**Gain computer.** Above the threshold the output level rises 1 dB for every `ratio` dB of input (manual's 3:1 example: +3 dB in, +1 dB out) [manual]. With Expand, the dB amount above the threshold is multiplied (ratio 1:2 means 1 dB in gives 2 dB out) [manual]. Measured: Expansion Ratio 2 gives 1.5 dB out per dB in above the threshold, and 1.5 gives 1.33: the gain added is (x - T)(1 - 1/r) [measured 12.4.6]. The curve is a standard static compressor characteristic in dB [inferred].

**Knee.** The manual's example (Knee 10 dB, threshold −20 dB): subtle compression starts at −30 dB and signals at −10 dB are fully compressed. That is a transition spanning threshold − Knee to threshold + Knee, a width of twice the knob value [manual]. This differs from the common convention where Knee is the whole width. The renders confirm it: the knob value is a half-width, and the transition is the textbook quadratic soft knee over threshold ± Knee [measured 12.4.6] (below).

**Detector.** Peak mode follows the instantaneous absolute value of the (side-chained, filtered) signal, probably with a very fast internal smoothing, because the device is reported to click with very fast attacks in Peak mode and to behave better in RMS or with 10 ms lookahead [source: 7: a user report quoted by a search summary] [inferred]. RMS mode averages the squared signal over a short window before the dB conversion; window length [measure] (a 10 ms to 50 ms exponential or boxcar window is a common choice [inferred]). The saved set stores two constants that have no control, `RmsTimeShort` = 8 and `RmsTimeLong` = 250 [als 12.4.6]; from the names, probably RMS window times in ms, perhaps a short and a long window combined [inferred] [measure]. Stereo handling: one detector for both channels, so the stereo image does not move [inferred] [measure]; whether it uses the maximum of the two sides or their sum is [measure].

**Envelope.** In Lin mode the gain reduction is smoothed with fixed attack and release times regardless of how much reduction there is [manual]. In Log mode the smoothing time shortens as the reduction grows, so sharply compressed peaks release faster [manual]. The most likely structure is smoothing in the dB domain for one mode and in the linear-gain domain for the other, as in the Giannoulis, Massberg and Reiss design (the manual says Compressor's algorithms were redone in Live 9 with Dr. Joshua Reiss of Queen Mary, University of London) [manual] [source: 9] [inferred]. Live 8's old Opto mode (non-linear release, quick at first and slower as the reduction nears zero) is the same idea that Log now covers [source: 3]. Whether Attack and Release are 63 % time constants or 10 %–90 % times is [measure]. Measured in Lin mode: Attack's t63 is close to the setting (1.2 ms for 1 ms, 9.5 ms for 10 ms), and Release behaves as a linear-domain time constant of the level, so the gain recovers at about 8.69 x (1 - 1/ratio) dB per Release time (below) [measured 12.4.6]. Attack and release may be combined "smooth-branching" or "decoupled" [inferred] [measure].

**Auto release.** The release time is set from the incoming audio instead of the knob [manual]. No source gives the rule. Candidates: two or more cascaded time constants that depend on the crest of the reduction, as in typical programme-dependent release [inferred] [measure]. The saved set stores `ReleaseTimeShort` = 15 and `ReleaseTimeLong` = 1500 [als 12.4.6], constants with no control; from the names, probably a fast and a slow release in ms that auto release moves between [inferred], so test the measurement against 15 ms and 1.5 s first.

**Lookahead.** The audio is delayed by the lookahead (0, 1 or 10 ms) so the attack can finish before the peak arrives [manual]. The device reports latency equal to the lookahead when it is enabled [source: 5, second-hand]. Peaks can still overshoot if Attack is longer than the lookahead [inferred].

**Makeup.** With Makeup on, the output is raised to compensate for the level the threshold and ratio take away; it is off for external side-chain [manual]. The rule is not given. A common rule: add `−threshold × (1 − 1/ratio)` dB, or compensate so that a 0 dBFS input stays at 0 dBFS [inferred] [measure]. Whether it also depends on Knee and Attack/Release is [measure]. Out is added on top [inferred].

**Dry/Wet.** Linear crossfade of compressed signal and input, per [common.md](common.md); whether the dry path is delayed by the lookahead is [measure].

**Measured in Live 12.4.6.** [measured 12.4.6] A behaviour run at 44.1 kHz with the defaults (Ratio 4:1, Knee 6 dB, RMS, Attack 1 ms, Release 30 ms) and a 1 kHz sine staircase whose levels are peak dBFS. These are observations, not a model:

- With Threshold at -28.8 dB, a -6 dBFS peak sine was reduced by 14.85 dB. That equals (-6 - 3 - (-28.8)) x (1 - 1/4): the RMS detector reads the sine 3 dB below its peak and the slope above threshold is 1/ratio [measured 12.4.6].
- Knee 6 dB is a half-width. At Threshold -28.8, -14 and -4 dB the gain change from 10 dB below to 10 dB above the threshold fits a quadratic soft knee spanning threshold ± 6 dB (12 dB wide) to within 0.01 dB at every 3 dB step, taking the detector level as the sine's peak minus 3 dB. A 6 dB-wide knee misses by up to 0.5 dB just above the threshold [measured 12.4.6].
- With Threshold at -inf dB (the bottom of the range) the static slope was 1:4 across -60 to -6 dBFS [measured 12.4.6].
- Bypass (device off) and the default (0 dB threshold) passed the impulse at 0 ms and 0 dB [measured 12.4.6].
- With Threshold -28.8 dB, for a step from -20 to -6 dBFS around the threshold (resolution 1 ms), the attack t63 was about 6 ms and the release t63 about 100 ms, against the knob values of 1 ms and 30 ms [measured 12.4.6]. The measured times are longer than the knob values; no conclusion about the time constants is drawn here.

**Top100 runs (Live 12.4.6).** [measured 12.4.6] `compressor-ratio`, `-knee`, `-detector`, `-attack`, `-release` in `measurements/29.09-compressor/`, 44.1 kHz. Static runs: Threshold -20 dB, Peak, Knee 0, Attack and Release at their minimum, Lookahead 0, Makeup off; levels are of the 1 kHz staircase in peak dBFS.

- **Ratio.** Gain at -12, -6 and 0 dBFS: Ratio 2: -3.79, -6.75, -9.74 dB; 4: -5.67, -10.12, -14.60 dB; 10: -6.80, -12.14, -17.51 dB; inf: -7.56, -13.48, -19.44 dB; nothing below the threshold [measured 12.4.6]. The slope between -6 and 0 dBFS gives ratios of 1.99, 3.95, 9.5 and over 100: the slope above the threshold is 1/ratio. The amounts are 3 to 5 % short of (x - T)(1 - 1/ratio) for a peak detector (-9.74 against -10 at Ratio 2), with 2 to 7 % THD: at the minimum release the gain follows the waveform within each cycle [measured 12.4.6].
- **Peak and RMS.** In Peak mode the threshold applies to the sine's peak (Ratio 4: -1.33 dB at -18 dBFS, 2 dB over); in RMS mode to a level 3 dB below the peak: -3.75, -8.25 and -12.75 dB at -12, -6 and 0 dBFS are exactly (x - 3 + 20) x 3/4, with no THD [measured 12.4.6].
- **Knee** (Ratio 4, Peak). Knee 3, 6, 10 and 18 dB all fit the quadratic soft knee over threshold ± Knee, (1 - 1/R)(x - T + K)² / (4K), within the same 3 to 5 % shortfall as the hard knee: Knee 18 dB gives -0.04, -0.62, -1.93, -3.99, -6.80 and -10.35 dB at -36, -30, -24, -18, -12 and -6 dBFS against 0.04, 0.67, 2.04, 4.17, 7.04 and 10.67 predicted [measured 12.4.6]. The half-width reading holds from 3 to 18 dB.
- **Expand.** Expansion Ratio 1 changes nothing; 1.5 and 2 raise the gain above the threshold by +0.59, +2.53, +4.52, +6.51 dB (1.5) and +0.89, +3.80, +6.78, +9.77 dB (2) at -18, -12, -6 and 0 dBFS [measured 12.4.6]. That is (x - T)(1 - 1/r): the compressor's reduction at the same ratio with the sign turned, an output slope of 2 - 1/r above the threshold (1.5 for a ratio of 2, not the 2 the manual's wording suggests) [measured 12.4.6].
- **Attack** (Lin, Threshold -24 dB, Ratio 10, Release 100 ms; the -30 to -6 dBFS step, about 16 dB of reduction, 1 ms resolution): t63 / t90 of the gain change: Attack 0.1 ms: under 1 ms; 1 ms: 1.2 / 4.0 ms; 10 ms: 9.5 / 30.2 ms; 100 ms: 73 / 183 ms (only 12.7 dB reached). RMS: 1 ms: 1.5 / 7.5 ms; 10 ms: 11.0 / 44.9 ms [measured 12.4.6]. So Attack is close to a 63 % time constant in Peak mode; RMS adds its window's lag.
- **Release** (Lin, Attack 1 ms, the -6 to -30 dBFS step): t63 / t90: Release 10 ms: 13.7 / 20.4 ms; 50 ms: 70 / 104 ms; 100 ms: 141 / 209 ms; 200 ms: 283 / 421 ms; 1000 ms: not finished within the 1 s step [measured 12.4.6]. The gain returns along a nearly straight line in dB, about 7.4 dB per Release time at first and slowing towards the end, not an exponential in dB (t90/t63 is 1.5, not 2.3). That fits a level detector that decays exponentially with the Release time constant in the linear domain, seen through the dB gain computer: 8.69 x (1 - 1/10) = 7.8 dB per time constant [inferred from the measurement].

**Remaining runs (Live 12.4.6).** [measured 12.4.6] `compressor-env-mode`, `-lookahead`, `-makeup` in `measurements/29.09-compressor/`, 44.1 kHz, Peak, Knee 0, Threshold -24 dB (-20 dB for Makeup) unless stated. These are observations, not a model:

- **Env Mode** (Attack 10 ms, Release 300 ms, the -30 to -6 dBFS burst and back; t63 / t90 of the gain change, 1 ms resolution):

  | Case | reduction | Attack t63 / t90 | Release t63 / t90 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Lin, Ratio 2 | 8.7 dB | 10.2 / 34.6 ms | 414.5 / 618 ms |
  | Lin, Ratio 10 | 15.7 dB | 10.2 / 34.6 ms | 415.0 / 618.5 ms |
  | Log, Ratio 2 | 8.6 dB | 15.7 / 43.4 ms | 273.6 / 578 ms, 0.4 dB still left at the end |
  | Log, Ratio 10 | 15.4 dB | 15.4 / 43.4 ms | 273.6 / 578 ms, 0.8 dB still left |
  | Auto release, Lin | 14.8 dB | 9.2 / 29.4 ms | 116 / 170 ms |
  | Auto release, Log | 14.3 dB | 13.9 / 37.9 ms | 87.5 / 196 ms, 0.0 dB at the end |

  - **Lin's times do not depend on the amount of reduction**: 8.7 and 15.7 dB give the same attack and release (the release t63 is 1.38 times the set 300 ms, as 1.4 times at 10 to 200 ms in the Release run above) [measured 12.4.6].
  - **Log is slower to attack and quicker at first on release**: attack t63 15.5 ms against 10.2 ms, release t63 274 ms against 415 ms, with a tail that leaves 0.4 to 0.8 dB of reduction at the end of the window (an exponential in dB, which does not reach zero). The times did not change between 8.6 and 15.4 dB of reduction, so the "release shortens with reduction" of the manual does not show between those two [measured 12.4.6].
  - **Auto release ignores the Release knob**: with 300 ms set the release t63 is 116 ms (Lin) and 87.5 ms (Log), t90 170 and 196 ms; the attack stays near the set 10 ms (9.2 and 13.9 ms). How it depends on the burst length was not measured [measured 12.4.6].
- **Lookahead** (Attack 1 ms, Release 100 ms; `compressor-lookahead`): the impulse comes back at 0 ms, 0.0 dB at Lookahead 1 and 10 ms and at Dry/Wet 50 %: **Live compensates the device's delay in the export**, so the latency itself cannot be read from these renders (the title-bar readout can). The burst attack improves with Lookahead: t63 / t90 0.73 / 3.0 ms at 1 ms and 0.73 / 0.73 ms at 10 ms, against 1.22 / 3.97 ms at 0 ms, and with Attack 10 ms and Lookahead 10 ms t90 is 20.2 ms where Attack 10 ms without Lookahead gave 30.2 ms (Attack run above). At Dry/Wet 50 % the sweep is flat at -3.5 to -3.8 dB (the mix of the dry input and the -10.5 dB wet at 5 to 15 kHz): **no comb, so the dry path is delayed to match the lookahead** [measured 12.4.6].
- **Makeup** (Peak, Knee 0, Attack and Release at their minimum, a 1 kHz staircase; gain at -60 dBFS, below the threshold):

  | Threshold, Ratio | Makeup gain |
  |---|---|
  | -20 dB, 2 | +7.0 dB |
  | -20 dB, 4 | +10.5 dB |
  | -20 dB, inf | +14.0 dB |
  | -40 dB, 4 | +21.0 dB |

  **Makeup = 0.7 x (-Threshold in dB) x (1 - 1/Ratio)**: 0.7 x 20 x 0.5 = 7.0, x 0.75 = 10.5, x 1 = 14.0, and 0.7 x 40 x 0.75 = 21.0, exact to 0.05 dB. It is 70 % of the gain reduction a full-scale (0 dBFS) peak would receive at a hard knee [measured 12.4.6]. The compressed curve above the threshold then follows (Ratio 4, Threshold -20: +10.5 dB at and below -24 dBFS, +9.2 dB at -18, +4.8 dB at -12, +0.4 dB at -6, -4.1 dB at 0 dBFS), with THD rising to 5.4 % at 0 dBFS because the attack and release at their minimum follow the 1 kHz waveform [measured 12.4.6].

**Meters.** The GR meter shows the instantaneous reduction in dB [manual]. In Expand mode it presumably shows the gain increase [measure]. The Output meter shows the level after Out [manual].

**Side-chain.** External signal × Gain is blended with the own input by Side-chain Dry/Wet into the detector; the EQ filters the blend [manual]. Automatic Makeup is not available with an external side-chain [manual]. Whether the order is "mix then EQ" or "EQ each then mix" is [measure] (the manual's wording suggests the EQ filters whatever the detector is given).

## Edge cases

- Ratio 1 (or Expand 1:1): no gain change at any threshold [manual].
- Knee 0 dB: no compression below the threshold, full compression at and above [manual].
- Threshold at its lowest value: the device is almost always compressing; the GR meter's range and whether −inf is a state are [measure].
- Attack at its minimum: clicks and "buzziness" from distortion [manual]; with Peak mode this can be audible as clicks [source: 7].
- Release at its minimum: pumping [manual].
- Lookahead change while playing: a change of delay, probably with a click or a short crossfade [measure]; the latency reported to the host changes, which in Live forces a recomputation of delay compensation [source: 5] [inferred].
- Mono input: the detector sees one channel; output is mono [inferred].
- Silence in: no gain change, GR meter returns to zero [inferred]. Denormals: not stated [measure].
- Sample-rate dependence of Attack/Release/RMS window: should be in seconds [inferred]; [measure] at 44.1 and 96 kHz.
- Switching Peak / RMS / Expand while compressing: detector state continuity and clicks [measure].

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** biquad low-pass, band-pass, high-pass, peak, low and high shelf (RBJ cookbook) for the side-chain EQ; one-pole smoothing for envelopes and parameter ramps; dB conversions. The gain computer and the attack/release smoothing in the decibel domain are the textbook design of Giannoulis, Massberg and Reiss [source: 9].
- **Also needed:** a Peak detector and an RMS detector (a running mean of squares; the window is [measure]); an Expand mode in the gain computer; Lin and Log envelopes; a programme-dependent release for Auto; Makeup (its rule is [measure]); a lookahead delay line of 0, 1 or 10 ms that reports its latency; stereo linking of the detector; the transfer-curve and activity displays, the GR and output meters, side-chain Listen.
- **Knee:** take Live's value as a half-width: the transition runs from threshold − Knee to threshold + Knee [measured 12.4.6].
- **Ranges to cover** [dump 12.4.6]: Threshold -inf (stored as -70 dB, below) to +6 dB, Ratio 1:1 to inf:1, Attack 0.01 ms to 1 s, Release 1 ms to 3 s, Knee 0 to 18 dB, Out ±36 dB.

`.als` import: the device's element is `Compressor2` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `Threshold` (stored as linear gain, 1 = 0 dB; -28.8 dB is `0.03630783036`, +6 dB `1.99526238`, and the panel's -inf is stored `0.0003162277571`, -70 dB), `Ratio` (stored 4 = 4:1), `ExpansionRatio` (stored 1.15), `Attack` (ms, stored 1), `Release` (ms, stored 30), `AutoReleaseControlOnOff`, `Gain` (the Out, dB, stored 0), `GainCompensation` (the Makeup), `DryWet` (stored 1 = 100 %), `Model` (0 Peak, 1 RMS, 2 Expand; stored 1), `LegacyModel` (stored 1; not a panel control in the dump, probably the pre-Live 9 model switch), `LogEnvelope` (boolean, true = Log), `LegacyEnvFollowerMode` (stored 0, also not in the dump), `Knee` (dB, stored 6), `LookAhead` (step index), `SideListen`, `SideChain/OnOff`, `SideChain/RoutedInput/Volume` (stored 1, presumably the side-chain Gain as linear gain), `SideChain/DryWet` (stored 1), `SideChainEq_On`, `SideChainEq_Mode` (stored 5 = High pass), `SideChainEq_Freq` (Hz, stored 80), `SideChainEq_Q`, `SideChainEq_Gain`.
