# Utility (manual 29.40)

**Family:** analysis and utility
**Note status:** dump checked

## Summary

A small tool device for gain, balance, stereo width, mono summing, low-frequency mono, phase
inversion per channel, choosing or swapping the left and right channel, muting and DC removal.
It is placed anywhere in a chain, most often for volume automation that leaves the track fader free,
for narrowing the bass, or for phase tricks [manual].

## Signal flow

Shared parts: [common.md](common.md) (title bar only; no side-chain, no dry/wet).

```text
input ─► Ø L / Ø R ─► Channel Mode ─► Width (or Mid/Side) ─► Mono ─► Bass Mono ─► Gain ─► Balance ─► Mute ─► DC ─► out
          [manual]     [manual]        [manual]                [manual] [manual]    [manual] [manual]   [manual] [manual]
```

The order above is the panel order, not a stated signal order. The manual says
only: the Channel Mode ignores the other channel; Width and Mid/Side are disabled in Left and Right
modes; Mute silences the input; and the DC switch is audible only when the signal has a DC offset that a later non-linear device would act on [manual]. The true
order (is Mute before or after Gain; does Balance come before Bass Mono; where DC sits) is [measure].
A workable starting guess is DC filter, then one 2×2 matrix (channel selection, phase inversion,
width, mid/side, mono), then bass mono, then gain and balance with mute [inferred].

### Context menu and hidden options

- **Mid/Side Mode** (right-click the Width control): switches the knob between Width and Mid/Side.
  Turning it on resets the width to 100 % and the Mid/Side knob starts at 0 [manual] [source: 3].
- Other options in the menu are not described [measure].

## Behaviour

- **Phase.** Polarity inversion per input channel, before everything else [inferred]. A fix in
  12.1: the cancellation of two tracks through the phase buttons no longer depends on the song start
  time [source: 7]; so phase inversion is sample-exact [inferred].
- **Channel Mode.** Left: the right channel is discarded, the left one is sent to both outputs;
  Right: the same from the right; Swap: left and right exchanged; Stereo: pass [manual]. Whether the phase buttons act on the
  input channels before the mode (so Ø R with mode Right inverts the signal now on both outputs) or
  after it is [measure].
- **Width.** Scales the side signal of the wet stereo signal: 0 % gives mono (the mid), 100 %
  leaves it unchanged and above 100 % raises the side component [manual]; forum measurements say
  the side gets louder above 100 % and that at 200 % in one case the channels came out as
  phase-inverted copies with a loss of bass [source: 4] [source: 5]; this fits a side gain of 2
  on near-mono material [inferred]. The usual M/S formulation is
  M = (L + R) / 2, S = (L - R) / 2, out = M ± width · S [inferred]; the normalisation of mid and side
  and the law above 100 % are [measure]. Stored range 0 – 4 means a 400 % maximum [source: 3].
- **Mid/Side.** From 0 to 100M the stereo signal is blended to mono (side gain from 1 to 0, mid
  unchanged); from 0 to 100S the mid is reduced and the side emphasised, ending with only the side
  (L and R opposite in sign) [manual]. Whether Live keeps one component at full and turns the other
  down linearly with the knob, or applies a sound-law (constant-power) curve,
  is [measure]. Whether the knob is in the amplitude or a dB domain is [measure]. The wrapper
  describes `midside` as two floats in 0 – 1 for the gain of mid and side [source: 2].
- **Mono.** Converts to mono [manual]; the sum is most likely (L + R) / 2 (no level change for
  identical channels) [inferred]; with a -3 dB sum law it would differ [measure].
- **Bass Mono.** The low band below the Bass Mono Frequency is summed to mono, the rest stays
  stereo [manual]; the crossover is probably Linkwitz-Riley (the band sum is flat) [inferred];
  its order (12, 24 or 48 dB/octave) and phase behaviour are [measure]. Audition sends only the low
  band, as bass mono leaves it [manual], so the frequency can be tuned.
- **Gain.** -inf to +35 dB [manual]; wrapper range -70.6 – +35 dB, where the lowest value maps to
  -inf [source: 2] [inferred]. Keyboard steps: 1 dB from -18 to +35 dB, accelerating towards -inf
  below -18 dB [manual], which suggests the knob's taper is linear in dB above -18 dB and steeper
  below it [inferred]; the exact curve is [measure].
- **Balance.** Pans between left and right [manual]; the display runs 50L to 50R [source: 3]. The pan
  law (linear attenuation of the opposite channel, constant-power, or -3 dB centre) is [measure].
- **Mute.** Silences the incoming signal; the track's own active/mute is always last in the chain,
  and Utility's Mute can sit mid-chain, for example to cut a delay's input without killing its tail
  [manual]. Whether it ramps is [measure].
- **DC.** Filters out DC offset and extremely low frequencies far below the audible range
  [manual]; a first-order high-pass at a few hertz is the usual form [inferred]; corner [measure].
- **Smoothing.** Parameter changes presumably ramp to avoid zipper noise [inferred]; the times are
  [measure].
- **Latency.** None stated; Bass Mono's crossover is a minimum-phase IIR so none is expected
  [inferred].

## Edge cases

- **Left or Right selected:** Width and Mid/Side are disabled and have no function [manual].
- **Gain at -inf:** silence [manual]. At +35 dB overs are not limited [inferred].
- **Width at 0 %:** mono; same signal on both outputs [manual].
- **Width above 100 % on a mono signal:** a mono signal has no side, so width does nothing in a
  strict M/S model, but a forum poster saw one channel inverted [source: 4]; [measure].
- **Mid/Side 100M:** sums to mono [manual]; 100S: left and right 180° out of phase [manual].
- **Mid/Side to Width switch:** resets the width to 100 % [source: 3].
- **Bass Mono Frequency at 50 / 500 Hz:** behaviour at the ends [measure]; the frequency's units
  and ends are observed [source: 3].
- **Both phase buttons:** both channels inverted: a polarity flip of the whole signal [inferred].
- **Mute plus DC:** [measure] whether DC filter state continues running while muted.
- **Silence and denormals:** [measure].

## Measurement plan

1. **Ranges and defaults.** Settled by the dump [dump 12.4.6]: Gain −inf to +35 dB with its taper, Balance 50L – 50R, Width 0 – 400 %, Bass Mono 50 – 500 Hz default 120 Hz, and the defaults of every toggle. Still to read (not in the dump): the Mid/Side knob's range and display, and Bass Mono Audition.
2. **Order of stages.** Impulse with Ø R on, Channel Mode Right, Width 200 %, Mono on, Bass Mono on,
   Gain +6 dB, Balance 50R, Mute: compare against the model orders (the one under the signal flow is the starting guess);
   switch one at a time. In particular Mute before/after Gain; Balance before/after Bass Mono.
3. **Width law.** Pink noise with correlated (mid-only) and anti-correlated (side-only) signals at
   Width 0, 50, 100, 150, 200, 400 %: output level of mid and side separately, against width.
4. **Mid/Side law.** The same signals at Mid/Side 100M, 50M, 0, 50S, 100S: level of mid and side
   against position (linear, dB, constant power).
5. **Mono law.** Hard-panned signal and identical channels with Mono on: output level (sum scale).
6. **Bass Mono filter.** Mid-only and side-only sine sweeps at Bass Mono Frequency 50, 120, 500 Hz:
   the side level against frequency (read the crossover's slope in dB/octave, the -3 dB point, phase),
   and the mid level (flat or not); Audition's output: the low band alone.
7. **Gain.** Steps from -inf to +35 dB; check that the typed value equals the measured value and the
   curve between -18 and -inf.
8. **Balance.** A centred sine at Balance 0, 25L, 50L, 25R, 50R: left and right levels for the pan law.
9. **DC.** A DC step and a sine at 1, 5, 20 Hz through DC on: the corner and slope.
10. **Smoothing and clicks.** Automate Gain and Mute while playing a sine; look for steps and the ramp length.
11. **Phase buttons.** A track and its inverted copy through Utility: cancellation depth.
12. **Latency.** Impulse alignment with each switch on.

## Implementation notes

- **Stored values to import** [source: 3]: Gain is a linear factor (convert to dB; 0 is -inf);
  Balance -1 … 1; Width the fraction 0 – 4; `MidSideBalanceOn` switches the Width knob to Mid/Side,
  and `MidSideBalance` runs 0 – 2 with 1 as the centre; Bass Mono Frequency in Hz; Channel Mode in
  Live's order Left, Stereo, Right, Swap; `BassMonoAudition` is a plain (not automatable) flag.
- **Design points:** Live's Width and Mid/Side are one control in two modes (a context-menu
  choice); Gain's top is +35 dB; the Mid/Side and Balance laws are [measure]; the keyboard step
  behaviour (1 dB steps) and the Gain taper are UI concerns.
- **Building blocks:** parameter smoothing, a Linkwitz-Riley crossover for Bass Mono (24 dB/octave
  is a reasonable first choice), a DC blocker, dB conversions with -inf as silence.
- **Open:** the laws (Width, Mid/Side, Balance, Mono) and the stage order (Measurement plan).
- **`.als` element:** `StereoGain` [dump 12.4.6] (read from saved sets [source: 3]). Parameter elements [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `PhaseInvertL`, `PhaseInvertR`, `ChannelMode` (index, 1 = Stereo), `StereoWidth` (fraction, 1 = 100 %), `MidSideBalance` (1), `Mono`, `BassMono`, `BassMonoFrequency` (Hz, 120), `Balance` (0), `Gain` (linear gain, 1 = 0 dB), `LegacyGain` (0), `Mute`, `DcFilter`. The 12.4.6 listing has no `MidSideBalanceOn`, `BassMonoAudition` or `LegacyMode`, which earlier sets from the corpus contain [source: 3]; `LegacyGain` and `LegacyMode` are unused (unverified meaning) [source: 3].
