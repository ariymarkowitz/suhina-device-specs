# Utility (manual 29.40)

**Family:** analysis and utility
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100, remaining and automation runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

A small tool device for gain, balance, stereo width, mono summing, low-frequency mono, phase
inversion per channel, choosing or swapping the left and right channel, muting and DC removal.
It is placed anywhere in a chain, most often for volume automation that leaves the track fader free,
for narrowing the bass, or for phase tricks [manual].

## Signal flow

Shared parts: [common.md](common.md) (title bar only; no side-chain, no dry/wet).

```text
input ─► Ø L / Ø R ─► Channel Mode ─► Width (or Mid/Side) ─► Mono ─► Bass Mono ─► Gain ─► Balance ─► Mute ─► DC ─► out
          [manual]     [manual]        [manual]                [manual] [manual]    [manual] [manual]   [manual] [manual]
```

The order above is the panel order, not a stated signal order. The manual says
only: the Channel Mode ignores the other channel; Width and Mid/Side are disabled in Left and Right
modes; Mute silences the input; and the DC switch is audible only when the signal has a DC offset that a later non-linear device would act on [manual]. The true
order (is Mute before or after Gain; does Balance come before Bass Mono; where DC sits) is [measure].
A workable starting guess is DC filter, then one 2×2 matrix (channel selection, phase inversion,
width, mid/side, mono), then bass mono, then gain and balance with mute [inferred].

## Behaviour

- **Phase.** Polarity inversion per input channel, before everything else [inferred]. A fix in
  12.1: the cancellation of two tracks through the phase buttons no longer depends on the song start
  time [source: 7]; so phase inversion is sample-exact [inferred].
- **Channel Mode.** Left: the right channel is discarded, the left one is sent to both outputs;
  Right: the same from the right; Swap: left and right exchanged; Stereo: pass [manual]. Whether the phase buttons act on the
  input channels before the mode (so Ø R with mode Right inverts the signal now on both outputs) or
  after it is [measure].
- **Width.** Scales the side signal of the wet stereo signal: 0 % gives mono (the mid), 100 %
  leaves it unchanged and above 100 % raises the side component [manual]; forum measurements say
  the side gets louder above 100 % and that at 200 % in one case the channels came out as
  phase-inverted copies with a loss of bass [source: 4] [source: 5]. Measured: with
  M = (L + R) / 2 and S = (L - R) / 2, the outputs are M + w · S and M - w · S, w being the Width as a
  fraction (0 to 4); the mid is not touched and nothing is normalised, so above 100 % the opposite
  channel gets an inverted copy of a one-sided signal (Measured › Width) [measured 12.4.6]. That
  explains the forum's inverted channels on material with a strong side component [inferred].
  Stored range 0 – 4 means a 400 % maximum [source: 3].
- **Mid/Side.** From 0 to 100M the stereo signal is blended to mono (side gain from 1 to 0, mid
  unchanged); from 0 to 100S the mid is reduced and the side emphasised, ending with only the side
  (L and R opposite in sign) [manual]. Whether Live keeps one component at full and turns the other
  down linearly with the knob, or applies a sound-law (constant-power) curve,
  is [measure]. Whether the knob is in the amplitude or a dB domain is [measure]. The wrapper
  describes `midside` as two floats in 0 – 1 for the gain of mid and side [source: 2].
- **Mono.** Converts to mono [manual]; the sum is most likely (L + R) / 2 (no level change for
  identical channels) [inferred]; with a -3 dB sum law it would differ [measure].
- **Bass Mono.** The low band below the Bass Mono Frequency is summed to mono, the rest stays
  stereo [manual]. It is not a band-split crossover: the mid passes unfiltered and only the side goes
  through a third-order Butterworth high-pass (18 dB/octave, -3.0 dB and +135° at the set frequency)
  at 50, 120, 250 and 500 Hz (Measured › Bass Mono) [measured 12.4.6]. The earlier guess of a
  Linkwitz-Riley crossover was wrong. Audition sends only the low
  band, as bass mono leaves it [manual], so the frequency can be tuned.
- **Gain.** -inf to +35 dB [manual]; wrapper range -70.6 – +35 dB, where the lowest value maps to
  -inf [source: 2] [inferred]. Keyboard steps: 1 dB from -18 to +35 dB, accelerating towards -inf
  below -18 dB [manual]; the knob's taper is in the dump (Controls). The audio gain equals the
  displayed value to 0.01 dB at -60, -30, -12, +12 and +35 dB, at every input level from -60 to 0 dBFS,
  with no limiting of overs at +35 dB (in 32-bit float); -inf is exact digital silence [measured 12.4.6].
- **Balance.** Pans between left and right [manual]; the display runs 50L to 50R [source: 3]. The pan
  law (linear attenuation of the opposite channel, constant-power, or -3 dB centre) is [measure].
- **Mute.** Silences the incoming signal; the track's own active/mute is always last in the chain,
  and Utility's Mute can sit mid-chain, for example to cut a delay's input without killing its tail
  [manual]. Whether it ramps is [measure].
- **DC.** Filters out DC offset and extremely low frequencies far below the audible range
  [manual]; a first-order high-pass at a few hertz is the usual form [inferred]; corner [measure].
- **Smoothing.** Gain ramps over 4.4 ms, an S evenly in knob position centred on the change
  (Measured › Gain) [measured 12.4.6]; the other controls presumably ramp too [inferred], their
  times are [measure].
- **Latency.** None stated; Bass Mono's crossover is a minimum-phase IIR so none is expected
  [inferred].

## Edge cases

- **Left or Right selected:** Width and Mid/Side are disabled and have no function [manual].
- **Gain at -inf:** silence [manual]; the render is exactly zero [measured 12.4.6]. At +35 dB overs
  are not limited: a 0 dBFS input comes out at +35 dBFS [measured 12.4.6].
- **Width at 0 %:** mono; same signal on both outputs [manual].
- **Width above 100 % on a mono signal:** Width scales only the side (Measured › Width), so a signal
  identical in both channels passes unchanged at any Width [inferred from the measurement]; the
  forum poster's inverted channel [source: 4] fits a signal with a side component [inferred].
- **Mid/Side 100M:** sums to mono [manual]; 100S: left and right 180° out of phase [manual].
- **Mid/Side to Width switch:** resets the width to 100 % [source: 3].
- **Bass Mono Frequency at 50 / 500 Hz:** the same third-order high-pass on the side, -3 dB at 50.0
  and 500.1 Hz [measured 12.4.6]; the frequency's units and ends are observed [source: 3].
- **Both phase buttons:** both channels inverted: a polarity flip of the whole signal [inferred].
- **Mute plus DC:** [measure] whether DC filter state continues running while muted.
- **Silence and denormals:** [measure].

## Measured

[measured 12.4.6] Behaviour runs at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.6 (`measurements/29.40-utility/`:
`utility-width`, `utility-gain`, `utility-bass-mono` in the top100 set; `utility-channels-order`, `utility-balance`, `utility-dc` in the remaining set), Channel Mode Stereo,
everything else at its default. The 2x2 matrix was read from the left-only and right-only 1 kHz tones
and, per frequency, from the independent noise in the two channels (mid = L→L + R→L, side =
L→L - R→L). These are observations, not a model.

### Width

| Width | L→L | R→L | Matrix (L→L, R→L) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 % | -6.0 dB | -6.0 dB | 0.5, 0.5 |
| 50 % | -2.5 | -12.0 | 0.75, 0.25 |
| 100 % (default) | 0.0 | silent | 1, 0 |
| 150 % | +1.9 | -12.0 (inverted) | 1.25, -0.25 |
| 200 % | +3.5 | -6.0 (inverted) | 1.5, -0.5 |
| 400 % | +8.0 | +3.5 (inverted) | 2.5, -1.5 |

- The outputs are L = M + w·S and R = M - w·S with M = (L + R)/2, S = (L - R)/2 and w the Width as a
  fraction; the matrix entries are (1 ± w)/2 exactly, flat from 31.5 Hz to 16 kHz, and symmetric
  for the right input [measured 12.4.6].
- So the mid is never changed and the side has gain w: +6 dB at 200 %, +12 dB at 400 %, with no
  compensation of the overall level [measured 12.4.6].

### Gain

- Gain -60, -30, -12, +12 and +35 dB give exactly that gain (to 0.01 dB) at every staircase step from
  -60 to 0 dBFS, with no distortion; -inf gives an all-zero render [measured 12.4.6].
- **The knob's taper, whole** (from automation, `measurements/automation/`, runs `utility-gain-*`):
  with the API's knob value k (-1 to 1), Gain = 35 k dB from k = -0.5 (-17.5 dB) up to +35 dB at
  1; below, the gain is a power of (1 + k), g = g(-0.5) ((1 + k) / 0.5)^n with n = 2.0148, the
  power that keeps the slope in dB continuous at -0.5 (35 * 0.5 * ln 10 / 20). The dump's points
  agree (-29.6 dB at -0.75); a free fit gives 34.99 dB per unit, the knee at -0.4999 and
  n = 2.0146 [measured 12.4.6].
- **Automation** moves evenly in that knob position (0.03 dB rms over every segment; evenly in dB
  is 5.7 dB rms off), along the set's Bezier curves, as every parameter does
  ([automation.md](../automation.md)) [measured 12.4.6].
- **Smoothing:** a jump in Gain is ramped over a fixed 4.3 to 4.4 ms (190 to 196 samples at 44.1
  kHz, 391 at 88.2 kHz), an S-shaped ramp evenly in knob position and centred on the jump, for a
  13 dB and a 62 dB step alike ([automation.md](../automation.md) › Smoothing of jumps)
  [measured 12.4.6]. Mute's ramp is not measured.

### Bass Mono

| Bass Freq | side at fc/4 | fc/2 | fc | 2 fc | side -3 dB at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 Hz | -35.9 dB | -18.1 dB | -3.0 dB | -0.1 dB | 50.0 Hz |
| 120 Hz (default) | -36.2 | -18.2 | -3.0 | -0.1 | 120.4 Hz |
| 250 Hz | -36.1 | -18.2 | -3.0 | -0.1 | 250.3 Hz |
| 500 Hz | -36.2 | -18.2 | -3.0 | -0.1 | 500.1 Hz |

(Third-order Butterworth: -36.1, -18.1, -3.0, -0.07 dB.)

- The mid is flat (0.0 ± 0.1 dB, phase within 1°) from 20 Hz up in every case: Bass Mono filters only
  the side [measured 12.4.6].
- The side's response matches an analogue-prototype third-order Butterworth high-pass at the set
  frequency (largest complex difference 0.004 to 0.05 between fc/2 and 4 fc; second and fourth order
  differ by 0.5), +135° of phase at fc, 18 dB/octave below [measured 12.4.6].
- Because the side is phase-shifted above the corner, a one-sided 1 kHz tone leaks into the other
  channel at -26, -18.4, -12.1 and -6.0 dB for 50, 120, 250 and 500 Hz: the side's phase near fc,
  not a level change [measured 12.4.6].

### Channel Mode, Mono and the order of the stages

The 2x2 level matrix of a 1 kHz tone in the left channel alone and in the right channel alone, and the polarity of the impulse (`utility-channels-order`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Case | L to L | L to R | R to L | R to R | Impulse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| default (Stereo) | 0.0 dB | none | none | 0.0 dB | +1.000 |
| Channel Mode Left | 0.0 | 0.0 | none | none | +1.000 in both |
| Channel Mode Right | none | none | 0.0 | 0.0 | +1.000 in both |
| Channel Mode Swap | none | 0.0 | 0.0 | none | +1.000 in both |
| Mono | -6.0 | -6.0 | -6.0 | -6.0 | +1.000 |
| Mono with Ø R | -6.0 | -6.0 | -6.0 | -6.0 | silent (the sum cancels) |
| Left, with Ø R | 0.0 | 0.0 | none | none | +1.000 in both |
| Right, with Ø L | none | none | 0.0 | 0.0 | +1.000 in both |
| Balance 50R with Bass Mono 500 Hz | none | -3.0 | none | +1.7 | +1.414 right, none left |

- **Left and Right copy that channel to both outputs at unity** (no -3 dB or -6 dB: the other channel is dropped); **Swap** exchanges them; **Mono** is (L + R) / 2 in both channels (-6.0 dB per input, 0 dB for a centred signal) [measured 12.4.6].
- **The phase buttons act before Channel Mode and before the Mono sum**: Ø R with Mono cancels a centred impulse completely (the sum of the signal and its right-inverted copy), and in Left mode Ø R changes nothing at all (the impulse stays +1.000 in both outputs), as does Ø L in Right mode: an inversion of the channel that the mode throws away has no effect, so it was applied first [measured 12.4.6].
- **Bass Mono comes before Balance**: with Balance 50R and Bass Mono at 500 Hz, a left-only 1 kHz tone appears in the right output at -3.0 dB although the Balance alone (below) never moves signal between channels: the leak from the side's phase shift (-6.0 dB at 500 Hz) passes into the right channel and Balance then raises it by 3.0 dB. The left output is silent [measured 12.4.6].
- Every switch here adds no latency: the impulse is at 0 ms in every case [measured 12.4.6].

### Balance

Left-only and right-only 1 kHz tones (`utility-balance`) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Balance | L to L | R to R | Cross-feed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50L (-1) | +3.0 dB | none | none |
| 25L (-0.5) | +2.3 | -5.3 | none |
| C (0) | 0.0 | 0.0 | none |
| 25R (+0.5) | -5.3 | +2.3 | none |
| 50R (+1) | none | +3.0 | none |

- **Balance is a constant-power pan on each channel separately**: with p the internal value (-1 to 1) the left gain is sqrt 2 cos((p + 1) pi / 4) and the right gain sqrt 2 sin((p + 1) pi / 4): 1.307 and 0.541 at p = -0.5 (+2.3 and -5.3 dB), 1.414 and 0 at p = -1. The centre is unity and the near channel is raised by 3.0 dB at the end of the travel; there is no cross-feed between the channels, and the impulse gives the same numbers (L 1.307 / R 0.541 at 25L) [measured 12.4.6].

### DC Filter

With the filter on, the sweep reads -0.5 dB at 20 Hz, -0.3 at 25 Hz, -0.2 at 32 Hz, -0.1 at 40 to 63 Hz and 0.0 above 80 Hz; the stored impulse response has -5.9 dB at 5 Hz, -1.9 at 10 Hz and -0.9 at 15 Hz (`utility-dc`) [measured 12.4.6]. A one-pole high-pass at 7.5 Hz fits from 10 Hz (-1.9, -1.0, -0.6, -0.4, -0.24, -0.15 dB at 10, 15, 20, 25, 31.5, 40 Hz) and the first sample of the impulse is 0.999, as the same filter gives (0.9989); 5 Hz reads 0.8 dB lower than the model [measured 12.4.6]. The silent segment stays silent (no DC added) and the tail of the response lasts 0.13 s.

## Measurement plan

1. **Ranges and defaults.** Settled by the dump [dump 12.4.6]: Gain −inf to +35 dB with its taper, Balance 50L – 50R, Width 0 – 400 %, Bass Mono 50 – 500 Hz default 120 Hz, and the defaults of every toggle. Still to read (not in the dump): the Mid/Side knob's range and display, and Bass Mono Audition.
2. **Order of stages.** Impulse with Ø R on, Channel Mode Right, Width 200 %, Mono on, Bass Mono on,
   Gain +6 dB, Balance 50R, Mute: compare against the model orders (the one under the signal flow is the starting guess);
   switch one at a time. In particular Mute before/after Gain; Balance before/after Bass Mono.
   Partly: Ø L / Ø R act before Channel Mode and before the Mono sum, Bass Mono before Balance (Measured › Channel Mode, Mono and the order of the stages). Where Gain, Mute and DC sit was not read.
3. **Width law.** Pink noise with correlated (mid-only) and anti-correlated (side-only) signals at
   Width 0, 50, 100, 150, 200, 400 %: output level of mid and side separately, against width.
   Done: mid unchanged, side gain = Width as a fraction, no normalisation (Measured › Width).
4. **Mid/Side law.** The same signals at Mid/Side 100M, 50M, 0, 50S, 100S: level of mid and side
   against position (linear, dB, constant power).
5. **Mono law.** Hard-panned signal and identical channels with Mono on: output level (sum scale).
   Done: Mono is (L + R) / 2 into both channels, -6.0 dB per input (Measured › Channel Mode, Mono and the order of the stages).
6. **Bass Mono filter.** Mid-only and side-only sine sweeps at Bass Mono Frequency 50, 120, 500 Hz:
   the side level against frequency (read the crossover's slope in dB/octave, the -3 dB point, phase),
   and the mid level (flat or not); Audition's output: the low band alone.
   Partly: 50, 120, 250, 500 Hz read from independent L/R noise: mid flat, side through a third-order Butterworth high-pass (Measured › Bass Mono). Still open: Audition (not automatable).
7. **Gain.** Steps from -inf to +35 dB; check that the typed value equals the measured value and the
   curve between -18 and -inf.
   Done: -60, -30, -12, +12, +35 dB and -inf are exact, and the whole taper below -18 dB is read from automation (Measured › Gain).
8. **Balance.** A centred sine at Balance 0, 25L, 50L, 25R, 50R: left and right levels for the pan law.
   Done at 50L, 25L, 25R and 50R with a one-sided tone: a constant-power pan per channel with unity at the centre and +3.0 dB at the ends (Measured › Balance).
9. **DC.** A DC step and a sine at 1, 5, 20 Hz through DC on: the corner and slope.
   Partly: the DC filter is a one-pole high-pass near 7.5 Hz (Measured › DC Filter). The step response and the 1 and 5 Hz sines were not rendered.
10. **Smoothing and clicks.** Automate Gain and Mute while playing a sine; look for steps and the ramp length.
   Partly: Gain (Measured › Gain: a 4.4 ms S-shaped ramp, no steps). Still open: Mute.
11. **Phase buttons.** A track and its inverted copy through Utility: cancellation depth.
    Partly: the phase buttons invert before the channel selection and the sum, and Mono with Ø R cancels a centred impulse (Measured › Channel Mode, Mono and the order of the stages).
12. **Latency.** Impulse alignment with each switch on.
    Done for Left, Right, Swap, Mono, the phase buttons and Bass Mono: every impulse is at 0 ms (Measured › Channel Mode, Mono and the order of the stages).

## Implementation notes

- **Stored values to import** [source: 3]: Gain is a linear factor (convert to dB; 0 is -inf);
  Balance -1 … 1; Width the fraction 0 – 4; `MidSideBalanceOn` switches the Width knob to Mid/Side,
  and `MidSideBalance` runs 0 – 2 with 1 as the centre; Bass Mono Frequency in Hz; Channel Mode in
  Live's order Left, Stereo, Right, Swap; `BassMonoAudition` is a plain (not automatable) flag.
- **Design points:** Live's Width and Mid/Side are one control in two modes (a context-menu
  choice); Gain's top is +35 dB; the Mid/Side and Balance laws are [measure]; the keyboard step
  behaviour (1 dB steps) is a UI concern. The Gain taper (Measured › Gain) is where automation
  moves, so it decides how an automated Gain sounds, not only how the knob turns.
- **Building blocks:** parameter smoothing, a third-order Butterworth high-pass on the side for Bass
  Mono (the mid is not filtered; Measured › Bass Mono), a DC blocker, dB conversions with -inf as silence.
- **Open:** the laws (Width, Mid/Side, Balance, Mono) and the stage order (Measurement plan).
- **`.als` element:** `StereoGain` [dump 12.4.6] (read from saved sets [source: 3]). Parameter elements [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `PhaseInvertL`, `PhaseInvertR`, `ChannelMode` (index, 1 = Stereo), `StereoWidth` (fraction, 1 = 100 %), `MidSideBalance` (1), `Mono`, `BassMono`, `BassMonoFrequency` (Hz, 120), `Balance` (0), `Gain` (linear gain, 1 = 0 dB), `LegacyGain` (0), `Mute`, `DcFilter`. The 12.4.6 listing has no `MidSideBalanceOn`, `BassMonoAudition` or `LegacyMode`, which earlier sets from the corpus contain [source: 3]; `LegacyGain` and `LegacyMode` are unused (unverified meaning) [source: 3].
