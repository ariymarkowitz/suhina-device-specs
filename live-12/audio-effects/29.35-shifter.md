# Shifter (manual 29.35)

**Family:** modulation and pitch
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Shifter changes the pitch or frequency of its input in real time in one of three ways: Pitch (true
pitch shifting by semitones and cents), Freq (frequency shifting by a number of Hz, which breaks
the harmonic relationships) and Ring (ring modulation, where a frequency in Hz is added to and
subtracted from every partial) [manual]. Around that sit a delay with feedback, an LFO and an
envelope follower that modulate the shift, stereo spread controls, a drive stage for Ring mode,
and a MIDI mode that sets the shift from incoming notes [manual]. It replaced Frequency Shifter
in Live 11.1 [source: 2].

## Signal flow

```text
                 ┌─ Pitch: pitch shifter (Window)  ─┐
input ─► shifter ┤  Freq:  frequency shifter        ├─► (Drive, Ring only) ─► Delay? ─► Dry/Wet ─► out
   amount = Coarse + Fine (L +, R − if Wide: the Fine knob is then Spread) + LFO·Amount + env·Amount + MIDI note
                 └─ Ring:  ring modulator ──────────┘          ▲
                                       Delay: time (Hz or sync) ─ feedback ─ Tone (low-pass) ─┘
dry ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- The shift amount is a sum of controls; its unit depends on the mode (semitones in Pitch, Hz
  in Freq and Ring) [manual].
- The manual's tip (high shifts give metallic echoes, low shifts drawn-out ones) says the shift is
  applied again on every repeat, so the shifter sits inside the delay's feedback loop [manual],
  [inferred]. Tone low-passes the feedback path [manual].
- Whether Drive is before or after the ring modulator is [measure]. The dry sound is not delayed: with the delay on and
  Dry/Wet at 50 %, the dry signal sits at 0 ms and only the wet signal repeats [measured 12.4.6]
  (Measured › Delay). The delay is in the wet path.
- Max wrappers exist for the pieces: `abl.dsp.pitchshifter~` (window 10 – 350 ms, ±24 semitones,
  wide) and `abl.dsp.ringmod~` (frequency 1 – 10000 Hz, drive 0 – 24 dB) [source: 3], [source: 4].

## Behaviour

**Pitch mode.** Real pitch shifting by a ratio 2^(semitones/12): the harmonic series is scaled
and stays harmonic [manual]. The Window setting recommends longer windows for low sources and
shorter for high, which is the classic delay-line/granular pitch shifter: two or more overlapping
read-heads with crossfaded windows in a ring buffer, window length 10 – 350 ms in the Max wrapper
[source: 3], [inferred]. The latency is half a window: 40.00 ms at Window 80 ms, 0 semitones [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Pitch mode); the device
reports it or not is [measure]. The ranges: coarse ±24 semitones in the wrapper [source: 3]; the
device's range is ±24 [dump 12.4.6], and +24 and -24 were both rendered [measured 12.4.6].
Pitch accuracy is better than 0.3 cent at the six shifts tested, with sidebands about 22 dB down and the tone 2.5 dB
louder than the input (Measured › Pitch mode). The pitch-shift wrapper has a `wide` switch that matches the Wide
button [source: 3].

**Freq mode.** A single-sideband frequency shifter: every partial moves up or down by the same
number of Hz, so harmonic sounds become inharmonic and small shifts give phasing or slow
beating against the dry [manual]. Measured: one output line at the input plus the shift, with the image
about 70 dB down; it is accurate to the stored value (Measured › Freq mode). Implementation: Hilbert transform to analytic signal,
multiplication by a complex oscillator, real part [inferred]. Sideband direction convention:
positive shift moves up [manual, "up or down"], [inferred].

**Ring mode.** A classic ring modulator: the input multiplied by a sine at the set Hz; output
contains sum and difference frequencies [manual]. At rates below about 20 Hz the effect is a
tremolo, and with Wide and small Spread a stereo tremolo [manual 29.35.6.3]. The ring
modulator has 1 – 10000 Hz in the wrapper [source: 4]; device range [measure].

**Drive.** A distortion in Ring mode only, 0 – 24 dB in the wrapper [source: 4]. Probably a
soft clipper or waveshaper before or after the modulation [inferred]; type [measure].

**Spread and Wide.** With Wide on, the right channel gets the opposite sign of Spread: raising
Spread shifts the left up and the right down [manual]. Spread is the Fine knob while Wide is
on, with Fine's range and unit in each mode (cents in Pitch, Hz in Freq and Ring); the left
channel is then shifted by Coarse + Fine and the right by Coarse − Fine, exactly [measured 12.4.7,
Measured › Fifth set]. With Wide off Fine is the same for both channels and gives no stereo
effect [measured 12.4.7].

**LFO.** Per channel LFO with shapes, Duty Cycle, stereo Phase/Spin/Width, Offset in synced
mode; modulation depth is Amount; the LFO adds to the shift in semitones (Pitch) or Hz (Freq,
Ring) [manual]. Shapes Triangle Analog, Triangle 8 and 16 are described in Phaser-Flanger
([29.29](29.29-phaser-flanger.md)); Random is a smooth random, Random S&H a stepped one
[inferred]. For Random and Random S&H the stereo control is Phase (Random) and Width
(Random S&H) [manual]. Spin's scale (wrapper: right frequency = left × (1 + spin)) [source: 5]
is [measure] for this device.

**Envelope follower.** Follows the input level with attack and release; its output times Amount
is added to the shift; sign convention (louder = up?) [measure].

**Delay.** The shifted output enters a delay of the set time with feedback; Tone low-passes the
feedback path. The time is a time (the dump shows ms and s), not a frequency; the earlier guess of
"Hz, so 1 Hz = 1000 ms" is wrong [dump 12.4.6, measured 12.4.6]; or in beat divisions
[manual]. A 12.4 fix removed a bug that cut off delayed audio [source: 2], so very long
delays or feedback tails must survive a mode change [inferred].

**MIDI mode.** The incoming note replaces Coarse (and Fine adds on top?) [manual], [measure]:
which note is the zero shift (probably middle C or C3 [measure]); how Fine, LFO and envelope
combine; velocity use; polyphony (probably last-note monophonic, with Glide) [inferred]; note
off behaviour (shift returns to 0, or holds) [measure]. PB range 0 – 24 semitones [manual].

**Dry/Wet.** [manual]; with the delay on and Pitch 0 the 50 % mix is equal-power: dry and wet each at -3.01 dB, so the law is
not linear [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Delay). Other modes not read.

## Edge cases

- Coarse and Fine both 0 in Pitch mode: output equals the input delayed by the shifter's
  latency (40.00 ms at Window 80 ms, unity gain) [measured 12.4.6]; in a mix with the dry the two comb unless latency is
  compensated [inferred]; whether Live compensates is [measure]. With the Delay on at 0 semitones the
  echoes are at the delay time exactly (plus one sample) with no extra 40 ms [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Delay).
- Freq mode with small shift (≤ 2 Hz) and 50 % mix gives phasing; the strongest effect is at
  50 % [manual tips].
- Ring at 0 Hz: the wrapper's minimum is 1 Hz [source: 4]; what the device does below it
  [measure].
- Wide on with Spread (Fine) 0: no effect [manual], [measured 12.4.7].
- Feedback high: Delay Feedback near 100 % with Pitch shifts above 0 climbs forever; limited
  or not [measure].
- Window changes during playback: reallocation or crossfade; clicks [measure].
- Sync with transport stopped: LFO and Delay free or frozen [measure].
- Mono input: Spread and Wide create stereo [manual].
- Extreme shifts (±24 semitones or more): quality and aliasing; band limiting of the shifted
  output to Nyquist is needed [inferred].
- Latency: Pitch mode adds latency (window based: 40 ms at Window 80 ms [measured 12.4.6]); Freq mode about 4 samples
  (0.09 ms) with a flat response [measured 12.4.6]; Ring not read; Freq and Ring probably only the Hilbert
  filter's [inferred], [measure].
- MIDI mode with no notes: shift stays at the last note, or returns to 0 [measure].

## Measured

- The output frequency is exactly the input times 2^(st/12) (within 0.3 cent); Coarse is whole semitones [measured 12.4.6].
- Every shifted tone is 2.54 dB louder than the input (-9.46 dB from -12.00 dB) in all six cases, so the shifter has a gain of

- Sidebands sit at the main line plus and minus k x 25 Hz x |2^(st/12) - 1| (k = 1: 22.4 dB down, k = 2: 34.4 dB down): the
  modulation of the two read heads, whose rate scales with the shift. 25 Hz is 2 / 0.08 s, so the
  spacing would be 2 |ratio - 1| / Window [inferred from a single Window value, measured 12.4.6].
- Latency at 0 st: the impulse comes out as one sample at 40.00 ms, unity gain: half the Window
  [measured 12.4.6; the half is [inferred], one Window tested].
- With a shift the impulse response depends on where the impulse falls in the read-head cycle, so it is not a simple delay: -12 st gives one
  echo at 20.0 ms (-3.4 dB), -24 st one at 60.1 ms (-3.6 dB), -5 st two (7.3 ms at -10.8 dB and 60.7 ms at -6.1 dB), +7 st three
  (20.6, 47.3, 74.0 ms at -12.2, -4.8 and -11.6 dB), +12 st four 20 ms apart (10.0 to 70.0 ms, -43 to -26 dB), and +24 st nothing above -240 dB [measured 12.4.6].
- Left and right are identical (no Spread) [measured 12.4.6].

- **Phase sets the right LFO's angle against the left's:** right minus left is the Phase value (180 by default, 90 gives +90 degrees, 0 the same LFO), in synced mode as in free [measured 12.4.7]. The fitted 173 Hz amplitude of the synced LFO is the 3 st of a sine seen in Hz around 1 kHz (+189 and -159 Hz, mean 1007.5 Hz) [measured 12.4.7].

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** an LFO, one-pole smoothing (Tone low-pass, glide), a static curve with
  oversampling for Drive, a biquad low-pass for Tone, a Hilbert pair (a 90° all-pass network or an
  FIR) for frequency shifting.
- **Pitch mode engine:** a spectral shifter such as Signalsmith Stretch (MIT) can transpose by a
  ratio with a known latency, but it has no window setting in ms, and its sound and latency differ
  from a delay-line shifter; Live's Window slider suggests a time-domain one [inferred]. A
  classic two-head delay-line shifter is a small block of its own (a ring buffer and crossfade
  windows).
- **Missing:** Hilbert transform (frequency shifter), complex oscillator, ring modulator, ring
  buffer delay with feedback and Tone, the Spread/Wide stereo stage, an envelope follower with
  attack and release, the extra LFO shapes (Triangle Analog, 8-step, 16-step, Random S&H, duty
  warp), MIDI input to a device (a sidechain of notes: see the Auto Shift note, which needs the
  same).
- **Import:** the `.als` element is `Shifter` [dump 12.4.6]; old Frequency Shifter sets (Live 10 and earlier) used a different device [source: 2]. Parameter elements [als 12.4.6] (stored units where they differ from the display): `On`, `Lfo_Amount` (Hz, 0), `Lfo_AmountPitch` (st, 0), `Lfo_Waveform` (index, 1 = Triangle), `Lfo_SyncOn`, `Lfo_RateHz` (Hz, 0.5), `Lfo_SyncedRate` (index, 12), `Lfo_SpinOn`, `Lfo_PhaseOffset` (degrees, 180), `Lfo_SpinAmount`, `Lfo_Offset` (degrees), `Lfo_SHWidth` (Width), `Lfo_DutyCycle`, `Pitch_Coarse`, `Pitch_Fine`, `Pitch_WindowSize` (seconds, 0.08), `ModBasedShifting_Fine`, `ModBasedShifting_FShift_Coarse`, `ModBasedShifting_RingMod_Coarse` (Hz, 1000), `ModBasedShifting_RingMod_Drive`, `ModBasedShifting_RingMod_DriveAmount`, `MidiPitch_Glide`, `EnvelopeFollower_On`, `EnvelopeFollower_Attack` and `_Release` (seconds, 0.006 and 0.2), `EnvelopeFollower_AmountHz`, `EnvelopeFollower_AmountPitch`, `Delay_On`, `Delay_SyncOn`, `Delay_TimeSeconds` (seconds, 0.25), `Delay_SyncedTime` (index, 9), `Delay_Feedback`, `Global_ShifterMode` (index, 0 = Pitch), `Global_Tone` (Hz, 22000), `Global_Wide`, `Global_DryWet` (1 = 100 %). Not API parameters but stored as plain values [als 12.4.6]: `Global_PitchMode` (0 = Internal [inferred]), `MidiPitch_PitchBendRange` (semitones, 2), and the MIDI source as `MidiSideChain` (a routing, target `MidiIn/None` by default). No element for Spread was found.
