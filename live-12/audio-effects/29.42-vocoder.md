# Vocoder (manual 29.42)

**Family:** modulation and pitch
**Note status:** dump checked

## Summary

The Vocoder analyses the level of a modulator signal (the track it sits on, usually speech or drums)
in a bank of band-pass filters and uses each band's level to set the level of the matching band of a
carrier signal (a synthesizer, noise, the modulator itself, or an oscillator that follows the
modulator's pitch) [manual]. The carrier is chosen from four types, an extra noise generator
re-creates unpitched sounds like "s" and "f", and a formant control shifts the carrier's filter bank
[manual]. Not available in the Intro and Lite editions [manual].

## Signal flow

```text
modulator (the track's input) ─► analysis filter bank (Bands, Range, BW, Precise|Retro)
      ─► per-band envelope followers (Attack, Release) ─► Depth ─► Gate ─► band gains ─┐
                                                                                        ▼
carrier (Noise | External | Modulator | Pitch Tracking) ─► (Enhance) ─► synthesis filter bank
      (frequencies shifted by Formant) ─► × band gains ─► sum ─► Level ─► Dry/Wet ─► out
unvoiced detector (Sens., Fast|Slow) ─► noise generator ─► Unvoiced level ─► sum ─────────────┘
dry = the modulator track's own signal ────────────────────────────────────► Dry/Wet
```

- Insert the device on the modulator's track; External carriers come from two Audio From choosers
  (source track and tap point), like a side-chain [manual]; see [`common.md`](common.md) for how
  tap points are named there. Unlike Compressor's side-chain, here the external signal is the
  carrier and is heard [manual].
- Order of Enhance, Level, Unvoiced, Dry/Wet and where Formant sits (shifting the synthesis bank
  or the analysis bank) are [measure]; the manual says the carrier's filter bank frequencies
  are the ones shifted [manual].
- No Max DSP wrapper for the vocoder is published (abl.dsp.vocoder~ does not exist) [source: 6].

## Behaviour

**Analysis and synthesis filter banks.** N band-pass filters (4 – 40) spaced between the two
Range limits, probably logarithmically (equal ratio) [inferred], the same set for analysis
(modulator) and synthesis (carrier), except for the Formant shift, which moves the carrier's
bank [manual]. Likely structure: second-order band-pass biquads, one set for the modulator and
one (per channel) for the carrier, perhaps with higher-order cascades for steeper skirts [inferred].
BW: low values give narrow, near-sinusoidal bands, up to 100 % where neighbours just meet
("most accurate"), higher values overlap more [manual]. Mapping of BW to Q: [measure].

**Precise and Retro.** Precise: bands equal in gain and relative bandwidth, so the bank is flat
[manual], [inferred]. Retro: modelled on analogue vocoders, where the filter bandwidth grows less
than the centre frequency, so higher bands are narrower (relative to their frequency) and, by the
manual, louder [manual]. A fixed-Hz-bandwidth bank (constant absolute bandwidth) has this
property [inferred]; the gain tilt (dB per octave) is [measure].

**Envelope followers.** One per analysis band: rectify (or square) then smooth with separate
Attack and Release; the output is the band's envelope [manual], [inferred]. Detector type (peak,
RMS, average of rectified) is [measure].

**Depth.** The envelope used on the carrier band is a function of the detected level and Depth:
0 % constant (the envelope is discarded), 100 % linear (classic), 200 % expansion so that only
high peaks pass [manual]. The curve is probably a power law gain = env^(Depth/100) on a
normalised envelope, or an expander around a threshold [inferred]; [measure].

**Gate.** Each band's envelope below the threshold produces silence in that band [manual]. Hard or
soft knee, and whether the gate has hysteresis or release, are [measure].

**Carrier types.**
- *Noise:* an internal noise generator. X: sample-rate reduction (a sample-and-hold or decimation
  of the noise, giving a band-limited, lower-frequency noise); Y: density (a sparser noise; at
  low densities probably random impulses, a crackle-like noise) [manual], [inferred]. Noise type
  (white) and the scales of both axes are [measure].
- *External:* the carrier comes from the routing choosers (Audio From), for example a synthesizer
  track [manual]. Latency alignment between the carrier and modulator and the tap points are as
  for the side-chain in [`common.md`](common.md); the manual recommends Post FX for the carrier.
- *Modulator:* the modulator is also the carrier: a resynthesis of itself; with Depth 100 %, Enhance
  on and the Formant control it works as a formant shifter [manual 29.42.1.2].
- *Pitch Tracking:* a monophonic oscillator (saw or one of three pulse waves) follows the
  modulator's pitch. It updates only when a clear pitch is detected and keeps its last pitch
  otherwise. High and Low limit the tracking range; Pitch tunes it [manual]. A pitch
  detector (autocorrelation-type) is needed [inferred]; Ableton ships a "frequency detector"
  block in its Max DSP package [source: 5]. Resetting (such as grouping the track) can
  change the sound, since the oscillator has state [manual].

**Enhance.** Evens out the carrier's spectrum and dynamics [manual]: the carrier's band levels are
flattened (an automatic gain per band, so that the carrier has a constant level in every band
regardless of its own spectrum) [manual], [inferred]. Probably an AGC with a slow time constant
per band, or a division by the carrier's per-band envelope [inferred]; time constant [measure].

**Unvoiced.** A noise generator (probably high-passed, as sibilants are high-frequency) whose
level is the Unvoiced knob times a detection signal; the detector decides whether the modulator
is pitchless at that moment, for example from high-to-low band energy ratio or zero-crossing rate
[manual], [inferred]. Sens. at 100 % forces the noise on; 0 % turns the detection output off.
Fast and Slow set the speed of the detector's switching, probably its smoothing time [manual],
[inferred]. A beta tester wrote that it lets plosives and "ess" sounds through [source: 2].

**Stereo modes.** Mono: both inputs summed to mono and output mono (or dual-mono) [manual],
[inferred]. Stereo: one analysis from the mono modulator, two synthesis banks for the carrier's
left and right. L/R: two complete channels [manual].

**Formant.** The carrier's band centre frequencies are multiplied by a ratio (shift in
semitones or by a unit [measure]) while the analysis bank stays; the envelopes of the original
bands thus modulate bands at shifted frequencies, which moves the spectral envelope of the
result [manual], [inferred]. At the range ends the shifted bank may leave the Range; behaviour
(clipping at the Range limit, or dropping bands) is [measure].

**Level and Dry/Wet.** Level scales the vocoded output (and the Unvoiced noise?) [manual],
[measure]. Dry/Wet mixes the unprocessed modulator track signal [manual]; mix law [measure].

**Band display.** Shows each band's level, as bars; clicking in it attenuates individual bands,
which are weighted in the carrier gain [manual].

## Edge cases

- Bands 4: a few broad bands, robotic or "vowel-like"; 40: finer detail; CPU grows with bands
  [manual], [source: 4].
- BW 0: each filter approaches a sine line; very quiet output [manual], [inferred]. BW above 100 %:
  overlapping bands, brighter and louder [inferred].
- Depth 0 %: constant carrier gain; Depth 200 %: only strong peaks pass [manual].
- Gate high: only the loudest bands sound [manual].
- Range collapsed (low = high): [measure] (single band?); Range limits crossing.
- Pitch Tracking with unpitched or polyphonic material: unpredictable [manual]; the oscillator
  holds the last pitch through silence [manual].
- External with no source chosen: silent or the modulator [measure].
- Carrier = External: the carrier track must be running; a carrier track's own audio is also heard
  there unless muted (the manual solos the voice track) [manual 29.42.1.1].
- Mono modulator with Stereo mode: single analysis [manual].
- Attack and Release at the minimum: distortion artifacts at fast times [manual].
- Formant beyond the Range limit: [measure].
- Latency: analysis and synthesis filters (IIR): none beyond filter group delay, if the
  external carrier is aligned [inferred], [measure]; Pitch Tracking adds the pitch detector's
  delay [inferred].
- Denormals: IIR bands and envelope followers decay to subnormal numbers on silence [inferred].
- Sample rates: band count above 40 not offered; range limit at Nyquist.

## Measurement plan

1. **Ranges and defaults:** settled by the dump [dump 12.4.6] for the 21 parameters it lists. The Carrier, Audio From and band-level defaults come from plain values in the saved set [als 12.4.6]. Still to read: the Bands chooser's entries and default (stored 4, meaning unclear), and the units behind Formant and Pitch (semitones?).
2. **Band layout:** the modulator and the carrier are both white noise (Carrier = External with a
   separate noise track) or use Carrier = Modulator; Depth 0 % and Enhance off: the output spectrum
   reveals the synthesis bank: centre frequencies and gain per band for Bands = 4, 8, 20, 40, for
   several Range settings, Precise and Retro. Measure bandwidth (−3 dB) per band, and the flatness
   across bands: gain tilt in Retro.
3. **BW:** same, BW at 0, 25, 50, 100, 150 %; read the Q.
4. **Envelope following:** a tone burst train as modulator, a constant carrier (a saw track) as
   external carrier; Depth 100 %: the carrier's output envelope follows the burst; measure
   attack/release times at several knob settings (10 – 90 %).
5. **Depth curve:** the modulator with a stepped level (−40 to 0 dB in 6 dB steps), steady carrier:
   output level against input level at Depth 0, 50, 100, 150, 200 %; fit.
6. **Gate:** steps again with the Gate at several thresholds: per-band silencing; knee and
   hysteresis.
7. **Noise carrier:** X-Y pad corners and centre, Depth 0 %: spectrum and the sample-rate
   reduction (alias images); density: time-domain look at the noise; the pad's scales.
8. **Pitch Tracking:** a sine sweep or a sung note as modulator: oscillator frequency vs. input
   frequency; the High and Low limits; hold behaviour on silence; the pulse widths of the three
   pulse waves; Pitch's unit; latency of the tracking; behaviour with chords and drums.
9. **Enhance:** a dull carrier (low-pass filtered saw) and a bright one: compare the output
   spectra at Enhance on and off; measure the AGC time constant with a carrier level step.
10. **Unvoiced and Sens.:** a spoken sentence with fricatives; the noise level against
    Unvoiced, Sens. at 0, 50, 100 %, Fast and Slow: the detector's switching time; whether the
    noise is high-passed (its spectrum).
11. **Stereo modes:** a hard-panned modulator and a stereo carrier in Mono, Stereo, L/R: what
    arrives at each output channel.
12. **Formant:** ±12 and the extremes at BW 100 %: the shift of the synthesis bank in semitones;
    behaviour at the Range edges.
13. **Dry/Wet and Level:** mix law; Level dB range; whether Level changes the Unvoiced noise.
14. **Latency and CPU:** impulse through a Carrier = Modulator setting; CPU against Bands.

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** biquad band-passes (0 dB peak, the Q from a bandwidth in octaves) for both
  banks, one-pole smoothing, dB conversions; no crossovers are needed.
- **Also needed:** an envelope follower with separate attack and release (an asymmetric
  one-pole); a noise generator and a sample-rate reducer (Noise carrier with X and Y); a sawtooth
  and pulse oscillator with band-limiting (PolyBLEP or wavetable) and a monophonic pitch detector
  (Pitch Tracking); the voiced/unvoiced detector; an input for an external carrier (an audio
  side-chain that must be audible, [common.md](common.md) › Side-chain section); a per-band gain
  display.
- **Cost:** 40 bands × (analysis + synthesis) × 2 channels × biquad (possibly cascaded) is well
  within a block budget; per-sample envelope follower per band.
- **Hard to match:** Retro's gain and bandwidth law, the Depth curve, Enhance, Unvoiced
  detection, Formant's exact units.
- **Import:** `.als` element `Vocoder` [dump 12.4.6]; carrier routing (Audio From) uses the same routing fields as other side-chain devices. Parameter elements [als 12.4.6] (stored units where they differ from the display): `On`, `LowFrequency` (Hz, 80), `HighFrequency` (Hz, 12000), `FormantShift` (0), `FilterBandWidth` (1 = 100 %), `Retro` (false), `LevelGate` (dB, −60 = the −inf end), `OutputGain` (dB, 0), `EnvelopeRate` (Attack, ms, 1), `EnvelopeRelease` (ms, 150), `UvdThreshold` (Sens., 0.5), `UvdSlow` (false), `UvdLevel` (Unvoiced, linear gain, 0.000316), `CarrierSource/NoiseRate` (Hz, 20000), `CarrierSource/NoiseCrackle` (1 = 100 %), `CarrierSource/DetectionLowerBound` and `DetectionUpperBound` (Hz, 80 and 500), `CarrierSource/OscillatorPitch` (0), `CarrierSource/OscillatorWaveform` (0 = Saw), `CarrierFlatten` (Enhance), `MonoStereo` (1 = Stereo), `DryWet` (1), `ModulatorAmount` (Depth, 1 = 100 %).
