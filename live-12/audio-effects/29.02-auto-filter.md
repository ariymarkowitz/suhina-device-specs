# Auto Filter (manual 29.2)

**Family:** EQ and filter
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

A multimode filter with two modulators: an LFO with separate left and right channels, and an
envelope follower that listens to the filter's own input or to an external side-chain. The
12.2 redesign (June 2025) has ten filter types, four analogue-style circuits, a drive stage before
the filter, a soft clipper and output level after it, a dry/wet control, and a real-time display
that draws the filter curves for left and right and the output spectrum [manual] [source: 3].
This note describes the 12.2 and later device; the earlier device (Live 9.5 to 12.1) is
summarised at the end of Behaviour.

## Signal flow

Shared parts: [common.md](common.md) (side-chain section, title bar, tempo sync, dry/wet). The
side-chain here only feeds the envelope follower; departures are listed under Side-chain.

```text
input ─► Drive ─► filter (type, slope, circuit; Freq/Res/Morph) ─► Clip (soft clip) ─► Output ─► Dry/Wet ─► out
 │       [manual]  [manual]                                        [manual]            [manual]  [manual]
 │                     ▲ cutoff modulation: LFO (left and right separately) + envelope follower
 │                     │   [manual]
 ├─► (internal side-chain) ─► [SC filter] ─┐
 └─(dry)───────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────► Dry/Wet
 external side-chain ─► SC Gain ─► [SC filter] ─► Mix (internal vs external) ─► envelope follower
                                                                               [manual]
```

- Order Drive → filter is [manual] ("drive applied to the signal before the filter"). Clip and
  Output being after the filter, and Dry/Wet being last, is [inferred] from the manual's order
  ("Clip ... on the effect's output", "Output ... at the device's output").
- Whether the dry signal is taken before Drive (the device input) is [inferred].
- The LFO modulates left and right separately [manual]; the envelope follower value is shared
  unless the side-chain is stereo (Mono Sidechain is on by default, so it is shared) [manual].
- The envelope follower's input is the device's own input by default [manual]. The manual's
  introduction says it can respond to "the filtered signal itself", so whether it hears the
  signal before Drive and the filter or after the filter is [measure].

## Behaviour

**Filter structure.** The circuits are described by the manual as modelled on classic analogue
designs: SVF is a clean state-variable filter that distorts only when Drive is above zero;
DFM feeds more of its distortion back in; MS2 is a Sallen-Key design with soft clipping limiting
the resonance; PRD is a ladder design with no explicit resonance limiting [manual].
Most likely implementation: a zero-delay-feedback (topology-preserving) state-variable filter for
SVF, with a `tanh`-type non-linearity inside the loop for DFM, a Sallen-Key 2-pole with a clipper
in the feedback path for MS2, and a 4-pole transistor-ladder with feedback for PRD [inferred].
Circuits do nothing for the other five types [manual].

**Slopes.** 24 dB/octave is two cascaded 12 dB sections [inferred]; 6 and 48 dB for Morph are one
first-order and four second-order sections [inferred]. Whether the Q of a cascade is shared or
adjusted per stage is [measure]. Measured for SVF: at Res 0 the 24 dB low-pass is two
critically damped sections (exactly -12.04 dB at Freq, -6.02 dB per section with Q 0.5), but at Res
30 % the 24 dB slope peaks less than twice the 12 dB slope's peak, so the sections do not share one
Q (Measured › SVF responses) [measured 12.4.6].

**Type details** [manual unless tagged]:

- Low-pass, High-pass, Band-pass, Notch: standard responses with Res at the corner or centre.
- Morph: continuous low-pass → band-pass → high-pass over Morph 0 – 100 % (low values low-pass,
  middle values band-pass, high values high-pass). The shape may be an SVF with mixed outputs
  [inferred]; the older device's Morph also passed through a notch ([source: 5]: it swept low-pass,
  band-pass, high-pass, notch, and back), which the 12.2 manual no longer says [measure].
- DJ: one bipolar control, a low-pass on the negative side and a high-pass on the positive side,
  with resonance coupled to the position (small boost at 0 growing towards the ends). Freq and Res
  are not shown. A single-pole-pair filter with the cutoff derived from Control [inferred].
- Comb: a comb filter whose spacing follows Freq (peaks at multiples of the cutoff). Morph
  moves between peaks (low values) and notches (high values) at those multiples [manual], most
  likely by changing the sign of the feedback or feed-forward path [inferred]; Res sets the
  feedback amount [inferred].
- Resampling: lowers the high end by resampling at a rate set by Freq, producing aliasing; Res
  is disabled [manual]. A sample-and-hold or zero-order resampler without an anti-alias filter
  [inferred]; whether it interpolates is [measure]. Reviewers hear it as closer to a bit-crusher than a filter [source: 7].
- Notch + LP: a notch plus a low-pass at Freq; Morph moves the notch relative to the cutoff,
  far away at 0 % and exactly on it at 100 % [manual].
- Vowel: a formant filter that reshapes the spectrum towards the vowels a, e, i, o, u: Formant
  moves along them, Pitch shifts all formants in semitones, and Morph increasingly equalises the
  gains of the formants [manual]. Likely a bank of parallel band-passes with tabulated vowel
  formants [inferred]; the formant table is [measure].

**Modulation.**

- Cutoff = Freq modulated by the LFO and envelope, most likely added in the logarithmic (octave)
  domain [inferred]. For the envelope this is measured: the cutoff moves in octaves in proportion to
the input's linear level, about 7.7 octaves per unit of peak level at ±100 % [measured 12.4.6]
(Measured › Envelope). For the LFO, how many octaves ±100 % corresponds to is [measure].
- LFO: two LFOs, left and right. Phase mode: same rate, right offset by Phase degrees; Spin mode:
  the rates differ by Spin percent (left slower, right faster, or a split about the rate)
  [inferred]. Phase Offset moves the start only in synced modes, because free-running LFOs have
  no defined start [manual]. Steps divides each cycle into N equal steps; S&H samples the shape at a
  beat-time rate; Smooth smooths the S&H wave only [manual].
- Waveforms: Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square, Ramp Up, Ramp Down, S&H (random positive and negative
  values), and Wander (a smooth random drift) [manual]. Morph reshapes the wave [manual]; the
  wrapper's extra shapes (Analog Triangle, Triangle 8-Step, 16-Step, Sharktooth) suggest what Morph
  may change between [inferred] [source: 2].
- Envelope follower: an amplitude detector with Attack and Release; at 12.2 the attack always
  completes when Hold is on [manual]. Release is close to a one-pole fall with the set time as its
  time constant, and the output scale is about 7.7 octaves of cutoff per unit of peak level at 100 %
  [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Envelope). Peak or RMS detection is [measure]. S&H on the envelope samples it at beat-time
  steps [manual].
- Side-chain: the follower can listen to an external source; Mix blends the internal and external
  signals before it; SC Gain scales the external one; the SC filter shapes whatever the follower
  hears [manual].

**Drive, Clip, Output, Dry/Wet.** Drive 0 % leaves the sound unaltered, then increases distortion
and saturation [manual]. Clip is a soft clipper (`tanh`-like) on the output meant to tame peaks
from high resonance; the LED flashes while it clips [manual]; the 12.2 notes say it keeps the
output from going above 0 dB [source: 3] ("soft clipping so output doesn't exceed 0 dB" as
reported in the release notes), which points to a ceiling at 0 dBFS [inferred]. Whether Dry/Wet
is a linear crossfade and whether the dry path is delayed to match the wet path is [measure]
(see [common.md](common.md)).

**Display.** Shows the filter curve for the selected type, separate modulated curves for left and
right, and the real-time spectrum of the output [manual].

**Earlier versions.** Before 12.2 (Live 9.5 to 12.1) the device had five types (Low-pass,
High-pass, Band-pass, Notch, Morph), each at 12 or 24 dB, five circuits (Clean, OSR, MS2, SMP,
PRD) where MS2, SMP and PRD applied to low-pass and high-pass only, Drive for non-Clean circuits
on low-pass, high-pass and band-pass, and resonance over 100 % self-oscillating on non-Clean
circuits [source: 5]. LFO shapes were Sine, Square, Triangle, Saw Up, Saw Down, and two S&H
(stereo and mono); the LFO offered Amount, Rate in Hz or beat, Phase, Spin, and a "Quantize Beat"
control (steps on a 16th grid) [source: 5]. The 12.2 device dropped Clean and SMP, added SVF and
DFM, and added DJ, Comb, Resampling, Notch + LP and Vowel, Morph with four slopes, the Wander and
Ramp shapes, S&H smoothing, LFO Morph, envelope hold, a side-chain EQ, Clip, Output, Dry/Wet and
the Mono Sidechain option [source: 3] [source: 4]. Sets saved before 12.2 are loaded with a
legacy device (Core Library: Devices/Audio Effects/Legacy/Auto Filter Legacy) [source: 3].

## Edge cases

- **Resonance at the maximum:** SVF does not self-oscillate at Res 100 %: at 440 Hz an impulse rings
  at 440 Hz and dies away at 335 dB/s (T60 0.18 s) [measured 12.4.6]. DFM, MS2 and PRD at 100 % are
  still [measure].
- **Drive 0 % with MS2, PRD or DFM:** the circuit's own non-linearity does colour the sound: at Res
  60 % the resonance peak falls by 6 (DFM), 12 (MS2) and 14 dB (PRD) from a -60 to a 0 dBFS tone, with
  0.5 to 3.5 % THD; SVF stays linear [measured 12.4.6] (Measured › Circuits).
- **Phase = 0° or Spin = 0 %:** left and right are identical [manual].
- **Wander or S&H waveform:** Spin is greyed out and ignored [manual]. Morph is replaced by Smooth for
  S&H; Morph stopped affecting Wander in 12.4.3 [source: 3].
- **Resampling filter:** Res greyed out [manual].
- **DJ filter:** Freq and Res not available; Control 0 is not a bypass (small boost) [manual].
- **Vowel filter:** Freq and Res are replaced by Pitch and Formant [manual].
- **Synced Phase Offset:** applies only in synced modes; the LFO step quantiser follows Phase and
  Phase Offset changes during playback since 12.2.5 [manual] [source: 3].
- **Transport stopped:** whether the synced LFO runs from the song position or free-runs when stopped
  is [measure].
- **External side-chain with nothing routed:** envelope sees silence [inferred].
- **Mono input:** left and right LFOs still move the cutoffs identically only if Phase/Spin is zero;
  with a mono input the output of a modulated filter is still stereo (the two filters differ)
  [inferred].
- **Sample rate:** cutoff above Nyquist/2 [measure].

### Envelope (Low-pass 24 dB, SVF, Freq 400 Hz, Res 0)

Read through the 1 kHz tone: with Res 0 the 24 dB low-pass is (1 + (f/fc)²)⁻², so the tone's gain gives
the cutoff fc at each moment (at Env 0 the gain is -34.46 dB, the formula's -34.4 dB).

- **Amount.** The cutoff's shift in octaves doubles for every 6 dB of input level, so it is proportional
  to the linear level: +0.24, +0.49, +0.97, +1.95 and +3.96 octaves at -30, -24, -18, -12 and -6 dBFS
  for Env +100 %, about 7.7 octaves per unit of peak level; +50 % gives exactly half, and -50 % and
  -100 % the same shifts downwards [measured 12.4.6]. The cutoff stops at the ends of Freq's range: at
  -100 % and -3 or 0 dBFS it sits 4.1 to 4.2 octaves below 400 Hz (about 22 Hz) [measured 12.4.6].
- **Times.** Taking the envelope as the cutoff shift divided by 7.7, for the -30 to -6 to -30 dBFS
  bursts: Release 100 ms gives a fall with t63 94 to 98 ms and t90 210 to 225 ms (a one-pole fall
  with the set time as its time constant gives 100 and 230 ms); Release 1 s left 39 % of the step after
  1 s (a time constant of 1.05 s) [measured 12.4.6]. Attack 1 ms reads t63 1.5 to 2.5 ms (at the 1 ms
  resolution); Attack 50 ms reads t63 61 ms and t90 146 ms, and with it the settled shift for the
  -6 dBFS burst is 0.74 times that at Attack 1 ms, as a rectifier followed by a slow-attack,
  faster-release follower would give [measured 12.4.6; the detector structure inferred].
- **Hold** on changed the 1 ms / 100 ms case little (t63 1.5 ms up, 98 ms down) for a 1 s burst
  [measured 12.4.6]; its effect on short transients was not measured.

### LFO shapes and depth

- The impulse comes back as a hold of the input value for 89, 11 and 3 samples (sum of the response / 0.5), with rounded edges: at 500 Hz it rises over about 25 samples and overshoots by 4.6 %, at 4 kHz over 3 samples (8 %), at 16 kHz not at all, then a ringing at Nyquist that decays over about 200 samples. So a sample-and-hold at Freq followed by a smoothing filter that scales with Freq [measured 12.4.7; the structure inferred].
- The spectra carry lines 100 Hz apart around every image: 44100 / Freq is not a whole number (88.2, 11.025, 2.756 samples), and the pattern of whole-sample holds that averages to it repeats every 441 samples (10 ms) [measured 12.4.7; the cause inferred].
- **Images are as loud as the tone:** 3 kHz at Freq 4 kHz comes out at -22.5 dBFS with its image at 1 kHz at -13.0 dBFS; 10 kHz at Freq 4 kHz at -28.2 dBFS with an image at 2 kHz at -8.0 dBFS; 10 kHz at Freq 16 kHz at -12.7 dBFS with the image at 6 kHz at -9.4 dBFS [measured 12.4.7]. 3 kHz passes Freq 16 kHz at -0.5 dB.
- At 500 Hz both test tones are whole multiples of Freq and fold to near 0 Hz (only the 100 Hz lines are left, -12 to -50 dBFS) [measured 12.4.7].

**The LFO's upward travel and the envelope at ±100 %** (`autofilter-depth-up`) [measured 12.4.7]:

- With Freq 100 Hz and LFO Amount 50 %, the 1 kHz tone is open at the top of the cycle with the resonance bump (-11.1 to -11.9 dB RMS, +3 to +4 dB): the cutoff reaches about 1 to 1.4 kHz, 3.3 to 3.8 octaves above Freq, against 4.4 octaves below at the same Amount (remaining set). So the travel is close to symmetric on the log scale [measured 12.4.6; measured 12.4.7]. At 100 % the tone is open (-15 dB) for half the cycle, so the cutoff goes well above 1 kHz.
- **Env Amount -100 % mirrors +100 %:** at Freq 2 kHz, Res 70 %, a -6 dBFS burst pushes the cutoff down until the 1 kHz tone is 69 dB below the input, a cutoff of about 140 Hz (3.9 octaves down, as the 7.7 octaves per unit of peak level of the remaining set predict for a peak of 0.5). At +100 % the same burst opens the filter fully. When the burst ends the cutoff sweeps back through 1 kHz within about 150 ms and the resonance lifts the tone to +9 dB over the bed on the way [measured 12.4.7].

**LFO parameters** (Low-pass 24, Freq 1 kHz, Res 40 %, Sine at 1 Hz, Amount 100 %; `autofilter-lfo-params`) [measured 12.4.7]:

| Setting | What the tone's envelope shows |
|---|---|
| Phase 90° / 180° | the right channel runs 0.25 / 0.50 s ahead of the left (the phase as a fraction of the cycle) |
| Spin 25 % | left at 0.80 Hz, right at 1.25 Hz |
| Spin 50 % | left at 0.67 Hz, right at 1.50 Hz |
| Steps 4 / 16 | 4 / 16 held values per cycle (0.25 / 0.0625 s each), the level jumping between them |
| S&H 1/8 | identical to Steps 4 (an eighth at 120 BPM is a quarter of the 1 s cycle) |
| Smoothing 50 % | identical to the default render: with no Steps or S&H there is nothing to smooth |
| Time 0.5 s | 2.00 Hz |
| Synced, Rate 12 | 2.00 Hz (a quarter note at 120 BPM) |
| Triplet, Rate 12 | 3.0 Hz |
| Dotted, Rate 12 | 1.33 Hz |
| Sixteenth, 3 | 2.67 Hz (three sixteenths, 0.375 s) |
| Morph -100 % | closed for only 60 ms of each cycle; for 0.6 s the tone sits on the resonance bump (-11 to -12 dB), so the LFO lingers near Freq |
| Morph +100 % | open for 0.33 s, a sudden fall, closed for about 0.15 s, then a slow 0.4 s rise back |
| Wave S&H | a new value each second: open for 1 to 3 s at a time, closed at -52, -151 and -102 dB in the three closed stretches |

- **Spin divides the left rate and multiplies the right by (1 + Spin)**: 0.8 and 1.25 Hz at 25 %, 0.67 and 1.5 Hz at 50 % [measured 12.4.7].
- **Phase advances the right channel** by the phase angle [measured 12.4.7].

**Envelope detector, Hold and S&H** (Low-pass 24, Freq 400 Hz, Env 100 %, Attack 1 ms, Release 100 ms; `autofilter-env-detector`). Peak gain of the -6 dBFS 1 kHz bursts [measured 12.4.7]:

| Burst | 1 ms | 5 ms | 10 ms | 30 ms | 100 ms | 300 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hold off | -12.8 dB | -1.0 dB | -0.6 dB | -0.4 dB | -0.4 dB | -0.4 dB |
| Hold on | -8.4 dB | -0.4 dB | -0.4 dB | -0.4 dB | -0.4 dB | -0.4 dB |
| Env S&H 1/16 | -19.2 dB | -19.0 dB | -19.0 dB | -18.9 dB | -0.4 dB | -0.4 dB |

- A 5 ms burst opens the filter almost fully, a 1 ms burst only part of the way: a peak-like detector with the 1 ms Attack, not an RMS window of tens of milliseconds [measured 12.4.7; the detector type inferred].
- **Hold** lets short bursts open the filter further (1 ms: -8.4 against -12.8 dB) and slows the fall after them: 100 ms after a 1 ms burst on the -30 dBFS bed the output is 6 dB higher than without Hold [measured 12.4.7].
- **Env S&H 1/16** samples the envelope every 125 ms (a sixteenth at 120 BPM) and holds it: bursts of 30 ms or less fall between samples and pass at -19 dB, and after a burst the level stays flat for 125 ms, then steps [measured 12.4.7].

## Implementation notes

- **Building blocks:** biquad low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, peak and shelf filters (RBJ
  cookbook) cover the static 12 dB types and cascade for 24 dB; a tanh soft clip for Clip and
  Drive; oversampling around Drive and the non-linear circuits; an LFO with Sine, Triangle, Saw
  up and down, Square and Random; one-pole smoothing for parameters and envelope followers; a DC
  blocker.
- **Also needed:** a TPT (topology-preserving) state-variable filter for modulation without
  artefacts (cookbook biquads click when swept fast), a notch, the DFM, MS2 and PRD circuit
  structures, a comb filter with a fractional delay, a formant bank and its table, a resampling
  (sample-and-hold) filter, an envelope follower with attack/hold/release, LFO Wander and Ramp
  shapes plus Morph and Smooth, beat-synced LFO and S&H quantisers, a side-chain EQ, the
  stereo-offset LFO pair, the display (response curve plus spectrum).
- **Hard to match:** the vowel formant table, the self-oscillation and the exact circuit
  non-linearities, the Morph curve over slopes.
- **Side-chain:** this device needs the detector-only side-chain with Mix and SC Gain
  ([common.md](common.md) › Side-chain section).
- **`.als` element:** the 12.2 and later device is `AutoFilter2` [als 12.4.6]; the pre-12.2 device
  was `AutoFilter` (unverified); old sets map to the Legacy device [source: 3]. Parameter elements
  [als 12.4.6] (stored values are the display units unless noted): `On`, `Filter_Frequency` (Hz,
  stored 9999.99805 for 10 kHz), `Filter_Resonance`, `Filter_Morph`, `Filter_Type` (0 to 9),
  `Filter_Slope` (0 = 12 dB, 1 = 24 dB, stored 1), `Filter_MorphSlope` (0 to 3, stored 2),
  `Filter_Circuit` (0 to 3), `Filter_Drive`, `Filter_DjControl`, `Filter_VowelPitch` (st),
  `Filter_VowelFormant`, `Lfo_Amount`, `Lfo_Waveform`, `Lfo_TimeMode`, `Lfo_Frequency` (stored 1 = 1 Hz),
  `Lfo_Time` (stored 1 = 1 s), `Lfo_SyncedRate` (stored 4), `Lfo_Sixteenth` (stored 16),
  `Lfo_Phase` (degrees, stored 4), `Lfo_PhaseOffset`, `Lfo_StereoMode`, `Lfo_Spin`, `Lfo_Morph`,
  `Lfo_Smoothing`, `Lfo_QuantizationMode`, `Lfo_Steps`, `Lfo_SahRate` (stored -4), `Envelope_Amount`,
  `Envelope_Attack` (stored in seconds: 0.001 = 1 ms), `Envelope_HoldOn` (true), `Envelope_Release`
  (stored 0.25, i.e. seconds), `Envelope_SahOn`, `Envelope_SahRate` (-4), `Output` (linear gain,
  1 = 0 dB), `SoftClipOn`, `DryWet` (stored 1 = 100 %), `SideChainEq_On`, `SideChainEq_Mode` (stored 5
  = High pass), `SideChainEq_Freq` (Hz), `SideChainEq_Q`, `SideChainEq_Gain`, `SideChain/OnOff`,
  `SideChain/RoutedInput/Volume` (stored 1; presumably the SC Gain as linear gain, 1 = 0 dB, matching the
  API's 0.00 dB at internal 0.4), `SideChain/DryWet` (presumably the Mix). Not parameters but plain values
  [als 12.4.6]: `SideChainMono` (true), `SideChainListen` (false), `HiQuality` (false), `InternalSideChainGain`
  (1, a constant with no control [measure]) and the External Source as `SideChain/RoutedInput` (target
  `AudioIn/None`).
