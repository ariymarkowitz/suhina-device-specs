# Multiband Dynamics (manual 29.26)

**Family:** dynamics
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (top100 and remaining runs, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Splits the signal into up to three bands and gives each band two independent level-dependent gain stages: one above an "Above" threshold (downward compression, or upward expansion) and one below a "Below" threshold (upward compression, or downward expansion). A single instance can therefore do up to six kinds of dynamics processing at once [manual]. It was designed as a mastering tool; with ratios at 1 it works as a plain three-band crossover. Well-known uses: multiband compression, de-essing, "uncompression" (upward expansion) and presets such as OTT-style squashing [manual] [source: 2].

## Signal flow

Shared parts: [common.md](common.md) (side-chain; note that this device's side-chain has no EQ block).

```text
input ─► crossover split: Low | Mid | High (High and Low buttons switch bands on) [manual]
          Low band  ─► Input ─► [Below stage + Above stage] ─► Output ─┐
          Mid band  ─► Input ─► [Below stage + Above stage] ─► Output ─┼─► sum ─► global Output ─► device output
          High band ─► Input ─► [Below stage + Above stage] ─► Output ─┘
Detector source: own input, or external side-chain × Gain blended by the side-chain Dry/Wet [manual]
Each stage: level detector (Peak or RMS, shared switch) ─► static curve (threshold, ratio, Soft Knee)
            ─► attack / release (scaled by Time) ─► gain; ratio exponent scaled by Amount [manual]
```

- With both outer bands off, only the Mid controls act; the device is single-band [manual].
- The detector reads the band signal after Input gain (the input meters show the level before processing, the output bars after) [manual]. The detector sees the band after Input gain: with Input +12 dB the thresholds act 12 dB lower (Measured › Above stage) [measured 12.4.6].
- A deactivated band keeps its content out of the neighbouring band's processing: the split still happens, only that band's dynamics and gain controls are bypassed [manual] [source: 2].
- Dry/Wet in the side-chain section blends side-chain and own input as the detector's source, not the output [manual].

## Behaviour

**Crossovers.** Two crossover frequencies split the signal into three bands. The manual's example: low band 0 Hz to 500 Hz, mid 500 Hz to 2 kHz, high above 2 kHz [manual]. Filter slope and type are not documented. Measured: the splits are 24 dB/octave Linkwitz-Riley filters, -6 dB at each crossover, and the sum of the three bands is flat in magnitude to within 0.011 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz at three crossover settings; it is an all-pass, with a group delay of 4 ms at 120 Hz (Measured › Crossover) [measured 12.4.6]. Forum users report that using the device as a splitter (ratios 1) colours the sound and shows boosts or notches near the crossovers [source: 5]; at ratios 1 the render shows no magnitude coloration, so any coloration those reports describe is phase (the all-pass) or comes from the dynamics stages acting at low thresholds, not from the filters [inferred]. A deactivated band stays split off from its neighbours [source: 2].

**Static curve per band.** Two thresholds per band. Below the Below threshold the signal is processed by the Below stage, above the Above threshold by the Above stage, and between the thresholds the signal is unchanged (ratio 1) [manual] [inferred]. Above ratio R_a: output rises 1/R_a dB per dB above the threshold, so R_a > 1 is downward compression and R_a < 1 upward expansion. Below ratio R_b: output falls more than 1 dB per dB below the Below threshold if R_b < 1 (downward expansion) or less than 1 dB if R_b > 1 (upward compression) [manual] [inferred]. The ratio display "1:x" with x the compression ratio: 1:2.00 halves the dB distance, 1:0.50 doubles it. Typing 0.5 gives 1:2.00 and 2 gives 1:0.50 [source: 2], so the number typed is a slope and the display is its reciprocal [inferred]. Measured on the Mid band at the minimum Attack and Release: the Above ratios 1:2, 1:4 and 1:Inf compress and 1:0.67 expands; the Below ratios 1:2 and 1:Inf raise quiet signals and 1:0.5 and 1:0.25 lower them, as above. The Below 1:2 curve has the textbook slope (0.497 dB of gain per dB below the threshold); the Above curves fall short of it (gain slope 0.46, 0.64 and 0.77 dB per dB at 1:2, 1:4 and 1:Inf), and expansion matches (+0.496 dB per dB at 1:0.67) (Measured › Above stage, Below stage) [measured 12.4.6]. Nothing acts at or below the Above threshold with the knee off, and the gain change of the Below stage is limited to about 32 dB [measured 12.4.6] [inferred].

**Amount.** Scales the ratios toward 1: at 0 % every ratio is effectively 1 [manual]. Measured at 50 %: the gain change in dB is 0.51 to 0.53 of the 100 % one at every level, so the form is the gain change in dB multiplied by Amount (or the slope moved toward 1 by Amount, which is the same thing) (Measured › Above stage) [measured 12.4.6]. Amount does not go above 100 % [dump 12.4.6].

**Time.** A single factor on all attack and release times [manual]. Measured on the Mid band at Attack and Release 100 ms: Time 316 % makes the attack 3.1 times slower than 100 % (t63 34.5 ms against 11.1 ms) and the release 3.1 times slower (43 against 14 ms), and 10 % makes the attack finish in about 2.6 ms (Measured › Attack, Release and Time) [measured 12.4.6]. The range is 10 % to 1000 % [dump 12.4.6]; the 1000 % end was not rendered.

**Envelope.** For the Above stage, Attack applies when the level crosses over the threshold, and Release when it falls back; for the Below stage Attack applies when the level crosses under the threshold, and Release when it rises back [manual]. Measured on the Above stage (Peak, 1:4, Threshold -20 dB): the knob value is not a 63 % time. With Attack and Release 100 ms the gain change reaches 63 % after 11 ms and 90 % after 24 ms, and the reduction after a fall below the threshold is gone after 19 ms for Release 100 ms and 93 ms for Release 500 ms. A one-pole follower on the rectified level with a time constant of the setting divided by 8 (8 to 8.5 fits best) reproduces the attack curves to 0.4 dB [measured 12.4.6] [inferred from the measurement]. Because the follower acts within each cycle of the tone, the steady reduction depends on the pairing: Attack 100 ms with Release 0.1 ms gives no reduction at all (Measured › Things that affect how to read the renders). The Below stage's times were not measured; the manual's wording is unchanged [measure].

**Soft Knee.** Rounds the transition at both thresholds. Measured at 1:4 (Above) and 1:2 (Below), 3 dB resolution: the Above stage starts reducing about 6 dB below the threshold, with 1.3 dB more reduction than the hard knee at the threshold itself and under 0.2 dB difference from 12 dB above; the Below stage adds up to 0.8 dB of gain around its threshold, from about 6 dB below to 12 dB above. The shape is not resolved better than that; a quadratic knee remains likely [measured 12.4.6] [inferred]. With the knee off the corner is not sharp either in a sine measurement (1.11 dB of reduction 3 dB above a 1:4 threshold where the textbook gives 2.25 dB), because the gain follows the waveform within each cycle at the minimum times.

**RMS / Peak.** One switch for all bands [manual]. Peak: the detector follows peaks; RMS: a short running mean of squares, window [inferred]. Measured on the Mid band at Attack and Release 0.1 ms (1:4): the two modes give the same gain to within 0.03 dB at every level, with no 3 dB offset of the threshold for a sine (unlike Compressor's RMS mode) and the same onset speed; the RMS window did not show at the 1 ms resolution of the 1 s bursts, and no slower times were rendered, so the window is still [measure] [measured 12.4.6].

**Input and Output knobs.** Input is applied before detection and processing, band Output after; the global Output after the sum [manual]. Measured: the detector sees the band after the Input gain [measured 12.4.6]. A band's soloing mutes other bands (their processing continues so that the meters work, [inferred]) [manual].

**Display.** Bars show per-band levels, the "input" bars before the dynamics, the "output" bars after. Without processing, the input bars are aligned with the output bars' tops [manual].

**Side-chain.** An external signal, scaled by Gain, blended with the own input by Dry/Wet, drives the detectors of all bands; it is not heard [manual]. Whether the detector for each band sees the band-split side-chain signal or the full-range one is [measure] (the more usual design is that the same crossover splits the key).

**Latency.** The manual and the help centre mention none for Multiband Dynamics. Measured: the impulse response of the unity-gain device starts one to two samples after the input and peaks at sample 5 (0.11 ms); the delay is that of the crossover filters, no lookahead (Measured › Crossover). The title-bar tooltip was not read [measured 12.4.6].

## Edge cases

- Both High and Low off: only Mid acts, full range [manual].
- Crossover frequencies crossing each other (Low frequency above High frequency): [measure] (probably clamped so Low ≤ High) [inferred].
- Ratio 1 in all stages with Amount 100 %: crossover only. The magnitude is flat to within 0.011 dB, but the output is all-pass shifted (group delay up to 8 ms at low crossovers), so a null test against the dry signal fails at low frequencies [source: 5] [measured 12.4.6].
- Amount 0 %: all ratios 1, but Input and Output gains and soloing still act [manual] [inferred].
- Below threshold at its minimum (a tutorial uses −80 dB): the Below stage never acts [source: 4] [inferred]. The stage was measured only at −30 dB [measured 12.4.6].
- Above threshold below the Below threshold: [measure].
- Very short attack with low band: bass distortion, as for any compressor [inferred].
- Switching band activators or High/Low while playing: clicks or a crossfade [measure].
- Silence in, denormals, sample rates: [measure].

## Measured

[measured 12.4.6] Behaviour runs at 44.1 kHz in Live 12.4.6 (`measurements/29.26-multiband-dynamics/`: `multiband-dynamics-crossover`, `multiband-dynamics-above`, `multiband-dynamics-below`, `multiband-dynamics-time` in the top100 set; `multiband-dynamics-bands` in the remaining set; `mbd-sidechain`, `mbd-solo-crossovers`, `mbd-amount-time-below`, `mbd-rms-window`, `mbd-knee-ramp` in the third set, rendered by Live 12.4.7: Measured › Third set). Unless a case says otherwise: crossovers 120 Hz and 2.5 kHz, Peak detector, Soft Knee off, Amount and Time 100 %, every ratio 1 : 1, Mid Attack and Release at their 0.1 ms minimum, Input and Output gains 0 dB, so the 1 kHz probe tone is in the Mid band. The Mid Above Threshold is -30 dB in the crossover and Above runs, -20 dB (ratio 1 : 4) in the time run; in the Below run the Below Threshold is -30 dB and the Above Threshold 0 dB. Static gains are of the 1 kHz sine at the end of each 0.25 s staircase step: total RMS output over input RMS (the fundamental alone reads 0.1 to 0.5 dB lower under heavy compression), against the step's peak level in dBFS. Band responses come from the -6 dBFS impulse (65536-point FFT). These are observations, not a model.

### Things that affect how to read the renders

- **`multiband-dynamics-time att-100ms` is bit-identical to `multiband-dynamics-crossover default`.** The setting did reach the audio: that render has no gain change at all, so it is the unity-gain crossover render. In that case the Mid Above stage (threshold -20 dB, 1 : 4) is on, Attack is 100 ms and Release 0.1 ms, and the -6 dBFS burst gets 0.00 dB of reduction. With the same stage and Attack 1 ms the burst gets -6.3 dB, with 10 ms -0.29 dB, and with Attack and Release both 100 ms -7.1 dB (below, Attack, Release and Time). The cause is the pairing, not the Attack value: with a release a thousand times faster than the attack, the level follower falls back between the peaks of a 1 kHz sine and never climbs to the threshold [inferred from the measurement; a simple one-pole peak follower with time constants of Attack/8 and Release/8 gives 0.00 dB, -0.09 dB and -6.4 dB for 100, 10 and 1 ms, against 0.00, -0.29 and -6.3 dB measured]. Attack 100 ms is therefore not the device's default state (the defaults are 5 to 50 ms per band); the render only shows that the stage does nothing for this signal at that pairing, and says nothing about the 100 ms attack time itself (read from `time-100` instead).
- The Above and Below runs' `default` and `bypass` cases are the crossover run's renders (same device state, one shared track).
- The probe's tone is a staircase of 3 dB steps, 0.25 s each, so the soft knee and the thresholds are read at 3 dB resolution. With the minimum Attack and Release the gain follows the waveform inside each cycle (the reduction is about 7 dB near the zero crossings and 16.5 dB at the peaks for the -6 dBFS tone at 1 : 4, Threshold -30 dB), which is why THD figures of 10 to 35 % appear and why the fundamental's gain falls short of the textbook curve [measured 12.4.6].

### Crossover

Band responses at crossovers 500 Hz and 2 kHz, each band solved from the cases that hold the other two bands at -24 dB (the sum of the three equals the all-bands case to 0.00001) [measured 12.4.6]:

| Hz | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| 250 | -0.53 dB | -24.64 | -72.29 |
| 500 | -6.02 | -6.09 | -48.25 |
| 1000 | -24.62 | -1.09 | -24.65 |
| 2000 | -48.25 | -6.09 | -6.02 |
| 4000 | -72.48 | -24.81 | -0.52 |

- Each split is a 24 dB/octave Linkwitz-Riley: Low is 1 / (1 + (f / 500)^4) and High is its mirror, both -6.02 dB at the crossover and -3 dB at 0.80 times it for Low and 1.25 times for High (Low -3 dB at 401 Hz, High at 2494 Hz for 500 Hz / 2 kHz). The slopes read -24.2 to -24.8 dB/octave (Low, 2 to 8 kHz) and +24.0 dB/octave (High, 125 to 500 Hz); the magnitudes match the analog formula within 0.06 dB (High) and 0.23 dB (Low, to 4 kHz, where the stopband is below -72 dB) [measured 12.4.6].
- The Mid band is the high-pass at the lower crossover in series with the low-pass at the upper one (-1.09 dB at 1 kHz where the two filters give -0.53 dB each; -6.09 dB at both crossovers) [measured 12.4.6].
- The sum of the three bands is flat: within 0.003 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz at 500 Hz / 2 kHz, within 0.009 dB at the default 120 Hz / 2.5 kHz and within 0.011 dB at 60 Hz / 10 kHz (also flat from 16 to 20 kHz at 44.1 kHz). Flat in magnitude, not in phase: the sum is an all-pass, so it does not null against the input [measured 12.4.6].
- The phase shift shows as group delay of the sum: about 1.2 ms from 50 to 500 Hz at 500 Hz / 2 kHz, 4.0 ms at 120 Hz and 4.7 ms at 60 Hz at the default crossovers, 7.6 ms at 60 Hz with a 60 Hz crossover, and about 0.1 ms above 5 kHz. The low-frequency values agree with the delay of a fourth-order all-pass at the low crossover (2.83 / (2 pi f_c), 3.75 ms at 120 Hz) [measured 12.4.6] [inferred].

## Implementation notes

- **Crossover:** 24 dB/octave Linkwitz-Riley filters (1 / (1 + (f / f_c)^4), -6 dB at the crossover) [measured 12.4.6]; the Mid band is the high-pass at the lower crossover in series with the low-pass at the upper one, and the three bands sum to an all-pass flat to 0.011 dB, with group delay 2.83 / (2 pi f_c) summed over the crossovers plus about 0.1 ms. The Low and High bands' own phase was not read separately, so the all-pass compensation topology is open. Biquads, one-pole envelopes, parameter smoothing and dB conversions cover the rest.
- **Also needed:** a per-band two-sided gain computer (above and below thresholds), an RMS detector, a level-dependent display model, Amount and Time scaling, band solo handling, side-chain plumbing ([common.md](common.md) › Side-chain section).
- The Above stage is a standard compressor gain computer (threshold, ratio, quadratic soft knee, dB-domain smoothing), as in Compressor (29.9); the Below stage is the same function mirrored. Measured behaviour to match: the level follower acts inside each cycle at the minimum times, so a sine at 0.1 ms times shows gain modulation (THD 10 to 35 %) and a reduction well under the textbook value; Attack and Release are about 8 time constants (a one-pole follower on the rectified level with tau = setting / 8 fits), and the detector sits after the Input gain [measured 12.4.6] [inferred]. The gain change of the Below stage stops at about 32 dB.
- The display can follow the manual's interactions (bars, drag edges and middles, modifier keys), since they are plain UI behaviour.
- `.als` import: the element is `MultibandDynamics` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements in the set saved from Live 12.4.6 [als 12.4.6], each holding a `Manual` value: `On`, `SplitLowMid` and `SplitMidHigh` (Hz), `SoftKnee` (bool), `EnvelopeIsPeak` (bool, false = RMS), `OutputGain` (dB), `GlobalAmount` (fraction), `GlobalTime` (factor, 1 = 100 %), and per band (suffix `Low`, `Mid` or `High`): `GainLow`/`GainMid`/`GainHigh` (band output gain, dB), `InputGainLow`/`Mid`/`High` (dB), `ActiveLow`/`Mid`/`High` (bool), `AboveThresholdLow`… and `BelowThresholdLow`… (dB), `AboveRatioLow`… and `BelowRatioLow`… (the API values, 0 for 1 : 1.00; see the ratio rows), `AttackLow`… and `ReleaseLow`… (ms), and `SideChain/OnOff` (bool), `SideChain/RoutedInput/Volume` (linear gain, 1 = 0 dB), `SideChain/DryWet` (fraction). Times, thresholds and gains are therefore stored in physical units, not as the log-domain API values. Not parameters but plain values [als 12.4.6]: `SplitLowMidOn` and `SplitMidHighOn` (the Low and High buttons, true), `SoloLow`, `SoloMid`, `SoloHigh` (false), `SideListen` (false), `ActiveEditMode` (0, the T / B / A choice), and the side-chain source as `SideChain/RoutedInput` (target `AudioIn/None`).
