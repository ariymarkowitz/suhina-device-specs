# Random (manual 30.6)

**Family:** note transformation
**Note status:** dump checked (manual and public sources only; nothing measured yet)

## Summary

Random moves the pitch of some incoming notes by a randomly chosen number of semitones (or, with
Use Current Scale, scale degrees), upwards, downwards or both. A Chance control sets how many
notes are touched, Choices and Interval set how many different shifts exist and how far apart
they are, and an alternate mode steps through the shifts in turn instead of drawing them at
random. It is used on repeated notes (drum hits, arpeggios, step-sequenced lines) to add
variation [manual].

History: the device is in the Live 11 manual with the same idea (the Live 10 manual could not be reached); Live 11 called the
spacing control **Scale** (a multiplier, not a musical scale) and had a single Rnd/Alt mode
switch [source: 2]. Live 12.0 renamed Scale to **Interval**, split the mode switch into separate
Random and Alt toggles, added the **Use Current Scale** toggle and made per-note pitch bend follow
the transformed pitch [source: 3]. Live 12.1.10 fixed per-note pitch bend in Random when scale
awareness is on and the incoming note is outside the scale [source: 3].

## MIDI flow

Shared parts: [common.md](common.md) (title bar and Use Current Scale, Scale Awareness,
note-off and MPE handling, racks, latency).

```text
note-on  ─► Chance test ─► pick a shift (Random: draw; Alt: next in cycle) ─► apply Sign ─► new pitch ─► out
              [manual]        [manual]                                         [manual]       │
                                                                                             └─► LED +, 0 or − [manual]
note-off ─► looked up by the note it ends; sent at the pitch its note-on went out at          [inferred]
other messages ─► not described by the manual; per-note pitch bend is moved with the note       [source: 3]
```

- Only the pitch of note-ons is changed. Velocity, channel and timing of the note are not
  mentioned by the manual and are taken to be unchanged [inferred].
- The device has no clock, so no tempo, transport or sync dependence and no latency [inferred]
  (check in the Measurement plan, item 10).
- The random generator is part of the device. Whether its sequence repeats from one playback to
  the next, or is seeded per transport start, is unknown [measure].

### Context menu and hidden options

## Behaviour

### Chance and the shift pool (Random mode, Sign Add)

The manual gives two examples, both with Mode Random, Sign Add, Chance 50 % and a repeated C3
[manual]:

- Choices 1, Interval 12: about half of the notes sound at C3 and half at C4, in no order.
- Choices 12, Interval 1: about half stay at C3 and the rest land on any note from C#3 up to C4.

These fix the pool of shifts in Random mode with Add. The shift is a multiple of Interval, from
1 × Interval up to Choices × Interval, so Choices 12, Interval 1 reaches +1 … +12 semitones and
Choices 1, Interval 12 only +12 [inferred from the two examples]. A pool of Choices values drawn
uniformly with equal probability is the natural reading; the distribution is [measure].

Two readings the examples cannot separate:

1. The pool is {1, …, Choices} × Interval and "unchanged" comes only from the Chance test.
2. The pool also holds 0, {0, …, Choices} × Interval, so even Chance 100 % sometimes leaves a
   note unchanged. The manual's "half stay at C3" favours reading 1 (with reading 2 the fraction
   at C3 would be 50 % + 50 %/13, about 54 %), but a Computer Music tutorial summarised by a
   search engine says Choices 3 gives notes "up to three semitones higher or the note itself",
   which fits either reading [source: 7]. Which one holds is [measure] (item 1). The 0 LED is
   the observable: at Chance 100 % it only lights under reading 2.

### Alt mode

The manual says Alt steps through the possible pitches in a fixed round-robin order, and that
Chance behaves differently: at 100 % every note takes the next step of the sequence, and at 0 %
every note is output unchanged [manual]. Examples, Chance 100 %, repeated C3 [manual]:

- Choices 12, Interval 1: C3, then each following C3 one semitone higher, "until C4", then the
  cycle starts again from C3.
- Choices 2, Interval 2: alternates between C3 and D3.

The second example is the clearest: with two choices and Interval 2 the shifts are 0 and +2, so
**in Alt mode the cycle is 0, Interval, 2 × Interval, … (Choices − 1) × Interval**, which is
Choices values that start at the unchanged pitch [inferred]. For Choices 12, Interval 1 this
gives C3 … B3, 12 values, while the manual's wording "until C4" suggests C4 is also played; the
two examples cannot both hold unless the manual's wording is loose. Whether the cycle length is
Choices or Choices + 1, and whether C4 is output, is [measure] (item 3).

Open details of Alt mode, all [measure]:

- At Chance between 0 and 100 %: a forum description is that a counter steps up by a fixed amount
  and wraps, with Chance deciding whether each note takes the step [source: 7]. Whether a note
  that fails the Chance test still advances the counter is unknown.
- Whether there is one counter for the whole device or one per input pitch (or per channel).
- Whether the counter resets when the transport starts, a clip launches, or notes stop.
- How Sign works in Alt: Sub is expected to cycle downwards; Bi may alternate sign or cycle a
  symmetric list.

### Sign

Add moves the pitch up, Sub down, Bi either way [manual]. With Sub the pool is expected to be the
mirror image of Add's (−Interval … −Choices × Interval). Bi may draw the sign at random with equal
probability, or may use a pool of 2 × Choices values; the manual does not say [measure] (item 2).

### Scale awareness

With Use Current Scale on, the device uses the clip's root and scale; Choices and Interval count
scale degrees instead of semitones, and random pitches are constrained to the scale
[manual; source: 4]. What a shift of k degrees means for an input note that is not in the scale is
not stated; the 12.1.10 fix shows such notes exist as a case [source: 3]. Candidate rules to
test [measure]: the input is first moved to the nearest scale tone; or the shift is counted from
the nearest scale tone below; or the input is shifted by whole semitones to the next scale tone
above. Which clip supplies the scale, and what happens in a clip without a scale, are in
[common.md](common.md).

### Note-offs

The manual does not say how a note-off follows a note-on whose pitch was randomised. The only
workable behaviour is that the device remembers the output pitch of every sounding note and sends
the note-off at that pitch when the input note-off arrives [inferred]. Cases to check [measure]
(item 5): the same input pitch retriggered while the first note is held (two entries for one
input pitch; which note-off ends which), a changed Chance or Choices between note-on and
note-off, notes sent on different channels, and the device switched off while a note is held.
General handling (MPE, sustain, all-notes-off) is in [common.md](common.md).

### Range limits

The output is limited to MIDI pitch 0 – 127. Shifts past 127 or below 0 could be dropped,
clamped, folded back or redrawn; the manual does not say [measure] (item 6).

### LEDs

The three LEDs flash once per note-on after the decision: + if the output is higher than the
input, 0 if equal, − if lower [manual]. They are a display only and can be left out of the DSP.

## Edge cases

- **Chance 0 %:** every note is unchanged in both modes [manual for Alt; inferred for Random].
- **Chance 100 %, Random mode:** every note is shifted, unless the pool contains 0 (see above).
- **Choices 1:** one possible shift (Interval), so Random mode becomes a repeatable
  octave-style jump with probability Chance; Alt then alternates 0 only (every note unchanged)
  if the cycle is Choices long [inferred; measure].
- **Interval 12:** shifts are whole octaves [manual example].
- **Chords:** each note of a simultaneous chord is probably drawn separately; the Mode Audio
  article says the Velocity device's Random is per note of a chord [source: 6], but nothing is
  said for Random's pitch. Alt may step once per note or once per chord [measure].
- **Same pitch twice, overlapping,** note-offs after a changed setting, transport stop with held
  notes, pitch past 0 or 127: see Note-offs and Range limits.
- **Pitch bend, MPE:** per-note pitch bend follows the shifted note (12.0), and out-of-scale
  inputs were fixed in 12.1.10 [source: 3]; details in [common.md](common.md).
- **Device off:** passes everything unchanged; held notes at the moment of switching [measure].

## Measurement plan

Everything is read from the MIDI the device emits, so the output must be recorded as MIDI (route
the track to a second MIDI track and record, which needs Live's window; AGENTS.md) or, as a second
choice, played into an instrument with a known pitch response and read from the rendered audio by
pitch detection (the existing harness). MIDI recording is exact and is preferred. The parameter
ranges, defaults, step lists and display text come from the Suhina Probe's dump or the Remote
Script's `get_device_parameters`, and stored values from a saved `.als`.

**Input clip for items 1–4:** 4 bars at 1/16, all C3 (pitch 60), 64 notes per bar, length 1/32
(short, no overlap), velocity 100, repeated over 32 loops (about 2000 notes). Record the output
clip and tabulate shift = output − 60 and the LED state if it can be read.

1. **Pool and distribution, Random mode, Add.** Chance 100 %: Choices 1, 2, 3, 12, 24 with Interval
   1, 2, 12 (a few combinations are enough: 1/1, 3/1, 3/2, 12/1, 1/12, 24/12). Read the set of
   shifts and their frequencies: does 0 occur at Chance 100 %? Are the shifts uniform? Then Chance
   25, 50, 75 % to check that the unchanged share is 1 − Chance.
2. **Sign.** The same at Sub and at Bi: pool, symmetry, share of each sign.
3. **Alt mode.** Chance 100 %, Choices 12 / Interval 1 and Choices 2 / Interval 2, 3 / 4: the
   sequence for 40 notes (first output, cycle length, whether C4 occurs, restart). Then Chance 50 %
   and 0 %: does a failed test advance the counter? Alt with Sub and Bi.
4. **Randomness.** Take the same clip twice, once after stopping and restarting the transport and
   once without: are the sequences identical (seeded at start) or different? Also two instances
   of the device, and the device after a Set reload.
5. **Note-offs.** A clip with overlapping notes of equal and different pitches: C3 held 1 bar with
   C3 retriggered every 1/4 inside it; a chord of three notes with unequal lengths; notes on two
   channels. Compare each output note-on with its output note-off (pitch, time). Change Choices
   between a note-on and its note-off (automate in the clip).
6. **Limits.** Input pitches 0, 1, 126, 127 at Choices 24, Interval 12, Add and Sub: dropped,
   clamped, folded, redrawn.
7. **Scale awareness.** A clip in C major with Use Current Scale on: Choices 3, Interval 1 and 2,
   Chance 100 %, inputs C3, D3 and an out-of-scale C#3; read the shifts. Repeat with a minor
   pentatonic scale, a clip with no scale, and with Use Current Scale on while the clip's scale is
   changed during playback.
8. **Other messages.** Velocity, channel, note length, pitch bend, CC, aftertouch: unchanged?
   Per-note pitch bend with MPE input.
9. **Chords.** Three simultaneous notes, Alt: one step per note or per chord.
10. **Latency and off.** Output timing against input to the tick; device off with notes held.

## Implementation notes

- **Structure:** a per-block event pass. A held-note table keyed by (channel, input pitch)
  stores the output pitch (a small stack per key to handle retriggers); note-ons draw the shift
  and push; note-offs pop and send. A generator whose state is seeded and reset deliberately (the
  seeding rule is [measure]); xorshift or PCG-class generators are fine.
- **Alt:** a counter in 0 … Choices − 1 (cycle length [measure]), advanced per accepted note.
- **Scale:** a function from (input pitch, degrees offset) to output pitch that the scale-aware
  devices share; the clip's scale arrives from the host (see [common.md](common.md)).
- **Parameters to ship:** Chance (0 – 1), Choices (1 – 24), Interval (1 – 24), Mode (2 states),
  Sign (3 states), Use Current Scale [dump 12.4.6].
- **`.als` import.** The device element is `MidiRandom` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements, in
  stored order, all as the API shows them [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `Chance` (0 – 1), `Choices`
  (1 – 24), `Scale` (Interval, 1 – 24), `Sign` (index 0 – 2: Add, Sub, Bi), `Alternate` (Mode,
  bool: false = Rnd, true = Alt), `UseCurrentScale` (bool). No other state is stored. Sets saved
  before 12.0 hold the old single Mode switch and a Scale value
  that has to become Interval [inferred from source 3].
