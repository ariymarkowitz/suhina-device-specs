# Chord (manual 30.3)

**Family:** note generation
**Note status:** dump checked, partly measured (MIDI recordings, [Measured](#measured))

## Summary

Adds up to six extra notes to every note that passes through, each a fixed distance above or
below the incoming note, so one key plays a chord. Each added note has its own velocity scale and
its own probability of sounding. Since Live 12.0 the added notes can be spread out in time like a
guitar strum, with a speed curve and a velocity ramp, the shifts can be learned from a chord
played on a keyboard, and the shift distances can be counted in scale degrees of the clip's scale
instead of semitones [manual; source: 2]. The incoming note always sounds; the device is used to
turn a single-finger melody into chords or to thicken a bass or lead [manual].

History: the Live 11 device had only Shift 1 to 6 (±36 semitones) and a Velocity knob under each
[source: 3]. Live 12.0 added Use Current Scale, Strum, Tension, Crescendo, the Velocity/Chance
toggle (the per-note Chance sliders), Learn, the LEDs and the per-note MPE option [source: 2].
12.1 fixed a Chord device that altered per-note pitch bend while bypassed and following a scale,
and fixed per-note expression for strummed notes [source: 2].

## MIDI flow

Shared parts (title bar, Use Current Scale and how the clip's scale is chosen, note-off and MPE
handling, racks, latency): [common.md](common.md).

```text
incoming note-on (pitch p, velocity v)
   │
   ├─► root note (the incoming note itself): pitch p, velocity v ─────────────┐
   │                                                                          │
   └─► for each active Shift n = 1..6                                         │
          pitch  = p + Shift n  (semitones, or scale degrees in the clip     │
                                 scale when Use Current Scale is on) [manual] │
          velocity = v × Velocity n / 100  (only when the Velocity toggle     │
                                 is on [manual]; otherwise v [inferred])      │
          chance gate: sounds with probability Chance n                        │
                                 (only when the Chance toggle is on [manual]) │
                                                                              ▼
          Strum / Tension: start-time offset per note [manual] ─► Crescendo velocity ramp [manual] ─► out

incoming note-off for p ─► ends every note generated from that note-on, at the note-off's time [measured 12.4.6]
other messages (pitch bend, aftertouch, CC) ─► pass through; per-note MPE copied to the generated
   notes only if the context-menu option is on [manual]; see common.md
```

- Duplicates: a pitch can be assigned to only one Shift control; a later duplicate is switched
  off and shows as disabled [manual; source: 3]. The root is the incoming note; whether a Shift of
  0 (the same pitch as the root) counts as a duplicate is [measure].
- The order of the stages after the shifts (chance before or after velocity, Crescendo applied to
  the Velocity-scaled value, and so on) is stated only in part: the release notes say Crescendo is
  a further multiplier on top of the per-note Velocity controls, and that
  the ramp follows the order in which the notes are played [source: 2].

Names are the manual's; the Live API's names are in quotes. Left to right: title bar, Shift row,
per-note rows, strum block. Ranges, defaults and displays are from the Live 12.4.6 parameter
dump of a device freshly loaded from the browser [dump 12.4.6]; loaded and reset values agree
for every parameter. The API has 29 parameters: Device On, then per slot n = 1 … 6 "Shiftn",
"Shiftn Scale Degrees", "Velocityn", "Chancen", then "Strum", "Strum Tension",
"Strum Crescendo" and "Use Current Scale". Learn and the Velocity and Chance row toggles are not
API parameters.

## Behaviour

**Pitch.** Output pitch of a Shift is `p + Shift`, in semitones; a pitch outside MIDI 0 – 127 is
**dropped**, the other notes of the chord still sound: input 120 with +4 and +36 gave 120 and 124
only, and input 5 with −4 and −36 gave 1 and 5 only [measured 12.4.6]. Shifts of −36 and +36 on
input 60 gave 24 and 96. With Use Current Scale on, each Shift counts scale degrees (the Scale
Degrees parameter) from the incoming note through the clip's scale, and the semitone Shift
parameters are ignored (Shift 3 = +12 changed nothing with the degree shifts +2 and +4)
[measured 12.4.6]. An incoming note that is not in the scale is first moved to the nearest scale
note, a tie going down, and the incoming note itself sounds at that scale note: in C major C♯3 plays
C3 with E and G, D♯3 plays D3 with F and A; in C minor (E♭ F G A♭ B♭) E3 plays E♭3 with G and B♭ and
the shifts +2, +4 degrees give the minor triad; with no scale on the clip the degree shifts count semitones
(+2, +4 gave 62, 64 above 60). The same rule as Pitch and Random [measured 12.4.6]. In scale mode the pitch
is **clamped** to 0 … 127 instead of dropped: +36 degrees from 65 gave 127, −36 degrees from
60 gave 0, where a semitone shift would be dropped [measured 12.4.6].

**Duplicates.** Two Shifts with the same value (Shift 3 = Shift 2 = +7), or a Shift of 0 (the root's
own pitch), make one note, not two: the output was C, E, G only [measured 12.4.6]. The Live 11
manual adds that two notes of one pitch never sound at once anywhere in Live [source: 3]. When two
incoming notes held together generate the same pitch (a chord with Shift +4 and +7 on C, E, G)
was not recorded (the case set was empty) [measure].

**Chance.** An independent random draw per Shift per incoming note-on, with probability
`Chance / 100`: with Chance 50 % on two Shifts over 200 notes, +4 sounded for 110 and 101 notes (two
instances), +7 for 99 and 99, and both together for 51 and 52 (a quarter) [measured 12.4.6]. 0 % never
plays (200 of 200 notes had only +7 when Chance 1 was 0 and Chance 2 was 100 %), 100 % always plays.
The two instances drew different sequences [measured 12.4.6]; repeatability across playbacks was
not recorded. The root is not subject to chance.

**Velocity.** `v_out = clamp(v_in × Velocity / 100, 1, 127)` with no rounding [measured 12.4.6]:
Velocity 50 % turned 100 into 50 and 127 into 63.5, 200 % turned 100 into 127 and 1 into 2, 1 %
turned 127 into 1.27 and 100 into 1; velocity 1 at 50 % stayed 1. The root keeps the incoming
velocity. Whether a hidden slider with a non-default value still scales is [measure].

**Strum.** Each added note starts `k × Strum` after the root's start, where `k` is its place in the
order of the notes that sound and Strum is the gap between neighbours (the display's ms: Strum 100 ms
at 120 BPM gave starts at 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 beats) [measured 12.4.6].
- Positive Strum: the root first (k = 0), then Shift 1, 2, 3 in control order; negative Strum: the
  reverse, so the highest Shift sounds first and the root last, delayed by the whole spread (Strum
  −100 ms played 72, 67, 64, 60 at 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 beats). The first note to sound plays at once
  [measured 12.4.6].
- A Shift that is disabled (a duplicate, or 0) is skipped and the others close up: with Shift 1 = 0
  and Shift 2 = Shift 3 = +4, the +4 played at k = 1 (100 ms) [measured 12.4.6].
- **Each strummed note ends with the input note**, at the input note-off: with Strum 400 ms and an
  input of 1 beat, the note at 0.8 beats lasted 0.2 beats, and a note whose start comes after the
  input's end (1.6 and 2.4 beats) did not sound at all. With a 1/32 input only the root sounded.
  [measured 12.4.6].
- Tension: 0 gives equal gaps. A negative value moves the starts toward the front with
  `t_k = span × (k/N)^p`, where `N` is the number of strummed notes (3 in the recording), `span =
  N × Strum` and `p = 1.5` at −50 % and `p = 3` at −100 % (with Strum 400 ms, start times 0.462 beats at
  −50 %, and 0.0889 and 0.711 beats at −100 %, against 0.8 and 1.6 beats at 0) [measured 12.4.6]. The exponent
  could be `1 + 2·|Tension|²` (1.5 and 3) or another curve through those two points. Positive Tension pushed the first
  strummed note past the 1-beat input in both recorded cases (+50 and +100), so it is later than
  at 0; its law is [measure] (a longer input note is needed).
- Crescendo: a velocity multiplier of `k / N` for +100 % and `1 − k / N` for −100 % (N = 3): at
  +100 % the root played at the floor 1, Shift 1 at one third (33.3 for 100, 42.3 for 127); at −100 % the
  root kept its velocity and Shift 1 played at two thirds (66.7, 84.7). Intermediate values: [measure]
  [measured 12.4.6].

**Note-offs.** Every generated note ends when the incoming note ends, or earlier if it has not
started by then (see Strum) [measured 12.4.6]. A change of Shift 1 from +4 to +5 by automation while a
4-beat note was held left all four notes (60, 64, 67, 72) as they were, ending at 4 beats, with no
hanging note; the device switched off from beat 3 to 6 (and on again) under an 8-beat held note changed
nothing: all four notes lasted 8 beats [measured 12.4.6]. Release velocity of the generated note-off
is presumably the incoming one [inferred].

**Learn.** While Learn is on, notes arriving at the device set Shifts 1 to 6 from the held keys:
shift_n = (nth later held pitch) − (first held pitch) [manual]. Whether keys are taken in order of
pressing or in order of pitch, what happens to Shifts left over when fewer than six extra keys are
held, whether pitches more than 36 semitones from the root are refused or clamped, and whether the
learned notes also play through the device while learning, are [measure]. Whether the held
velocities set the Velocity sliders is a disagreement between the manual and the release notes
(see the Learn row of the Controls table).

**MPE.** The incoming note's per-note expression is copied to the generated notes only when Send
Per Note Events to Generated Notes is on [manual]. With scale awareness the bends are re-mapped so
that every note of the bent chord stays in the scale [manual; source: 2]; the mapping is [measure].
12.1 fixed a case where instruments that read expression relative to its value at note start got
wrong values for strummed notes, which means a delayed note starts with the expression's value
at the moment of its own start [inferred from source: 2]. Also see [common.md](common.md).

**Latency.** None: the root and, without Strum, every added note start at the incoming note's start; the
strum delays are musical delays that host delay compensation does not remove [measured 12.4.6].

## Edge cases

[measured 12.4.6] where tagged.

- **Same pitch twice in one chord** (duplicates): deactivated, one note [measured 12.4.6]; with the
  context-menu option, an extra note plays [manual; not recorded: it is not a parameter].
- **Pitch out of 0 – 127:** dropped in semitones, clamped in scale degrees [measured 12.4.6].
- **Velocity 1 and 127:** 200 % on an incoming 100 gives 127 and 1 % gives 1, not 0
  [measured 12.4.6].
- **Transport stop, loop jump or tempo change during a strum:** not recorded (one tempo, no stops);
  strum time is in ms so a tempo change does not alter it [inferred].
- **Held notes when the device is switched off:** the sounding generated notes keep their
  note-offs [measured 12.4.6]; Use Current Scale toggled while notes are held: not recorded. A
  deactivated Chord leaves per-note pitch bend alone since 12.1 [source: 2].
- **Shift changed while a note is held:** the note-offs match the old chord [measured 12.4.6]. Learn:
  not recorded.
- **Chance < 100 % on a held chord:** the note-off for a note that never started: not recorded as a
  separate case (no stray note-offs were seen in the notes read back).
- **Sustain pedal:** not read by Chord [inferred]; see common.md.
- **More than six notes** are not possible; a forum suggestion for larger chords is parallel Chord
  devices in separate Rack chains [source: 7].

## Measurement plan

Done: items 2 – 5, 8 and 9's duplicate case without the option (the option is not a parameter) were recorded; items
6, 7 and 10 were not; see [Measured](#measured).

Observation: record the device's output on a second MIDI track and read the notes back, as in
[common.md](common.md) › Measurement plan shared by the MIDI effects (arming and recording are
the user's step, AGENTS.md). The shared cases C1 (switch-off while notes sound), C2 (latency),
C3 (transport stop with generated notes sounding), C4 (scale degrees), C5 (MPE) and C6 (Rack)
apply to Chord; items 8 and 10 below are C4 and C5 with Chord's settings. Fallback: a sine
instrument after the device and the harness' audio analysis, which gives pitch and onset time
but not exact velocity.

Input clip A: one bar at 120 bpm, a single C3 (MIDI 60), length 1 beat, velocity 100, at beat 1
of 4 (and a copy with velocity 1 and 127). Clip B: notes C3, E3, G3 held together for 2 beats.
Clip C: C major scale ascending one note per beat, then the chromatic octave, with the clip scale
set to C major (root C, Major) to test scale mode; a second copy with scale off.

1. **Ranges, defaults, steps, display text.** Done: the Live 12.4.6 parameter dump and saved set
   (Controls, `.als` import). Learn and the Velocity/Chance row toggles are not parameters.
2. **Shift pitch, duplicates and disabled slots.** Clip A with Shift 1 = +4, Shift 2 = +7; then
   Shift 3 = +7 (duplicate), then Shift 3 = 0, then Shift 1 = −36, Shift 2 = +36. Read which
   pitches come out; try on notes near 0 and 127 (Clip A transposed to MIDI 120 and 5).
3. **Velocity and Chance.** Clip A with Velocity 1 = 50 %, 200 %, 1 %; read output velocities
   (rounding and the 127 limit). Chance 50 % on one Shift over 200 repeats: the hit rate, and
   whether two Shifts are independent. Rerun twice to see if the sequence repeats. Toggles off with
   non-default sliders: do they still act?
4. **Strum.** Clip A with Shifts +4, +7, +12; Strum 400, 200, 100, 0, −100, −400 ms: start time
   of each note. Tension 0, +50, +100, −50, −100: gap sequence. Crescendo ±100 %: velocity
   sequence. Include a Shift disabled in the middle, and Chance 0 on one Shift. Repeat at 60 and
   180 bpm to confirm milliseconds.
5. **Note-off handling.** Clip A with note length 1 beat, Strum 400 ms: when does each generated
   note end? Then a note shorter than the strum (1/32). Then change Shift 1 from +4 to +5 with the
   transport running and a held note, and compare the off pitch (hanging note check).
6. **Same pitch from two sources.** Clip B (C, E, G) with Shift 1 = +4, +7: note count and the
   overlapping pitches' note-offs.
7. **Learn.** Needs notes held at the device input while Learn is on: a clip with C3, E3, G3
   held for 4 beats on the track, Learn switched on for those beats by automation of the Learn
   toggle is not possible (it is not a parameter), so the user plays a keyboard; then read the Shifts and
   Velocities back with `get_device_parameters`. Repeat with velocities 40, 80, 120 to settle the contradiction.
8. **Scale mode.** Clip C with the scale C major and Use Current Scale on, Shift 1 = +2 (a
   third), Shift 2 = +4 (a fifth); read outputs for in-scale notes and for each chromatic note.
   Repeat with C minor and with no scale (toggle on, scale off). Shifts in degrees at ±36 sd:
   pitches past 0 / 127.
9. **Play Duplicate Notes When Strumming.** Shift 1 = 0, Shift 2 = +4, Shift 3 = +4, option off and
   on, Strum 100 ms.
10. **MPE.** A clip with per-note pitch bend, option on and off, scale on and off: output
    channels and expression values (needs an MPE-aware route).

## Measured

Recorded from Live 12.4.6 (`devices/30.03-chord/results/*.json`; runs `chord-shift`, `-velocity`,
`-chance`, `-strum`, `-noteoff`, `-scale`; 120 BPM), laws in Behaviour [measured 12.4.6]:

- Added notes `p + Shift`, dropped past 0 – 127 (clamped in scale degrees); duplicates and 0 make no note.
- Velocity scaled and clamped to ≥ 1, ≤ 127, not rounded. Chance independent per Shift.
- Strum: gap `Strum` between neighbours, root first (reverse for negative); notes end with
  the input, so a delayed note after the input's end is lost; Tension negative = power curve,
  Crescendo ±100 % = ramp k/3.
- Use Current Scale: nearest scale note, ties down, then degrees; semitone Shifts ignored.
- Note-offs follow the input; no latency.

Not measured: positive Tension, Crescendo below ±100 %, Strum at other tempos, Learn, the duplicate
option, MPE, the case of two input notes producing one pitch.

## Implementation notes

- Per-block MIDI event list in, event list out; a delay line of timestamped events for strummed
  notes (sample-accurate, seconds not samples); a held-note table keyed by incoming (channel,
  pitch) that stores each generated note's actual pitch so the matching note-off is exact even if
  the Shifts change while the note is held.
- Random: a seeded generator advancing once per Shift per note-on, reset on transport start so
  renders are repeatable. Live's own generator is unknown, so no sequence match is possible.
- Scale mode needs the clip-scale service (see common.md). Treat the scale as `(root, interval
  set)`; map degree shifts through it.
- Hard to match: Learn semantics, positive Tension and the Crescendo curve between 0 and ±100 %.
- **`.als` import.** The device element is `MidiChord` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements, in
  stored order [als 12.4.6]: `On`, then per slot n = 1 … 6 `Shiftn` (semitones, −36 – 36),
  `ShiftScaleDegreesn` (degrees, −36 – 36), `Velocityn` (the factor, 0.01 – 2, 1 = 100 %),
  `Chancen` (0 – 1), then `Strum` (**stored in ms**, −400 – 400, not the API's −20 – 20:
  stored = sign(x) × x²), `StrumTension` and `StrumCrescendo` (percent, −100 – 100) and
  `UseCurrentScale` (bool). The stored ranges are the elements' `MidiControllerRange`. Not
  parameters, also stored: `PlayDuplicateNotesWhenStrumming` (false) and
  `SendPerNoteEventsToGeneratedNotes` (true) for the two context-menu options, and four
  view-scroll positions (`ShiftScrollPosArranger`, `…Envelope`, and the same for the degree
  Shifts). The Velocity / Chance row toggles are not stored.
