# Pitch (manual 30.5)

**Family:** note transformation
**Note status:** dump checked

## Summary

Transposes every note that passes through by a fixed amount: up to 128 semitones either way, or,
since Live 12.0, up to 30 scale degrees of the clip's scale. A pitch window set by Lowest and
Range decides which notes are allowed through, and a Mode chooser decides what happens to those
outside it: they are blocked, folded back into the window or pushed to its nearest edge. Step
Up and Step Down buttons move the transposition by a set width, so a note or a controller
button can change key in jumps. Used for key changes, octave doubling (in a Rack), limiting a
player to a register and live transposition [manual].

History: Live 11 had Pitch, Lowest and Range with one behaviour: notes outside the window were
blocked, the window being tested on the *untransposed* pitch, before transposition [source: 3].
Live 11.2.10 slightly updated the UI [source: 4]. Live 12.0 added Use Current Scale, the Step
Down / Step Up buttons with Step Width, the Mode chooser (Block, Fold, Limit), moved Lowest and
Range, and made per-note pitch bend follow the scale [source: 2]. 12.0.5 made the Pitch control
less sensitive to the mouse and fixed the device's width regardless of control-surface lock;
12.1.10 fixed per-note pitch bend for out-of-scale incoming notes with scale awareness on
[source: 2].

## MIDI flow

Shared parts (title bar, Use Current Scale and how the clip's scale is chosen, note-off and MPE
handling, racks, latency): [common.md](common.md).

```text
incoming note-on (p, v)
   │
   ▼
 range test on p vs [Lowest, Lowest + Range]   [Live 11 manual: on the untransposed pitch]
   │ inside ─────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ outside ─► Mode:                            │
   │            Block: note dropped, LED flashes │
   │            Fold:  p moved into the window   │   (before or after the transposition is [measure])
   │            Limit: p set to the nearest edge │
   ▼                                             ▼
 transposition by Pitch (semitones or scale degrees) ─► out-of-range 0..127 handling [measure] ─► note-on (p', v)
incoming note-off ─► note-off for the same output pitch the note-on produced [inferred]
other messages ─► pass through; per-note pitch bend of MPE notes is re-mapped when scale awareness is on
```

The order of the range test and the transposition is the central open point: the Live 11 manual
says the range is tested on the pitch *before* the transposition [source: 3] and a third-party
explainer repeats it [source: 7]; the Live 12 manual text no longer says it [source: 1], and what
Fold and Limit do relative to it is not stated at all.

## Behaviour

**Transposition.** `p' = p + Pitch` in semitones for Pitch measured in st. Pitch is an integer;
there is no fine tuning and no cents (the device does not send pitch bend) [inferred]. With Use
Current Scale on, `Pitch` counts steps along the clip's scale, so +1 moves a note to the next
scale note up [manual], and the interval in semitones depends on where the note is in the scale
(+1 degree from C in C major is +2 st, from E it is +1 st) [inferred]. How degrees are counted
from a note that is not in the scale, how the count wraps at the octave and whether degrees are
counted from the note's own degree are [measure] (shared question: common.md, case C4).

**Range test and Block.** Lowest and Range define the pitch window `[Lowest, Lowest + Range]`
through which notes may pass [manual]. Notes outside are "blocked": no note-on is sent and the
LED next to Range flashes [manual]. The Live 11 manual states the test uses the pitch *before*
transposing [source: 3]; this is the most plausible reading for Live 12 too, since Block is
unchanged [inferred], but the Live 12 text omits it [measure]. Consequence if it holds: with
Pitch = +24 and a window C3 – C4 a note C3 passes and sounds C5; a note C5 is blocked.
Whether the bounds are inclusive at the top (Lowest + Range) and the bottom is [measure]; a forum
post that uses Lowest = C2, Range = +11 to hold a bass line to one octave suggests the window spans
Range + 1 notes [source: 6].

**Fold.** Notes outside the window are "transposed to fit within the range" [manual]. The manual
does not say by what. Candidates, all [measure]: shift by whole octaves until inside (impossible if
the window is narrower than an octave, in which case the note is perhaps blocked or clamped); a
modulo of the offset from Lowest with period Range + 1 (wrap, which for Range = 11 equals octave
folding and for Range = 0 collapses all notes onto Lowest, as the forum post suggests [source: 6]);
reflection at the edges. The window of 12 notes is the common use, and it is the only case where
all readings agree.

**Limit.** Notes below the window are raised to its lowest note, notes above are lowered to its
highest note [manual]. This is a clamp to `[Lowest, Lowest + Range]`.

**Whether Fold and Limit act before or after the transposition.** Not stated. If the window
test is on the untransposed note (Live 11) then Fold and Limit also adjust it before the
transposition, so the output can lie outside the window by the Pitch amount. The alternative
(a window on the output, which would make the device a pure output limiter) is what the Mode
names might suggest; the Live 11 sentence argues against it [source: 3]. [measure].

**Out-of-range output.** `p'` below 0 or above 127: the note is dropped or clamped; which is
[measure] (dropping, as MIDI cannot carry the note, [inferred]).

**Step Up / Step Down.** A press changes `Pitch` by ±Step Width, clipped at ±128 st or ±30
degrees (the two Pitch parameters' ranges [dump 12.4.6]) [inferred]. They are momentary buttons and the whole set is mappable [manual]. A press
while notes are held does not change the sounding notes (their note-offs are matched), only
later notes [inferred; measure]. Step Width is 1 – 48 st or 1 – 30 degrees [manual; dump 12.4.6];
these are two separate parameters, and Use Current Scale chooses which one applies
[dump 12.4.6; inferred].

**Note-offs.** The device must end each output note with the pitch it started, even when Pitch,
Lowest, Range or Mode have changed in between, and must drop the note-off of a blocked note
[inferred; measure]. The note-offs of Fold and Limit notes carry the folded pitch. Releasing a
note when the parameter has been automated mid-note is the central edge case below.

**Scale-aware per-note pitch bend.** With Use Current Scale on, the pitch bend of an incoming MPE
note is re-mapped so a bent note stays in the scale; 12.1.10 fixed this for notes that were not in
the scale [manual; source: 2]. The re-mapping law is [measure]. Without scale awareness per-note
pitch bend passes through unchanged [inferred].

**Velocity, release velocity and other messages** pass through unchanged [inferred]. Pitch has
no timing, so there is no latency [inferred].

## Edge cases

All [measure] unless tagged.

- **Pitch 0, Lowest 0, Range 127:** pass-through. Pitch 0 with any window: the device is a pure
  range filter.
- **Output beyond 0 or 127** (note 120 with +24): dropped or clamped.
- **Range 0:** one-note window; Block passes only that pitch; Fold collapses everything onto it
  [source: 6]; Limit likewise.
- **Lowest + Range > 127:** is Range reduced or the top capped?
- **Pitch automated or Step pressed while a note is held:** the note-off goes to the original
  output pitch. Hanging note check.
- **Lowest/Range/Mode changed while a note is held:** the held note's off must still match.
- **Use Current Scale toggled while Pitch is large** (say +100 st then degrees): the stored value
  is clipped or kept.
- **No scale on the clip / tuning system loaded** with Use Current Scale on (common.md).
- **Incoming note not in the scale** with a degree shift.
- **Velocity 0 note-on, velocity 127:** unchanged.
- **Device off with notes held:** common.md case C1.
- **Drum Rack chain:** the transposition applies after the pad's Receive note matched
  (common.md).

## Measurement plan

Observation: record the device's output on a second MIDI track and read the notes back
([common.md](common.md) › Measurement plan shared by the MIDI effects; arming and recording are the
user's step). The shared cases C1, C4 (scale degrees), C5 (MPE) and C6 (Rack) apply. A sine
instrument after the device is a sufficient fallback for pitch-only checks.

Input clips (120 bpm; each note 1 beat, velocity 100, one note per beat):

- **A.** Every pitch from 0 to 127 in turn (128 notes, 128 beats, 32 bars in 4/4). Used for
  the window maps.
- **B.** C3 to C4 chromatic (13 notes) with the clip's scale set to C major (and again with
  D Dorian, C minor pentatonic, and with the scale off).
- **C.** Held C3 with the Pitch value changed by clip automation mid-note.

1. **Parameter facts.** Done: the Live 12.4.6 parameter dump and saved set (Controls, `.als`
   import). Semitones and degrees are separate parameters, so nothing clips when Use Current
   Scale is toggled.
2. **Transposition.** Clip A with Pitch = 0, +1, +12, −12, +127, −128 and +128, Lowest 0, Range
   127: output pitch per input pitch, and which notes are lost at the ends.
3. **Range test: block.** Clip A with Lowest = C3 (60), Range = 12, Pitch = 0: pass band and
   inclusive edges. Then Pitch = +24: does the window follow the input or output? Read the
   passing input pitches.
4. **Fold.** Clip A with Lowest = 60, Range = 11, then 7, 12, 0 and 24, Pitch = 0: output
   pitch for each input (the fold law) and then with Pitch = +5, +12 to see the order of fold
   and transposition.
5. **Limit.** The same maps with Limit, including Range = 0.
6. **LED.** Observe in Live's window with the user: whether the LED flashes in Fold and Limit
   (cannot be read through the API).
7. **Steps.** Step Width at 1, 7, 48; press Step Up / Down through `set_device_parameter`
   (the buttons may be API parameters) from Pitch 120 and −120: the clip. Check the Step Width
   limits with Use Current Scale on.
8. **Scale degrees.** Clip B with C major, Pitch = +1, +2, +7 (an octave), −1; the same with D
   Dorian and C minor pentatonic (5 notes per octave), and with scale off (Use Current Scale on).
   Include the notes not in the scale (C#3): rounding of the starting degree. Compare against
   C4 in all cases of 7 notes. Lowest and Range with Use Current Scale on: semitones or degrees?
9. **Note-offs.** Clip C, with the Pitch automation: read the pitch of the note-off against the
   note-on.
10. **MPE.** A clip with per-note pitch bend at ±2 semitones on an in-scale note and an out-of-
    scale note, Use Current Scale on (common.md C5).
11. **Latency.** C2.

## Implementation notes

- Stateless apart from a held-note table (input (channel, pitch) → output pitch) that makes the
  note-off match. Integer arithmetic only; no audio.
- Keep three code paths for the three modes; the range test goes before the transposition as in
  the Live 11 description until a measurement says otherwise, with a flag for the other order.
- Scale mode needs the clip-scale service (common.md): map the pitch to a degree index in the
  scale, add the offset, map back; treat a note outside the scale by one rule (to be measured).
- Parameter units: Pitch and Step Width each have a semitone and a degrees parameter, and Use
  Current Scale chooses between them; the import keeps all four numbers and the flag.
- **`.als` import.** The device element is `MidiPitcher` [als 12.4.6]. Parameter elements, in
  stored order, all as the API shows them [als 12.4.6]: `On`, `Pitch` (semitones, −128 – 128),
  `PitchScaleDegrees` (−30 – 30), `Lowest` (MIDI note, 0 – 127), `Range` (0 – 127), `RangeMode`
  (Mode, index 0 – 2: Block, Fold, Limit), `UseCurrentScale` (bool), `StepWidth` (1 – 48),
  `StepWidthScaleDegrees` (1 – 30). Not parameters, also stored: six view-scroll positions
  (`PitchScrollPosArranger`, `…Envelope`, and the same for the degree Pitch and for Lowest).
